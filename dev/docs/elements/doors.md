# Doors

Each floor has two doorways in its back wall: the start door he comes out of at the first run's head, and the exit door at the last run's
end, whose stairs he climbs into the light. The exit may be sealed until its plate (`xplate`) is pressed. Both are drawn live by style,
on every floor; the generator places them, everything else is look and the two short scripted states `enter` and `exit`.

## Example

He reaches a sealed exit and presses Up: the leaf rattles in its slot (`sealed`; until v89 "Sealed. A floor plate somewhere opens this
door."). He finds the `xplate`: the door's rumble comes from where it stands, panned and muffled by the distance, and if it is out of view
(round 49a, `gateCue`; until v89 "A heavy door grinds open somewhere on this floor."). Since round 50e no glance: it is the great door ([gameplay](../gameplay.md#the-gate-cue-round-50e)):
`gdoor` (3.8 s: a deep rumble with judders as the leaf rises, a heavy boom at the top, the outside air rushing in while the room's air bed
lifts), a shudder of 2.3 px and 2.5 at the boom, grit at the view's edge toward it (or over it in view), his head lifted toward it, and
the doorway's light a little brighter while the leaf rises (`doorFlare`); the leaf rises at 0.45 a second, as before. Back at the door he presses Up 14 px right of its middle, facing left.
He walks left to the middle at 40 px/s, turns to face the stairs (they recede to the right; `EX_TURN` 0.16 s) and climbs: 3.2 treads a
second, one footfall each. From the first rise (`EX_CUT`) he is cut at the doorway's lintel and jambs, as SDLPoP's `clip_char` cuts the kid
at the level door; his own pixels warm and whiten toward the stair light and fade; he is gone at tread 3.5 (`EX_GONE`), his steps go on
out of sight (`stairsaway`), the doorway's light swells, and the card comes at tread 4.6 (`EX_N`).

| The climb | `P.exS` counts treads at `EX_RATE` 3.2 a second; each tread lifts him `EX_RS + EX_SY` and moves him `EX_SX` right, eased in the second half of the tread; the first also lifts `EX_UP` from mid-depth onto the tread | his feet land on drawn treads (`stairscan.py`) |
| The end | `levelComplete` at `EX_N` 4.6 treads (or 3.6 s in the state): the card, `S.level` + 1, checkpoint and floor clock reset, save, `clear` | about 1.4 s of climbing after the walk |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Guards | do not engage him while he is in `exit` or `enter`; a guard often stands before the exit ([guards](guards-and-snakes.md): the exit guard, the captain every fifth floor) |
| His drawing on the stairs | cut by `doorClip(L.door)` once `P.exS > EX_CUT`; tinted and faded by `drawPTint` |
| His drawing in the entrance | drawn `P.enU * EN_DX` right and `P.enU * EN_DY` up (deep in the doorway), cut by the entrance's opening (where `startDoorX` draws it) while `P.enU > 0.3`, as a dark silhouette (`rgb(7,5,11)` at `P.enK`, 0.92 fading to 0 by `EN_LIT`) taking its colours as he reaches the light |
| His poses | `walkPose` on the approach (0.93 strides a second); on the stairs half a stride a tread, the stride moving only while he rises (a planted foot stays put); the turn is `turnSettle` |
| Shadow and beams | no lamp shadow and no beam occlusion during `exit`, nor while deep in the entrance (`P.enU > 0`) |
| The bot | ends every floor with `door`; plays the entry only with `opts.entry` |

## How it is drawn

`drawDoor(ctx,x,r,open,isExit)` is the first thing `drawDynamic` draws after the lamps, live every frame, in both modes: the start door
at `startDoorX()` with `entryOpen()`, the exit at `L.door.x` with `L.door.open`. The room picture under each doorway and its frame is one flat tone of the back wall
(`drawRoomStatic` stage 0, a 36 px rectangle from the ceiling's edge to the floor's back edge; `EGP.nocut` skips it), because Torchlight's
relief reads that picture and embossed the courses and friezes on the door and the lit stair (round 15; `doorscan.py`).

```
          ctop  ===========================  lintel block (th.wall), tucked under the ceiling's edge; a gold dot on the exit's
            y0  |  |   winged sun disc   |  |  (palace, tile, crypt)            ctop = r*TH + ceilOff (5 under an upper floor)
                |  |  head: lintel, or   |  |
                |  |  round vault (dungeon)  |
                |po|  .-------------.    |po|  posts 5 px (th.wallB, lit edge)
                |st| /jamb| leaf or  |   |st|  leaf at 0.45 of the doorway's depth, slides up into the lintel by open*(h+4)
                |  ||    | stairs   |    |  |  exit open: stair light and 8 treads receding up and right
            yb  |__||____|__________|____|__|  threshold; yb = SURF(r) - HY, the floor's back edge
                      24 px, centred on x + HX
```

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Opening | 24 px wide, from `y0 = ctop + 6` down to the floor's back edge; nothing of the doorway shows above the ceiling's front edge |
| Head (`doorHead`) | the dungeon style (Nun, Garama, Hawara) a round vault of radius 12; every other style a flat lintel 2 px under `y0` |
| Inside | the open exit: a stair light (`#fff0c8` to `#b8552e`) and 8 treads, each `EX_RS` 6.2 high and `EX_SX`, `EX_SY` (0.22 of the room's depth) deeper, the same in every style; otherwise dark (`#07050b`) |
| Leaf (`doorLeaf`) by style | dungeon: oak planks, iron straps and rivets; palace: walnut, a gold star, brass studs; tile: painted blue, a gold star of the tomb ceilings, a turquoise border; forge: riveted blackened iron, its seams still glowing; crypt: verdigris bronze, a pale star medallion, turquoise glaze bands |
| Jamb and threshold | the left jamb's inside face and the threshold, nearer than the leaf, dithered; warmed when lit |
| Frame (`doorArch`) by style | spandrels; a ring along the head (voussoirs in the dungeon); the winged sun disc over palace, tile and crypt lintels; a moulded frame (palace, tile); turquoise bands (crypt); studs and a glowing line (forge); lintel joints (dungeon) |
| Exit glow | an additive radial sprite (46 px) at its foot, `open * 0.5`, drawn under him in both modes; in Classic times `1 + 1.3 exitSwell()` (Torchlight swells the `door` light instead) |

The start door's place (round 39, the layering rule R1, [layers](../layers.md)): a doorway is cut in the back wall (L1), and the face of a
wall or a low ceiling in the next cell stands `HX` nearer and `HX` further left (L3). On a floor whose first run heads left the start
door's cell has a wall on its right, and the frame (posts and lintel, `DOOR_HW` 18 px either side of the opening's middle at the back
wall) lay 18 px over that face (38 of 96 floors, floors 1-48 seeds 1-2; floor 10 seed 2, door cell 349 row 12). `startDoorX` moves the
door along the back wall, as `cpX` moves the sky clock, until the frame's edge is on the face's: `min(x, (d+1)*TW - 2*HX - DOOR_HW)`
with a face in the next cell (`cpFace`), `max(x, d*TW - 2*HX + DOOR_HW)` with one in the cell before. The live door, the doorway cut
out of the room's art (stage 0), the entry he comes out of (`entryX`, `doorClip`) and the ceiling over the cells it spans all take that
place; `L.start.x`, the respawn spot and play keep the cell. The exit door is drawn at `L.door.x` as before.

The exit on the stairs, by tread count `k = P.exS` (`EXF.look` true):

| From | What changes |
|---|---|
| 0.5 | his pixels start to take the stair light's tint, up to 0.86 by about 2.9 (`exitTint`: amber `EXT0` to pale gold `EXT1`, by `(k-1.2)/1.8`) |
| `EX_CUT` 0.55 | cut at the opening's head and jambs (`doorClip`, open below the floor) |
| 1.8 | he starts to fade, gone by `EX_GONE` 3.5 |
| `EX_SW0` 2.6 | the doorway's light swells (`exitSwell`), peaks as he is gone, eases before the card; `exitlight` sounds (round 50c: air swelling from outside, no chime); the floor's fanfare (`clear`) is gone: silence, then the stairs |
| `EX_GONE` 3.5 | gone; `stairsaway` (four fading steps over 0.5 s) |
| `EX_N` 4.6 | the card |

`drawPTint` draws the figure whole into a buffer (`PTB`, 96 by 96 at the canvas's scale, `_glv` for Torchlight), tints only his own pixels
(`source-atop`) and lays the buffer down at an alpha: a fade with no overlaps showing through and a tint that never spills round him; never
a glow, haze or bloom. `EXF.look` false draws him plain (`stairscan.py` measures his feet that way). The Crossing's doorway reuses
`doorHead`, `doorArch` and `drawPTint` on its own land ([the Crossing](the-crossing.md)).

## Rules for changing it

- The entry plays only from `startLevel(n,-1,true)` with no checkpoint; nothing may snapshot or time it.
- Never draw wall art under a doorway: keep the flattened rectangle in step with the doorway's size and place; check with `doorscan.py`.
- A doorway is drawn clear of a nearer face beside it (`startDoorX`); anything that reads the start door's place (the cut-out, the entry,
  `doorClip`, the tools) reads `startDoorX`, never the cell.
- He is cut at the opening's head and jambs (`doorClip`, `doorHead`), never at the ceiling's edge; the stairs recede right, so his facing
  never moves him on them.
- The tint and fade are his own pixels only (`drawPTint`); no glow or bloom round him.
- A change to the treads' geometry (`EX_RS`, `EX_SX`, `EX_SY`, `EX_UP`) must keep his feet on them: `stairscan.py` (floors 1-48, both facings).
- The bot's `door` action and `opts.entry` must still end and start a floor.
