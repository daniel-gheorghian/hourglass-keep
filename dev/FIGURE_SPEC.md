# The prince's body and poses (spec)

## The rule (round 23): the body and every pose come from motion capture

Owner, after round 22d: "Start from those clips and build the athletic teen prince body and scrap the current skeleton and proportions
so that we don't get swayed in the wrong direction." The skeleton, the drawing's widths and every pose are generated by
`dev/tools/r23poses.py` from measured data; nothing is fitted to the earlier figures. The rounds 22b-22d sections below are superseded.

Sources:
- CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu ("The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
  The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217."). Per subject an .asf skeleton, per trial an .amc of joint angles at
  120 fps (`dev/tools/ref/cmu/`, not in git: `index.tsv` lists the clips; fetch an .asf/.amc from
  `http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/subjects/NN/NN_MM.amc`). Parsed by `dev/tools/mocap.py` (units: `:units length 0.45`, so metres = value /
  0.45 x 0.0254; forward kinematics L = C M C^-1 per bone, M = Rz Ry Rx of its dofs).
- ANSUR II (US Army, 2012; calmcode.io's copy in `dev/tools/ref/ansur2_ergonomics.csv`), men aged 17 to 20: the lean ones (BMI under 23,
  n = 124) for the trunk's lengths and depths, all 422 for the limbs' girths and the chest (an athlete's muscle on a lean frame).

### The head: 6.5 heads (decision)

A real 17 to 20 year old is 7.7 heads tall (ANSUR stature 1764.5 mm; head 228 mm, 0.13 of stature, Drillis and Contini 1966); Loomis
(Figure Drawing for All It's Worth, 1943) draws an average man at 7.5 heads and a youth a little less. Games with a figure of this
height keep the head larger than real so the face reads: the 1989 Prince of Persia kid, rotoscoped from Mechner's brother, is 41 rows
with a 7-row head, 5.86 heads (measured from his DOS frames, round 22d); the other rotoscoped side-view games of the time (Another
World, Flashback) draw 6.5 to 7 heads (round 22b's survey); stylised platformers go to 2 to 3. Our figure is 52 px at 1x and drawn
at 2 to 3 device pixels a game pixel on the phone, so the eye, brow, nose and jaw need about 8 px of head: **6.5 heads**, between
the real 7.7 and the kid's 5.86. Everything below the chin is real, only the head is enlarged (1.24 times real).

### The poses (MCP, MCK in the source)

Every pose is the game's angles (`skel`: lean and head from straight up, the limbs from straight down, knees and elbows as the bend
from the bone above, positive forward) measured from one clip frame, or a short average, projected onto the plane of the body's own
facing (the pelvis's forward, `mocap.facing`). The near side ('F', drawn last) is the subject's right, or his left in the fight
poses (his lead leg). What places a pose in the game is v69's and unchanged: air, hy (the hip's height in the air), sw (the
blade's angle), rot, kn, and the held hands (hold/hb: the hand goes to v69's place, `r23grips.json`, moved with the hip on the
ground). Where the new arm cannot reach v69's grip, the trunk is turned toward it (logged by r23poses.py: pull 0.41 rad, garde 0.31,
lunge 0.46, parry 0.07, lowStrike 0.10). The low leaps keep their legs and are pitched forward until the head is no higher than v69's.

### How to add or change a move

1. Find a clip in `dev/tools/ref/cmu/index.tsv`, fetch its .amc (and the subject's .asf) into `dev/tools/ref/cmu/`.
2. Look at it: `python3 mocap.py sheet CLIP a b step out.png` (a contact sheet in the side view) and `mocap.py video CLIP frame`.
3. Add or change its line in `SEL` (or a cycle in `build()`) of `r23poses.py`; run it: it rewrites the block between `// <R23-MOCAP>`
   and `// </R23-MOCAP>` in the source and prints each pose's source and any grip correction.
4. Check: `figscan.py` (held hands, crown, soles), `egyptr23.py moves` (mocap | new | v69), lowceil, stairscan, swingtest.

the low leaps and the slide), the sole on the floor where v69's was, the swing's hands on the bar, the drink's hand at the mouth.

## Muscle bulk (round 23b): one multiplier, MUS

Owner: "Yeah needs to be bulkier. Add a bulk multiplier, but in the sense of more muscle. Give me three variants of him with the current size
then more and more bulky". The owner picked 1.28 in round 23c; round 23b itself drew 1.0 (round 23, unchanged pixel for pixel).

The rule: `MUS` (r23poses.py) is the upper arm's girth over round 23's (ANSUR II's median). Every other measure grows at its own rate per
unit of the upper arm's (`GROW`): `factor = 1 + rate x (MUS - 1)`. Joints (elbow, wrist, knee, ankle), bones, the skeleton (R23B), every pose,
the held hands, hit boxes and timing never change. The waist barely grows (no fat), so the V-taper sharpens. Robbers keep 1.15 times the
prince's breadth on top of the level.

Round 23c: the owner picked 1.28 ("1.28 is fine"); the game drew it until round 24 (now 1.65, below) with no override (`MUS` in r23poses.py; R23D is the 1.28 table, R23DV keeps the others).

## A supple run (round 25a): what the side view threw away

Owner: "The prince is somewhat stiff when running. Humans are somewhat flexible. Research a bit how games are handling that in their
characters." Round 23 projected each run key to the game's ten angles. That drew the trunk as one straight line from the hips' middle to the
shoulders' middle, hung both arms from one point (moved only by the drawing's 0.9 sin(upper arm), -0.66 to -0.12 px), and kept the feet flat.
`egyptr25a.py measure` (r25a.json) measures the rest in 09_01 and four other run clips (09_02, 09_03, 16_35, 16_36: they agree). Pixels are
at the game's scale, 27.25 px a metre:

| Measure (one stride) | 09_01 | the five clips | Drawn |
|---|---|---|---|
| thorax pitch less pelvis pitch (the spine's bend) | 0.7 to 12.3 deg, twice a stride | -1.6 to 14.6 | `sb`: the torso's spine curve |
| thorax yaw less pelvis yaw (counter-rotation) | -11.9 to 8.6 deg | 19 to 27 deg peak to peak | as the shoulders' shift |
| near shoulder joint fore and aft of the middle | -0.8 to 0.7 px | up to +-1.4 px | `soF`, `soB` (the far one opposite) |
| foot pitch, ankle to ball (flat -21 deg) | -135 to -13 deg | -135 to -8 | `fpF`, `fpB` from flat: +6 to -114 deg |
| hip joint fore and aft of the middle | +-0.2 px | +-0.4 px | no: under the threshold |
| pelvis drop (near hip over far) | -0.4 to 0.6 px | | no: under the kilt |
| wrist flexion | 11 to 15 deg, steady | 6 to 15 | no: 0.1 px at the hand |
| head pitch in the world | -3.8 to 1.3 deg | | already the clip's (steadier than the chest) |

How it is drawn (look only; timing, footfalls, hit boxes, crowns under v69's, wall hiding and lift occlusion unchanged):
- `sb`: the control point of the torso's quadratic spine (`TG` in drawFigureHD0) moves by -LEN.to/2 tan(sb/2) along the forward normal.
  The tangents at the pelvis and at the chest then differ by sb. The chord (`lean`), the shoulder joint and the head stay where they were.
- `soF`/`soB`: each arm (shoulder, elbow, hand) is drawn moved that far along the trunk's normal. `sm` = 1 fades out the drawing's own
  swing-driven root shift (`armG`), so the clip's values are not counted twice.
- `fpF`/`fpB`: the foot turns about the ankle joint, `R23B.ah` (ANSUR's lateral malleolus, 1.95 px) up the shank from the sole point
  the leg ends at (`footRot`, inside `leg`). Flat is the clip's own foot angle while ankle and ball are both down (`footFlat`: run -20.8,
  walk -21.1 deg). In `cycPose` the hip stands `cl` over the turned foot's lowest point (`footLow`, the drawn foot's points `FOOTP`), so he
  rises onto the ball at toe-off as the clip's hip does. v71's flat foot sank him 1.8 px there; heel strike and mid-stance are as before.
- Follow-through, procedural: the kilt's hem is laid on the thighs as they were `KH_LAG` (0.035) of a stride before, `KH_K` (0.8) of the
  way (`khF`/`khB`, set in `runPoseHD` from a second `cycPose` into its own buffer). It is a pure function of the phase: rewind-safe, no
  state, no allocation.
- The walk (16_15) carries the same keys. A part stride (amp < 1: the exit stairs, pushing and pulling blocks) keeps the feet flat and the
  rest scaled. All the keys are 0 in every other pose. Robbers never run.

Research (sources on the page, round 25a): rotoscoped games (PoP 1989, Another World) keep the real body's weight shifts; Dead Cells poses a
3D skeleton at ~50 px; Overgrowth (Rosen, GDC 2014) layers procedural motion on few keys; the principles (overlapping action, foot roll,
shoulders against hips, a bending spine, a steady head); running biomechanics (Schache 2002, Novacheck 1998, Pozzo 1990). Checks: figscan
(ok against v69; against v71 only the run and walk differ), lowceil, stairscan, run2, rendersmoke.

## No stiff moves (round 25f): overlap, settles, measured follow-through

Owner: "I ment a stiff prince in the broader sense: check all his movements for stiffness and make adjustments based on research". The audit (every
move, what the side view drops, where it is rigid between keys, what should trail) is on the page (egypt_themes.html, round 25f) and in
`egyptround25f.py` (AUDIT). Three things made him stiff: every part eased to a new pose at one rate (lockstep), landings, stops, hangs and falls
were single held frames, and nothing trailed a turn, stop or landing.

**The rule (every move from now on):** (1) overlap: parts arrive at different rates, the trunk and legs first, the upper arms, then the forearms
and head (followPose, PKR); (2) anticipation and settle around events, measured from a clip where one is local (a settle: g toward the clip's
peak by the hip's height, plus each angle's residual against that blend), else a pure function of the time since the event; (3) the extra
degrees of freedom of round 25a (two-part trunk, shoulder girdle, foot roll) where the clip shows them. Never a timer, transition, grip, hit box or
contact; nothing that accumulates state outside the pose filter and the look state (`e.lk`).

| Part | How | Values |
|---|---|---|
| overlap | `followPose(cur, tgt, dt, rate, LAGM[state])`: each key at rate x PKR | upper arms 0.7, forearms 0.55, head and hp 0.75, sb 0.85; in the air (mode 2) also thighs 0.75, knees 0.7. Only held poses and short transitions; the clip cycles (run, walk, crawl, climb, drink, swing) and the wall's IK poses at one rate as before; held hands (gF/gB) always at the full rate |
| land settle | `MCK.set_land`, 16_05 f187-270, peak f203 (= PS.land); arms follow the blend | deepest 0.13 s after touchdown, standing by 0.5 s; strength (impact speed - 60)/240, 0.3 to 1; landHurt: from its peak into stand |
