# Stonework

The keep itself: walls, floors, steps, ledges, low ceilings and the back wall behind them, with their wear, breaches, corner shading and
the cloth and scars hung on them. It is on every floor; the generator lays out the tiles (`L.base`), and everything about their look is a
hash of the cell and `L.seed`, drawn once per room into the room picture.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Code | tiles `EMPTY` 0, `FLOOR` 1, `WALL` 2 (`B`, `solidBelow`); feature `low`; `drawRoomStatic` (stages 0-2), `wallCell`, `sideFace`, `lowCeil`, `cornice`, `backCell`, `backBrick`, `tilePanel`, `decor`/`niche`, `slab`, `aoBack`/`aoFloor` (`AO_K`), `wearAt`/`wearLevel` (`WEAR_T`, `WEAR_BIAS`, `WEAR_UP`), `breaches`/`makeBreach`/`drawBreaches`/`drawBreachRubble`/`inBreach`, `planHangings`/`drawHanging`/`tapCloth` (`TAP`, `fadeCol`), `settleLights`/`drawWallScars`/`drawFallen`, `roomCanvas`/`roomCache`/`invalidateCell`/`roomWork`, `ceilY`/`ceilOver`/`beamOverhead`, `clampMove`, `wallPress`/`figClip`; constants `TW` 32, `TH` 64, `DX` 16, `DY` 8, `HX` 8, `HY` 4, `FHH` 5, `LOW_H` 30, `LOW_R` 40, `CEIL_LIP` 5, `CEIL_TOL` 3, `STONE`, `DRIFT` |
| Generated by | `buildLevel` (runs, turns, steps `up`/`down`/`drop2`, `tunnel` sets `low`), then the open-hall pass near the end of `buildLevel` (headroom; 16% of opened cells get a window from the main stream `rng`); `L.base0` is the terrain as built. | Pictures | `egyptshot.py`, `egyptfx.py`, `egyptpage.py` (themes), `egyptr20a.py` (low ceilings and beams), `egyptr35c.py` (art over tiles), `beamshot.py` |

## Example

He hangs from the lip of a step's top, a lamp on the back wall to the left. The step is one `WALL` cell; its front face is drawn `HX` (8 px)
left of its cell, so it stands in front of him. `updateLook` sets `P.hideMode` 2 and `figClip` draws only what is above the ledge: his
hands (SDLPoP `draw_tile_fore`, `clip_char`). In Torchlight his lamp shadow (`glShadow`) would fall on that lit face; `glMask` carves the
stone out of the effect mask as it is drawn (the face from `c*TW-HX`, its top and right side), so the face stays lit and the shadow falls
only on the back wall. Before round 20a the carve was at the cell and left an 8 px prince-shaped stripe on the face.

## Geometry

The view is oblique: depth recedes up and to the right by `DX` 16 and `DY` 8 px across a room's depth. Cell `(c,r)` has `X = c*TW`,
`Y = r*TH`; the floor surface is `SURF(r) = Y+60`, the actors' line at mid-depth.

```
 A WALL cell (c,r)                         A floor cell (c,r): FLOOR, or EMPTY over WALL

        X+8               X+40                     X+8                 X+40  (X+24 when a WALL is on the right)
 Y-8     +-----------------+               Y+56     +-------------------+   back edge, d = 1
        /   top            /|                       /    top face       /
 Y     +-----------------+  |              Y+60    / . . actors' line . /   feet at d = 0.5: (x, SURF(r))
       |                 |  |                     /                   /
       |                 |s |              Y+64  +-------------------+     front edge, d = 0
       |   front face    |i |                    |  lip (FHH 5 px)   |     Y+64..Y+69, then 3 px of shade
       |                 |d |                    +-------------------+
       |                 |e |                   X-8                 X+24
       |                 |  + Y+56
 Y+64  +-----------------+ /                onFloor(x,d,ys) = (x + (d-0.5)*DX, ys + (0.5-d)*DY)
      X-8               X+24
       |<-HX->| the cell starts at X
```

| Face | Where | Drawn by |
|---|---|---|
| Wall front face | `X-HX`..`X+TW-HX`, `Y`..`Y+TH` | `wallCell` (clipped to that rect), stage 1 |
| Wall right side | parallelogram from `X+TW-HX` up and right by `DX`,`DY`; only where the right neighbour is not `WALL` | `sideFace` (dithered `th.lo`), stage 1, under every front face |
| Wall top | the floor slab of the cell above (an `EMPTY` cell over a `WALL` is a floor) | `slab`, stage 2 |
| Floor top face | front edge `SURF+HY` from `X-HX`, back edge `SURF-HY` from `X+HX` | `slab`, stage 2 |
| Floor lip | `SURF+HY`..`+FHH`, hanging into the row below; a ledge's open left end is a slanted line, its open right end the slab's own 5 px side | `slab` (`lo`, `ro`, `wr`) |
| Low ceiling block | the top `LOW_H` (30) px of a `low` cell, front face `HX` left; side face at the tunnel's right end | `lowCeil`; `sideFace(...,Y+TH-34)` |
| Cornice | an 8 px frieze along the foot of a wall block over open space (not dungeon, not forge) | `cornice` |

## In play

| Rule | Example or number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A `WALL` cell is solid: `clampMove` stops a body whose head-to-shin band (`y-34`..`y-6`) would enter it | he stops `BW` short of the cell | the wall's play edge is its cell, not its drawn face |
| A floor's lip at head height blocks too | a `FLOOR` in the next column with `SURF` inside his band | he cannot walk through the edge of an upper floor |
| A step is one storey of `WALL` | `up()`: `(c+1,r)` stays wall, `(c+1,r-1)` becomes floor; bot `climb` | climb at its near face |
| Steps down | `down()` one storey (64 px: safe, under 100); `drop2()` two storeys, bot `hangdrop` (hang first) | falls: under 100 px safe, 100+ hurts, 170+ kills (see [gameplay](../gameplay.md)) |
| A low ceiling (`low` feature) is a crawl | `tunnel()`: 3 to 4 cells (to 5 from floor 12), the cell above must be wall; bot `crawl` | `clampMove` stops him walking into a `low` cell unless crawling |
| Under a low ceiling | Up/Jump bump (`hint('crawl')`); the sword cannot be drawn (draw it low before crawling in: crouch, then Grab); snakes may lurk | `underLow()` keeps him crouched |
| A ceiling at his height makes any floor jump the low leap | `ceilY`: a wall's bottom or an upper floor's slab (`CEIL_LIP` 5 under the row's top), two rows up at most; `beamOverhead` tests the full arc, a graze of `CEIL_TOL` 3 px keeps the full jump; `LOWJ` run 212/128, stand 150/112 | his head would go through the stone; falling short is his own doing (v60) |
| A saw under a ceiling becomes spikes | after the open-hall pass, any saw with stone over its span | the low leap cannot clear its blade |
| The open-hall pass opens one row of headroom over every floor, gap and shaft top | a non-wall cell under a `WALL` gets its upper cell set `EMPTY` | so most corridors are two storeys tall and jumps have room |
| It keeps stone where something needs it | `gate`, `chomp`, `low` cells; the exit door's row within 2 columns; the start door's column and the one in front; `keepWall` (a kick shaft's far wall, lift shafts); `lin` (a counterweight lift's top mouth, a sand lift's walls) | gates and blades slide up into stone; doors sit in a wall (round 25d, 26e) |
| A corridor under another floor stays one storey | the pass opens only a `WALL` overhead, never a `FLOOR` | those are the low-leap corridors |
| Wear, breaches, arches, hangings, scars | look only | nothing about them changes play |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Walking into a wall | `clampMove` stops him; guards use the same (`self`), and keep to their side |
| Pressed against a face | `wallPress`: the figure is drawn up to `HX+12` px to the left so it does not sink into the face; anything still poking in (a lowered sword) is cut at the face. Guards too |
| Jumping up at, hanging from, climbing onto or down from a ledge on a wall's near side | hidden behind the front face but for his hands (`P.hideMode` 1 while jumping up, 2 hanging; `figClip`); the hidden part casts no lamp shadow. Detail in [prince](prince.md) |
| In a tunnel | crawls; he does not go en garde under it (`underLow` in the crouch state); snakes may lie there ([guards and snakes](guards-and-snakes.md)) |
| Under a low ceiling or a one-storey corridor | the head clamp in `stAir` keeps either leap under it |
| The bot | plays the steps, drops and crawls from `L.path` like any other move |

## How it is drawn

Both modes draw the same room picture: `drawRoomStatic` into a canvas per room (10 by 3 tiles), cached, and in Torchlight uploaded once
to WebGL (see [rendering](../rendering.md)). Nothing in it moves. It is built in three stages over three light frames
(`startRoomJob`/`stepRoomJob`); faces and floors are drawn one cell beyond the room's edges too, so overhanging faces leave no seams:

```
stage 0  back plane      backCell + decor per cell (not in WALL, low or open cells)
                         arch recesses ('fill') -> breaches (cut through) -> arch frames ('frame') -> windows
                         lamp scars, hangings (skipped with EGP.noover)
                         EGP.noart flattens all of it to th.wall ('atop': openings stay open)
                         the doorways' rectangles flattened to th.wall (EGP.nocut skips)
                         aoBack (GLFX.ao)
stage 1  wall faces      side faces first, then front faces (wallCell), low ceilings (lowCeil), cornices
stage 2  floors          slab() per floor cell (not live tiles, not a lift pit), mark = 254
                         onto a scratch canvas (rlBegin): spike holes, rubble, hurtdrop bites and heaps,
                           breach rubble, fallen lamps and cloth -> laid down, marked RL_A 251 (rlEnd)
                         aoFloor (GLFX.ao)
```

Live tiles are left out of stage 2 and drawn in `drawDynamic`: a cracked slab (`loose` with `s<2`), `plate`, `xplate`, `lplate`,
`closer`, `grate`, and a lift's foot (`liftPit`, round 37a). The cell under a pushed block is drawn from `L.blockUnder`. Decoration is laid
out on `L.base0` (`D0`, `solidBelowD`), so an arch or hanging stays when a tile falls.

### Styles and themes

The stonework is drawn by `style`; the theme gives only the colours (`THEMES`: `mortar`, `brick`, `brick2`, `wall`, `wallA`, `wallB`,
`hi`, `lo`, `top`, `front`, `trim`, `inlay`, `tileA`-`tileD`). The theme's `look` picks backdrop, hangings and light, not the stone.

| Style (themes) | Front face (`STONE`: courses x stone width) | Back wall | Its own marks | Arches, windows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dungeon (Nun, Garama, Hawara) | 4 x 32 | 16 x 8 bricks, two tones, 1 in 11 missing | damp moss along the foot, green streaks, rust under an iron fitting, an iron ring, a short hanging rope under a ceiling (`backRope`: knotted with a frayed tail, or a sling of three cords holding a small jar; v61's iron chain only with `EGP.lamps` and `EGP.traps` off) | round vault, voussoir ring, stone piers; barred slit |
| palace (West, Kerma, Shali) | 3 x 32 | 32 x 16 blocks, soft stone pits (alveolization) | under a ceiling a frieze of small arches; a dado shade band; kheker cornice; lip inlaid with diamonds | lintel under a cavetto cornice, slender posts; clerestory under a lintel |
| tile (Blue Chambers, Well Corridors) | 3 x 32 | 16 x 8 bricks | Djoser's faience dado (`tilePanel`) on floor cells; kheker cornice with a faience line; lip with a faience band | lintel under a cavetto cornice, faience ring; clerestory with a grille of upright slats |
| forge (Meroë, Napata) | 6 x 16 | 16 x 8 bricks | soot under the ceiling, a glowing furnace vent at the foot; no cornice; lip joints every 8 px | lintel under a cavetto cornice, dashed ring, stone piers; round window with a grid, lit from inside |
| crypt (Osiris, Aksum) | 4 x 32 | 32 x 16 blocks | two glazed turquoise brick bands; false-door niches (`niche`) by lamps and 1 cell in 4; diamonds under a ceiling; kheker cornice | lintel under a cavetto cornice; star window, lit from inside |

On every wall front face: a string course every second storey (`gr%2===1`) and a blind niche one cell in 17. Floor slabs: flagstones of
uneven length (joints every ~22 px along the whole floor, so they run across tiles), each a shade apart, some split across the depth.

### Wall art

"Wall art" is everything the back plane carries besides plain masonry: arch posts and frames, the faience dado and glazed bands, the
friezes (kheker cornice, the palace's arcade band, the crypt's diamonds), niches, hangings and lamp scars. It lives only in the room
picture. Because Torchlight's relief reads that picture, art must never show under something drawn live over the back wall: doorways
are flattened in stage 0 (round 15, `doorscan.py`), and live tiles, blocks, gate and lift slabs, jaws and sand are rubbed back in the relief
pass (`glRelFloors`, `glRelSlab`, `glRelLifts`, `glRelWell`, `glRelJaws`; round 35c for tiles: before it 154 of 2,064 tiles showed art in
Torchlight). `EGP.noart` (true or `'atop'`) and `EGP.noover` exist for those scans.

### Wear

`wearAt(x,y)` is smooth value noise across the keep (cells 4.5 tiles by 1.3 storeys, seeded by `L.seed`) plus `WEAR_BIAS` by style
(palace -0.06, tile -0.09, dungeon +0.05, forge +0.02, crypt +0.08) plus `WEAR_UP[L.prog.wear]` (0, 0.06, 0.13, 0.19: each 20-floor cycle
up the tower). `wearLevel` cuts it at `WEAR_T` 0.39 / 0.57 / 0.74 into 0 pristine, 1 worn, 2 damaged, 3 partly fallen. Each stone and brick
takes the level at its own centre, so the change runs across a wall, not cell by cell.

| Level | Wall front face (`stoneFace`) | Back wall (`backBrick`, `backCell`) | Floor (`slab`) | Arches, cornice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | crisp, rounded corners | crisp | kept, crisp lit front edge | intact |
| 1 | arrises lost, grime on the lower part, a knocked corner now and then | arrises rounded, tone drifts; soiling streaks from the ceiling, a rising-damp tide mark with salt bloom (not tile) | a trodden band down the middle depth, stains | grime at the foot of the posts |
| 2 | out of true, cracks, chips | chipped, spalled (pale scar, shadowed lip), pitted in soft stone; stepped cracks along the joints | dust or sand drifted against the back (`DRIFT`: soot in the forge, moss-green in crypt and dungeon, moss on the lip) | chips in the ring and the sill, a crack out of the ring; cornice chipped |
| 3 | 30% of stones broken open, the rubble core showing | the odd brick dropped out; breaches open here | deep drifts, the lit front edge rubbed away in places | 70%: a piece out of the ring and one post snapped, its pieces at the foot; cornice arches fallen |

Floors age by neglect, never by breaking: breaking is the look of the tiles that crumble under him ([cracked slabs](cracked-slabs-and-sand-wells.md)).
From the second cycle (`L.prog.wear >= 1`) cobwebs hang in top corners and a few fallen stones lie at the foot of the wall. A theme at
wear 2 or more is named "The Ruined ..." (`themeName`). `stoneWear` is the same idea for drawn blocks (roof blocks, slabs), with a cap in px.

### Breaches

Where the wall is partly fallen (`wearLevel` 3), holes open right through the back wall: `breaches()` (once a floor, `L.breaches`)
finds runs of cells with floor under them and open space above, takes 2 to 5 cells at a time (85% each) and `makeBreach` models the
failure: bricks come out whole along the real bond (`brickBond`: 16 x 8 or 32 x 16), the masonry over the gap arches so the hole's top is a
corbelled point, 40% run down to the floor, the rest stop at a broken sill with loose bricks; in big-block walls the facing comes away
from a wider patch than the hole through the core. Around it: spalled and chipped bricks (`halo`), stepped cracks from the point and the
sill's corners, a pale lit lip on the broken edge, the wall's thickness `BR_T` (5, -2.5) inside, a mound of rubble along the back of the
floor (`drawBreachRubble`). With the background on the hole is cut open onto the view (see [openings](openings-and-backdrop.md)); off, it
shows mortar. Never beside an open arch, in a lift shaft or beside one (`liftNear`, round 26a), nor over a `gate`, `chomp`, `low`,
`banner` or `bar` cell. A window, lamp or hanging that a breach takes is not drawn (`inBreach`).

### Corner shading

`GLFX.ao` (both modes) bakes ambient occlusion into the room picture from the tile map alone, as tile/voxel AO does: each edge shaded
from its neighbour, corners from both. `aoBack`: the back wall darkens above a floor, under a ceiling or ledge, beside a wall on either
side; `aoFloor`: the walking surface darkens where it runs into a wall. Strengths `AO_K` floor .36, ceil .32, side .26, top .28, reach 20 px.
Drawn `source-atop`, so an opening onto the sky is left alone.

### Hangings and lamp scars

Decoration, not the banner he slides down ([banners](banners-and-slides.md) share `tapCloth`). `planHangings` (in `settleLevel`, no
stream) puts one at a hashed cell (one in 9 dungeon, 5 palace, 6 tile, 8 forge, 7 crypt) under a wall ceiling 2 to 5 rows up, hung from a
rod 2 px under it with its foot a head's height over the walking line (56 px under a one-storey ceiling; 118 px long ones in tall halls),
clear of windows, arches, openings, lamps, doors and `banner`/`gate`/`low`/`chomp`/`bar`/`pend`/`shaftfoot` cells. The cloth is Egyptian
painted linen in the look's pigments (`TAP`): lotus on water, lotus registers, a reed mat, a star ceiling under a kheker frieze, the winged
disc over a broad collar, a papyrus column. By wear: faded (`fadeCol`: yellow first, red, blue last), torn, down (rod on one hook, the
cloth crumpled on the floor, `drawFallenCloth`), or gone with the wall (state 4, in a breach). Lamps on damaged wall (`lightWear` 2+) have
come down: `drawLampScar` leaves the empty bracket and soot by style, a cut cord in an opening (rope since round 18b), and
`drawFallenLamp` lies broken on the floor below; `settleLights` lights a replacement within 6 columns. Lamps themselves:
[lamps and light](lamps-and-light.md).

### The room picture cache

| Event | What is redrawn |
|---|---|
| A room comes into view | `roomCanvas` builds it at once if it is needed now; otherwise `roomWork` builds one stage a light frame (three a room): a dirty room nearest the camera first (`pickDirty`), else the next room ahead in the camera's direction (`pickPrefetch`) |
| A tile falls, rubble lands, a block moves | `invalidateCell(c,r)` marks the rooms that draw that cell or its neighbours' overhanging faces and arches (columns c-1..c+1, rows r-2..r+1) dirty; the stale picture shows until the new one is complete (the falling tile is drawn live meanwhile) |
| A rewind | the snapshot's `base` and `rubble` are compared with `L`'s and only changed cells are invalidated (v53; `rewindrooms.py`: a hole knocked in a floor and new rubble, the room equals one drawn from scratch) |
| New floor, canvas resize, Graphics' background toggle | `clearRooms()` |

The cache holds 18 rooms (least recently used goes). Torchlight uploads a room whole when needed now, else in strips of 80 rows
(`g2Warm`/`g2Stage`); the relief reads its mip level 1.

## Rules for changing it

- Look only, from hashes: wear, breaches, arches, hangings and lamp scars read `L.seed` and the cell, never a generator stream. The
  open-hall pass draws its window chance from `rng` for every candidate cell, kept or not, so keeping a cell solid moves nothing else.
- Decoration reads `L.base0` (`D0`), the terrain as built, never `L.base`.
- Post and lintel everywhere but the dungeon style's round vaults.
- A new tile drawn live: leave it out of `drawRoomStatic` stage 2 and `aoFloor`, add its type to `GLREL_LIVE` (`glRelLive`), mark it with
  `fpMark` if flat, and check with `tilescan.py`.
- A new solid thing in front of the back wall: record its outline in `SHC` so `glMask` carves it (`shadowscan.py`), cut it out of the beams (tile map, `beamBlockers` or `ryBeams2D`), rub
  the relief back over it, and keep the figure's shadow off it.
- A run-time tile change calls `invalidateCell`; a rewind must restore `L.base` and `L.rubble` so the compare finds it.
- Nothing drawn on the back wall under a doorway or over a live tile; check with `doorscan.py`, `openscan.py`, `tilescan.py`.
- New hung things use rope (`barRope`, `ropeOf`), not chains.
- A change to tiles or the open-hall pass changes play: check with `metrics40.py`, `run2.py` and `lowceil.py`.
