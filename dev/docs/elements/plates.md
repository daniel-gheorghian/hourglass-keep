# Plates

Plates are floor tiles that act when he stands on them: a gate plate opens its gate (latched or on a clock), a closer slams its gate
shut, the exit plate opens the exit door, a lift plate calls a lift. Every floor has one exit plate; gate plates come from floor 3
(in-line gates), closers from floor 4, gate puzzles and remote plates from floor 4 to 7 by kind. The gates themselves are in
[gates](gates.md).

## Example
Floor 7, seed 4407 (`__HK.genLevel(7,4407)`, The Halls of the West): a double gate on row 30, the amber gate at column 304 (latched)
and the teal gate at 306 (timed, 10.8 s). Both plates stand at column 286, the teal one in a gallery two storeys up (row 28,
`where:'below'`), the amber one in a cellar (row 32, `where:'above'`), each at the far end of a side passage. Pressing teal first
wastes its clock on the long way through the cellar; the bot does the cellar, then the gallery, and runs back down to the gates. The
teal timer is `estPath` of the gallery's way back plus the drop, times `timerK()`, plus 0.3 s.

## In play
| Kind | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `plate`, latched (`latch`) | `pressPlate`: `g.tm = Infinity` (stays open); `door` sound and `gateMsg` unless `near` | in-line gates, P1/P5 branch plates (62% of timed ones made latched after the build), P3's plate, the amber double plate, the violet plate |
| `plate`, timed | `g.tm = g.dur` every frame he stands on it, `slam` cleared; the clock runs once he steps off; `gateMsg` once (`told`) | in-line timed gates, the teal double plate, P4 (`fwd`: the gate is "ahead", in the next turn) |
| `plate`, held | a 0.2 s gate: open only while something stays on it | P2 (`slabF`: a gallery slab above it) and `blockSeg` (a block) |
| `closer` | `g.tm = 0`, `slam`: the gate drops at 4/s; `gate` sound, `shake(0.9)`, hint `closer`; nothing once `jam` | right before an in-line gate (floor 4 on: 45% of in-line gates with `m` >= 3 and no pit); four in a row before P3's gate |
| `xplate` | `L.door.opening`: the exit door opens at 0.45/s (2.2 s); `door` sound; message "A heavy door grinds open somewhere on this floor." | one a floor, past 82% of the route, before the exit's guard |
| `lplate` | sends its lift (`lf`) to its end (`e`: 0 top, 1 bottom) unless docked there or ridden; `gate` sound, hint `lift` | one on each landing of a counterweight lift (see [lifts](lifts.md)) |
| `trigger` | the flask tile of a gateless secret passage: arms its crumbling chain and ambush (`armPassage`) | no gate; drawn as plain floor under the flask (see [flasks and secrets](flasks-and-secrets.md)) |
| Passage plates | a branch plate with a `chain` (P1, double) also arms it: the five sturdy cracked slabs beside the plate start to fall (`tm` from -(1.8 + 0.36 k) s) | "The cracked floor behind you is starting to give way. Run!"; the ambush (`amb`) belongs to a gateless passage's flask `trigger`, not to a plate |

What presses a plate:

| Presser | Plates | How |
|---|---|---|
| The prince | all kinds | `standOn`, called each frame while standing, running or crouching, and on landing and at the end of a step, push, pull, climb or lift ride; never in the air or hanging, so a jump clears a closer |
| A movable block | `plate` only | `updateBlocks`: a block at rest on a plate presses it every frame and sets `g.heldAt`, so the HUD shows no clock for it (see [movable blocks](movable-blocks.md)) |
| A falling slab | `plate`, `closer` | `jamAt` when a slab lands on the cell: a plate becomes latched for good (`latch`, `near`, its gate `tm` Infinity, message); a closer is jammed (`jam`) and never slams again. Rubble lies on it (`drawHeap`) |
| Guards, snakes, darts, debris other than slabs | none | no code path |

Persistence:

| Event | Plates |
|---|---|
| Checkpoint lit | `S.cpPlates = pressedPlates()`: latched plates whose gate is open for good, and jammed closers |
| Respawn there | the floor is rebuilt; those plates' gates reopen, and a jam is made again with its slab already fallen (`jamRestore`); timed plates start fresh (`respawnplates.py`) |
| Continue (`applyResume`) | a slab that had fallen on its plate or closer still lies there (`jamRestore`); latched gates reopen (`slabresume.py`) |
| Rewind | every feature field (`press`, `jam`, the gate's `tm`) comes back from the snapshot |

The puzzle menu (`puzzlePick`, `MENU`: name, weight per roll, first floor; a floor's featured puzzle family holds 55% of the draw):

| Name | Plate | Gate | From | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `P1G1` | branch: a gallery above or cellar below, walked out and back (`detourGate`) | on the corridor past the branch | 4 | 0.056 |
| `P5G1` | a one-storey alcove: a ledge up one climb, or a nook down one drop (`alcovePlate`), one obstacle in it | on the corridor | 4 | 0.03 |
| `P2G1` | under a loose slab in the gallery above (`slabGate('p2')`); the gate is held (0.2 s) | 10-13 tiles on | 4 | 0.03 |
| `P3G1` | latched plate, then four closers (too wide to jump) before the gate; they share one feature object, so the gallery slab landing on one jams all four | on the corridor | 5 | 0.022 |
| `double` | two branch plates, amber and teal; one gate latched, one timed | two gates in a row | 7 | 0.05 |

Remote plates (`remoteRoll`, `REM`), planned per run with the gate in the next turn (G2 the top of a kick shaft, G3 the foot of a
banner slide, G4 `gateSlotSeg` between two moves): P4 timed corridor plate (floor 6, 0.4; 8-13 tiles before the run's end), P5
alcove (floor 4, 0.3), P1 branch (floor 5, 0.3). A run takes one with probability 0.03, or 0.12/0.34 when the floor features that
family (0.34 for P4). A P1/P5 whose gate finds no room makes the floor lay out again (`orphanscan.py`); a P4 that finds none is plain
floor. The maze flag multiplies the puzzle option's weight by 3.5 and queues one or two more after each (`mazescan.py`: 1.5 times ordinary
floors).

The HUD (`hudLayer`): the nearest gate (by x distance plus twice the y distance) whose clock runs (`dur > 0`, finite `tm > 0`, not
held by a block within 0.2 s) shows `drawGlass` (the water clock, `nutClock`) and its seconds at the top centre, dropped to a second
line when the left HUD reaches the middle; gold over half, orange over a quarter, then red and blinking at 2 Hz.

## The prince and the guards
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Standing on a timed plate | the clock is held full; it starts as he steps off |
| Running over a closer | slam; jump it from the run (`dashjump`), or hazardEdge's edge-walk stops him short before a standing jump |
| Latched plate pressed, then a closer stepped on | the gate slams; pressing the latched plate again reopens it |
| A slab falling onto a plate while he stands under it | the slab hurts him (1 life, `crush`) as any falling slab, then lies on the plate |
| Guards | never press plates; a guard can stand past a solved gate (`gateDue`) |
| Learning | the first press sets `learn('plate')`; hints `plate`, `closer`, `slabplate`, `slabcloser`, `block`, `door`, `lift` |

## How it is drawn
Live in `drawDynamic`, both modes; the room picture leaves the cell out (`drawRoomStatic` returns before the floor tile for `plate`,
`xplate`, `lplate`, `closer`, `grate` and an unbroken `loose`), and `aoFloor` and Classic's embers skip it. `dynSlab` draws the
floor tile, then the plate is a raised slab on it:

```
  top face: floorQuad(X+1..X+31, depth 0.03..0.97), raised by lift      lift 2.4 px up; 1 px while pressed
  ________________                                                       (pressed = within 0.12 s of the last press, or jammed)
 /  plate top    /|  <- dark band under it as its lip (lift px high)
/_______________/ |
|  floor tile (dynSlab: lip, top, joints)                               cut at the face of a wall on its right (CUT_AT_WALL)
```

| Kind | Colour |
|---|---|
| `plate` | the theme's floor top (`th.top`), washed 18% white; a paired plate in `PAIRC[pair]` (amber `#e0a43a`, teal `#45b8b0`, violet `#a77ee6`) |
| `xplate` | gold `#d8b04a` with a small dark dot at its middle |
| `lplate` | `#b98a4a`; beside a lift's mouth it is narrowed by `T_SLAB + 1` and its cell is cut round the mouth slab (`liftMouthCut`, round 16c) |
| `closer` | dark red `#8f2f2a` with three dark grooves running back in depth |
| jammed | a rubble heap on it (`drawHeap`), drawn pressed |

A latched plate springs back up when he steps off (only the gate stays open); a jammed one stays down.

## Light, shadows and effects
| Pass / effect | This element |
|---|---|
| Light map (`gatherLights`) | every plate, exit plate, lift plate and closer keeps a faint `trap` glint (r 30, `225,222,240`, 0.36) 8 px above it, so the dark never hides it; both modes (Classic's `lightPass` reads the same list) |
| Key light | none |
| Lamp shadow mask | carved: its cell is FLOOR, and `glMask` carves every floor's lip and top face, so no figure's shadow lands on it |
| Relief (`glRelFloors`) | rubbed back over its top face, 1 px over its back edge, and its lip plus 3 px (`GLREL_LIVE`, round 35c); without it the back wall's art showed on it (37 of 45 plates of all kinds in `tilescan.py` before) |
| Metal | none |
| Sky beams | `fpMark` takes the scene's alpha to 254 (`FPM_A`) over its top (raised top included), so the beam's footprint (`fpGate`) lands on it as on floor |
| Floor looks (`floorFx`) | `FT_FLAT`: its tile is 128 in `FLOOR_FT`, so embers and sheen keep off it; Classic's embers skip any cell with a feature |
| Heat, the dark | none |
| Bloom, grade, vignette | none of its own |
| Classic | same drawing and glint; no relief or footprint gate |

## Rules for changing it
- A new floor tile drawn live must be left out of `drawRoomStatic`'s floor, rubbed in `glRelFloors` (`GLREL_LIVE`, or `glRelLive`),
  marked with `fpMark` if it is flat, listed in `FT_FLAT`, and checked with `tilescan.py`.
- A new plate kind needs its `standOn` branch, its glint in `gatherLights`, its colour, a hint, and the bot's action in `L.path`.
- Anything that should hold a plate across a respawn must be in `pressedPlates()` and restored after `genLevel` in `startLevel`.
- Timers on its gate come from `estPath` times `timerK()` (or the in-line run formula); the bot must still win every one (`p4bot.py`).
- New puzzle shapes go in `MENU` (with a family in `PUZ_OF`/`PUZ_FAM`) and `puzzlescan.py`.

## History
- PoP1 roots: raise and drop plates, closing plates, a slab that falls on a plate holds it (PoP levels 4, 5, 9, 11): `jamAt`.
- The puzzle menu: P1-P5 and the double gate; remote plates with the gate in the next turn; P4 races (PoP1 level 3).
- Unit G1: a remote P1/P5 with no room for its gate lays the floor out again (no dead-end rooms).
- round 16c: the lift plate cut round a mouth slab.
- round 35b: live flat tiles marked 254 (`fpMark`) so beam footprints land on them.
- round 35c: live tiles rubbed out of the relief (`glRelFloors`, `GLREL_Q` 160).
- Details in CLAUDE.md (Trap density, Forks, Fighters, 16c, 35b, 35c).
