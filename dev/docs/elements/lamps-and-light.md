# Lamps and light

The keep's lamps and torches (`L.torches`) and every other light source the frame gathers: the figures' fill light, the openings'
sky light and its bounce, the exit door's light, the ambient floor, and the dark that eats them. Lamps stand on every floor in every
theme, each theme with its own lamp shape; they are look only, but they carry most of the light, the shadows and the relief in both
modes.

## At a glance

## Example

Round 19's case: a run's fill puts a lamp at the end of a run, against the step up. The step's front face stands `HX` left of its cell,
so the lamp sat half over the face. `lampClear` fails (a WALL beside it), so `lampsClear` tries the columns 1, -1, 2, -2 ... 6 away on
the same row and takes the first that is clear and at least 3 columns from another lamp; with none, the lamp is dropped (no lamp and no
scar). `lampscan.py` found 2,165 such lamps of about 9,600 on floors 1-48 before, 0 after.

## In play

| Rule | Example or number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lamps have no collision, damage or trigger | the prince walks past and in front of them | decoration and light only |
| A lamp burns only where both cells beside it are plain floor | not beside a WALL (a face or step), a ledge (no floor), or a `gate`, `lift`, `sandcol`, `chain`, `banner`, `shaftfoot`, `bar`, `pend`, `lplate` (`LAMP_NEXT`); not in or beside a lift's shaft (`lf.ca-1` to `lf.cb+1`, rows `rT` to `rB`); not within 1 column of a water clock, 2 of the exit door, 1 of the start door; not on a feature cell (`lampSpot`, `lampClear`) | round 19, owner: "sometimes lamps or torches are placed just like that" |
| Lamps on worn wall have fallen | `lightWear` >= 2 (or in a breach): `broken`, a scar on the wall and the lamp lying broken and cold on the floor; a working one is placed within 6 columns on wall that stands, by `lampClear`, at least 4 from another lit lamp | the keep's decay (`settleLights`) |
| More fall each cycle up the tower | `L.prog.wear` 1, 2, 3: a further 10%, 20%, 30% by hash, but never the last one lit within 9 columns and 1 row | progression reads as decay, never as a dark stretch |
| The floor's light never drops under `AMB_MIN` | Torchlight's ambient is at least 0.45 of full on its brightest channel | owner, v56: "make it feel dark, not be dark" |
| Every trap keeps a faint glint of its own | a `trap` light (r 30 to 60) on spikes, plates, saws, lifts, darts, bars, the pendulum, roof blocks, falling sand | the dark never hides a trap |
| Moving or dropping a lamp never changes a layout | `settleLights` reads no generator stream | layouts identical across look changes |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Walking past a lamp | drawn over it (lamps are the first thing `drawDynamic` draws); no collision |
| Lit from one side | the HD figure's rim and shading come from `keyLight` (`sx`, `col`, `k`); the forge's lamp colour is mixed toward white (`softCol` 0.4) so it does not wash the outline red; with old-style figures only the prince gets a rim (a copy in the light's colour behind him) |
| Near a lamp in Torchlight | his and each guard's silhouette casts a soft shadow on the back wall from the two nearest strong lights (`glShadow`); the part of him hidden behind a wall casts none |
| Every figure | carries a fill light: prince r 84, a 0.42; guards r 56, a 0.22; snakes r 34, a 0.25 in `200,230,170`; never on the sky (round 25c) |
| Standing still on a lit floor | a contact shadow ellipse under him (Graphics' Lit characters, both modes) |
| In a chase | lamps ahead of the dark dim and gutter, those behind it are out; his shadow is pulled back toward the dark (`drawShut`) |
| The bot | ignores lamps |

## How it is drawn

Live, every frame, in `drawDynamic` before everything else (`drawLight` for each unbroken lamp in view). With `EGP.lamps` on (the
default) `drawLampEg` draws the theme's own lamp; off, `drawLight` draws the v61 style's (a pitch torch in an iron bracket, a brass
lantern, ...). The room picture holds only what is left of a fallen lamp: the scar on the wall (`drawLampScar`, unless the wall is
gone: `lightWear` >= 4 or a breach) and the broken lamp on the floor (`drawFallenLamp`, laid on with `rlBegin`/`rlEnd`, alpha 251); and
in the crypt style a false-door niche behind a lamp's cell (`decor`).

The light sits at `x = c*TW+16+LAMP[style][0]`, `y = SURF(r)-38+LAMP[style][1]` (dungeon [3,-7], palace [6,0], tile [0,-2], forge
[0,-8], crypt [4,3]). `drawLampEg` puts the flame there, so Torchlight's light map lines up with the drawn flame. A lamp whose wall cell
is an opening or breach (`lampFree`, only with Graphics' Background on) has nothing to hang on: it stands on a floor stand or hangs
from the keystone, and in the palace and tile styles its light is 28 px higher (palace offset [0,0]).

The light's colour is the theme's `th.glow`; in the crypt style three lamps in ten (by a hash of the cell, `warmLamp`) burn amber
(`255,188,100`).

Flames (unit FL) are one flipbook per palette built once at load (`buildFlames`): 16 frames of 40 by 64 px, a scrolling-noise
flame under a teardrop mask with a white-hot core row; `flame()` cross-fades two frames, adds the core (`lighter`, 0.5), sways the tip
and stretches it by `flK`, the same noise that flickers the lamp's light, so the wall pulses with the flame. A lamp's flame is steadier
than a torch's (0.8 against 1); flames over 9 px tear off a tongue now and then (`lick`, a pure function of time); `embers` are sparks
cooling as they rise. "Softer flashes" (`S.set.calm`) cuts the flicker to 0.3, the flipbook to 12 frames a second (20), the sparks to
half. Both modes draw the same sprites; `EGP.unlit` draws every lamp out (debug). The fire vent uses the same code with its own sheet
(`FLV`, see [fire-vent](fire-vent.md)).

Around each lit lamp in view two moths flutter (`ambientGlow`, Graphics' Atmosphere, both modes); in the forge style a burst of 14
sparks leaves a lamp in view every 1 to 2.6 s (ambient, look only).

### The key light

`keyLight(x,y)` (round 20b) answers "from which side, how strongly, in what colour" for a point (measured 26 px above it, at the
chest). Every light that is not `fill`, `trap` or `sky` and is within 1.6 times its steady radius pulls toward its side:

```
S = -0.05, W = 0.05                      // a weak constant pull from the left (the old default)
for each light q within R = (q.r0 || q.r) * 1.6:
  u = 1 - d/R;  w = (q.a0 || q.a) * u*u
  S += w * dx / max(d, 8);  W += w;  M += u
sx = clamp(S/W, -1, 1);  dir = sign(sx);  m = clamp(M/0.15, 0, 1);  k = 0.35 + (clamp(M, 0.3, 1) - 0.35) * m
col = the strongest light's colour if m > 0.5, else the theme's window colour
```

`sx` is continuous in the lamps' places and steady in time: a lamp coming into reach fades in. Before round 20b it was the nearest lamp
within its flickering radius (150 x (0.92 + `flK`)), so the granite slab near the edge of a lamp's reach flipped every few frames between
lit from the right and the default from the left. Read by: the HD figures' rim (`drawActor`), the granite portcullis (`egSlabBody`'s
`uL` cross-fade of faces and cast shadow), the slicer's jaws, the snakes, the water clock (`nutClock`), `stoneGlints`, `graniteGlints`.

### The fill light

Every figure carries a fill light (`FILL_COL` `205,195,225`; the "lit characters" look) that lights him and the stone round him. It
landed on the sky seen through an opening too: a pale halo that went with him and stopped at the sill (round 25c, owner: "what is that
haze around the prince?"). Now:

- Torchlight: the light shader writes the fill's share into the map's alpha; the composite and the bright pass subtract it where the
  scene's alpha says the backdrop shows (`l -= FILL_C x fa x 1.5`, `fa` = map alpha x (1 - scene alpha)); the figures' shadows are
  scaled by the same alpha. A figure in front of the sky covers it, so he keeps his fill to his outline.
- Classic: `fillCut` draws the fills in a canvas of their own at the light map's size and cuts them wherever the room pictures are clear
  (an opening, less any block in front of it), except over the figures' silhouettes; it returns null, and the fills are drawn with the
  other lights, when no fill reaches an opening in view.
- The `?wgl=0` fallback still lights the sky.

### The ambient floor

Torchlight's ambient is `lookOf(style).amb` (the theme's `lk` over its style's, shifted by the hour) times `glAmbK`: the style's
`GLAMBT` (palace 0.62, forge 0.6, dungeon 0.78, tile 0.8, crypt 0.9; darker than Classic's map so the gaps between lamps fall into
shadow, owner v41), raised so the brightest channel is at least `AMB_MIN` 0.45 of full. Only the low ones rise. In the Forges of Meroë at
night the ambient is (104, 87, 100): the forge's 0.6 gives 62 on its brightest channel (0.24 of full); `glAmbK` lifts the factor to
1.10, so 115 (0.45). Classic uses `lk.amb` as it is. The Crossing has its own ambient (`CR.amb`).

### The door light and the exit

While the exit door is open its `door` light burns at the door. During the exit (round 26f) `exitSwell()` rises with the exit's
progress `P.exS` from `EX_SW0` to just past `EX_GONE`, then eases back by a third before the card (`EX_N`). Torchlight swells the door's
light by it (radius x 1.3, strength x 1.75 at full; the bloom stays); Classic swells `drawDoor`'s additive sprite under him instead.
See [doors](doors.md).

### The dark's drain

In a chase `dkDrain(x)` scales each lamp's light (round 29): behind the dark's front, 0 (out, by its fade); ahead of it, at most
`DK_DRAIN` 0.36 less, falling off over about 120 px and guttering (two fast sines). The radius takes 0.8 + 0.2 of the drain, the strength
and bloom all of it; a lamp under 0.01 adds nothing. Only lamps are drained; the lamp is still drawn, under the opaque rip. See
[the-dark](the-dark.md).

## Rules for changing it

- Lamps are look only. `settleLights` (`lampsClear` first) reads no generator stream: wear and decay are hashes of the cell and
  `L.seed`. A change to placement must leave `genLevel` layouts identical and `lampscan.py` at `ok`.
- A lamp burns only where `lampClear` allows; a replacement for a fallen lamp uses the same rule. A new feature a lamp must not stand
  beside goes in `LAMP_NEXT` (and in `lampscan.py`'s copy of that set).
- A new lamp shape keeps its flame at the light's place (`LAMP[style]`, the free offsets), or Torchlight's light stands off the flame.
- Never key a look on a flickering radius or on the one nearest light: use `keyLight`'s `sx` (`r0`/`a0` give a lamp's steady values).
- Light new things through `gatherLights` (both modes read it), with the kind that does what is wanted (table in
  [rendering](../rendering.md#light-map)); do not paint light into the scene.
- Nothing a figure carries lands on the sky: a light that goes with a figure is kind `fill`, so Torchlight keeps it in the map's alpha
  and Classic cuts it (`fillCut`).
- `AMB_MIN` only raises a dark ambient; a theme's darkness comes from its `lk.amb` and the lamps' spacing, not from going under it.
- Flames come from the flipbook built at load: no per-lamp bakes, no per-frame allocation (`lightSprite` caches every glow).
- Anything new hanging from the ceiling uses rope (`barRope`, `ropeOf`; round 18b).

## History

- v41: Torchlight's ambient darker than Classic's, so the gaps between lamps fall into shadow (`GLAMBT`).
- v56-v57: `AMB_MIN` 0.45 (the Forge by Moonlight had fallen to 0.26); the sky openings' bounce light.

- Round 20a: the bounce also tests low ceilings (`solidFront`).
- Round 20b: `keyLight` is the balance of all lamps in reach, not the nearest.
- Round 25c: the fill light never lands on the sky (map alpha, `fillCut`).
- Round 26f: the exit door's light swells as he goes (`exitSwell`).
- Round 29: the dark drains the lamps (`dkDrain`).
