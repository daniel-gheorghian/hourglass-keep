# Saws

A toothed blade runs back and forth along a slot in the floor; he jumps it as it comes. From floor 5, in runs, on landings and in side
corridors, in every style; the featured obstacle of some floors (`OBS_FAM.saw`). Where a corridor lies straight over its slot it is built
as spikes instead. Forged metal: steel, or iron in the forge style; the Egyptian looks do not re-skin it.

## Example
Floor 17 seed 35 (the Green Hall of Osiris): a two-tile saw on row 63, its blade running between x 7272 and 7320 with a period of 2.76 s
(`genLevel(17,35)`, cell 227,63). The bot stands on the tile before the slot until `sawSafe` finds a run and jump that keeps 16 px from
the blade while he is low, then runs and jumps 6 px before the slot. In Torchlight a sky beam's footprint lies on the floor round it;
since round 35b it is not laid over the blade or its slot (`egyptr35b.py`).

## The prince and the guards
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| He jumps as it comes | safe once his feet are 11 px up; the drawn flight is raised so his drawn feet clear the blade where it is at each moment (`jhK`, `JH_W`) |
| He hangs or climbs over its row | not hit |
| He walks or crouches into it | one life, pushed back |
| The bot (`sawSafe`) | simulates the run from rest at `from` (35 px/s, +650 px/s² to 100), the jump at `cx(from+dir)-22*dir` once over 45 px/s (`vy = -(190+RJ_DV)/RJ_K`, `vx 183/RJ_K`, g `700/RJ_K²`), running on at 100 after landing; unsafe if within 16 px of `sawX` while less than 13 px up; then holds the way through the landing |
| Guards | not tested by the blade; `guardOk` keeps them off the feature's own tile (the slot's other tiles carry no feature) |

## How it is drawn
Room picture: the slot's tiles are plain floor slabs (marked 254); nothing of the saw is baked.

Live (`drawDynamic`, in the `L.dyn` loop, before the figures), every frame from `sawGeom(f,gameTime,ys)`:
1. the slot from `x0-14` to `x1+14`: a 1 px rim in `metalPal('saw')[1]` at `ys-2.6`, a 3.4 px dark groove;
2. the blade: a sprite per style (`sawSprites`, drawn once at 4x: `sawPath`'s 12 hooked ratchet teeth, a radial body gradient, hammer
   marks, a dark ring where the teeth begin, four lightening holes, a hub disc in `metalPal('hub')` with four rivets), rotated by
   `gameTime*14`; at speed two faint ghosts of the teeth (`mb`, fewer at the ends of the run); clipped to the groove's middle (`y < ys-0.6`);
3. the fixed sheen: two white wedges that stay put while the blade turns, only while `METAL_PAINT_HI` (Classic; Torchlight's metal
   shader draws its own);
4. a dark lip over the blade's foot and the rim's two caps beside it.

| Style | Blade | Hub | Tint (`METAL[style].tint`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| dungeon, palace, crypt | steel | bronze | per style |
| tile | steel | gold | cool |
| forge | iron | bronze | warm |

`EGP.traps` leaves the saw as it is (`METAL_EG` holds only the jaws). Sparks are particles (`FX`), drawn in `drawEmissive` over the scene.
No wear, no cut by walls (the slot stays inside its run).

## Rules for changing it
- The drawing and the metal mask read the same numbers (`sawGeom`, `SAW_R`, the slot's span): change one, change both; keep the mask's cut
  at the slot as drawn.
- The blade and slot stay at alpha 255 (they stand on the floor) and keep their `ryStand` boxes.
- A change to the blade's height or reach must follow in `jhObs` (`SAW_R` about `ys+2`) and in the bot's `sawSafe` margins (16 px, 13 px).
- A new place for a saw pushes `saw` onto `L.path` and records it in `sawAct`, so the low-ceiling pass can turn it into `tiptoe`.
- Look changes are look only: the blade's path, period and hit box are play.
