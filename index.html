How to play

Run
Hold left or right on the pad.
Tiptoe
Hold Grab and tap a direction. Stops at edges and walks over spikes safely.
Jump
Jump from a standstill clears one tile. From a run it clears two, and on a three-tile gap you catch the far ledge.
Climb
Up under an opening to pull yourself onto the ledge. Down at an edge to hang, down again to let go.
Catch
Hold Grab while falling to catch a ledge. Two-floor drops hurt unless you hang first.
Crawl
Down gets you on hands and knees, left or right crawls, up stands. Low tunnels and swinging blades call for it.
Fight
Grab strikes. Up blocks when the guard raises his blade, then strike back fast. Down sheathes. Strike snakes before they coil. Skeletons only fall to a pit, so drive them over the edge.
Gates
A plate raises a gate for a few seconds. A red plate right in front of a gate slams it shut, so jump over it. Some gates open from a plate out of sight, up a climb or down a hole, and then stay open.
Flasks
Stand on one and tap Grab to drink. Red heals one, gold adds a life, dark blue is poison.
Exit
A floor plate somewhere opens the exit. Press up at the open door.
Keys
Arrows move, Space jumps, Shift grabs, P pauses.
