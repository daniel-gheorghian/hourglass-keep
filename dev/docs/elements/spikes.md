# Spikes

Stakes in the floor, after PoP1's spikes. Live spikes lie in a run or a side passage and rise as he comes near; fixed spikes stand at the
foot of every pit (a gap or a run of loose floor) and kill whatever lands on them. Live spikes from floor 2, spiked pits from floor 1.
In the Egyptian looks (`EGP.traps`) they are bronze-tipped stakes on acacia shafts; with `EGP.traps` off, v61's steel needles.

## Example
Floor 16 seed 2 (`genLevel(16,2)`, the Forges of Meroë): a three-tile saw on row 20 had a corridor straight over its slot, so the
end of `buildLevel` turned it into spikes on columns 427-429, and its bot action into `tiptoe` from 430 to 426. Walking in with Grab
held, he steps across 13 px at a time while the stakes stand round his feet; a run or a jump onto any of the three tiles kills him.
(`sawceil.py` finds 4 such conversions on floors 1-48, seeds 1-3; floor/seed 16/2, 19/1, 29/3, 43/3.)

## In play
| Rule | Number or example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Live stakes rise when he is on the row, within 44 px of the tile's middle, on foot | `f.ext` +9/s (0.11 s up); `hold` 1 while near, then 1 s, then down at 3/s | PoP1: spikes spring as the kid comes near |
| They do not wake for him in `hang`, `climb`, `climbDown`, `air`, `jumpUp`, `wallrun`, `slide`, `swing` | the run up to them usually does (44 px from the middle is 28 px before the tile) | only feet on the floor wake them |
| A careful step is safe | `startStep`: 13 px over `STEP_T`, no spike test; `stand` on a spike tile is safe | PoP1's careful step |
| Running or skidding onto one kills | `runMove` (state `run` and `stop`): entering a spike tile with \|`P.vx`\| > 35 sets `ext` 1 and `die('spikes')`; a run starts at 35 and gains 650 px/s² | |
| Landing on one kills, live or fixed, from any height | `land()`: `die('spikes')` before the fall-damage test, so a one-storey drop into a pit kills | PoP1's spiked pit |
| Death, not a life | `die`: red sparks; card "Impaled on the stakes", then "Spikes spare a careful step: hold Grab and tap toward them." | |
| Run spikes | 1 tile, or 2 from floor 6 (40%), then one plain tile; side passages the same | |
| Fixed spikes | `fixed:true, ext:1`, never updated; at the foot of every pit 1 or 2 storeys down (`pit()`) | |
| Jumps are taken at their edge | `hazardEdge` counts a spike tile as an edge (with no floor, loose floor and a closer): a standing jump walks to it first (`tryJump`, `edgeWalk`, within 30 px), a running jump pressed early is held to the lip (within 80 px, `P.jLe`); the Grab button reads "Step" on or before spikes | |
| Hints | `HINTS.spikes` on the tile; learned by a careful step that ends on a spike tile or a jump that clears one (`land`) | |
| Touch controls | fade to a ghost when a spike tile is under them (`CTL_WATCH`) | |
| Rewind | `ext` and `hold` are restored with the features (`snapAll`) | |

## The prince and the guards
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| He tiptoes across | stakes up round his feet, no harm; the bot's `tiptoe` |
| He runs, skids or lands on a tile | death (`die('spikes')`) |
| He jumps a row of live spikes | they rise as he runs up and stay while he flies; the drawn flight is raised so his drawn feet clear each needle (`jhK`, below) |
| A pit with a lip he hangs from and drops | death on landing |
| Guards | never stand on a feature tile (`guardOk`), and their ranges stop short of one (`clearRun`); spikes test only the prince |
| A skeleton knocked into its pit (crypt, `skeleton()`) | its pit has fixed spikes at the foot; the skeleton falls and is gone (`updateGuard` state `fall`); the spikes are not tested |

## How it is drawn
Room picture (`drawRoomStatic`, floors pass): the tile is an ordinary floor slab (`slab(...,mark)`, alpha 254). The four holes the
stakes come out of (`SPK` roots, `onFloor`, dark ellipses 2.2 x 0.9 px) are drawn on the scratch canvas (`rlBegin`/`rlEnd`) and marked
`RL_A` (251): what the room's picture lays on the floor's face.

```
SPK, one tile seen from above (x 0..32 across, depth 0 front .. 1 back); odd columns mirrored about x 16
  depth .78                           x 25,   10 px high, lean +3
  depth .66      x 7,  16 px, -1.9
  depth .48                   x 19,   21 px (the tallest), +0.6
  depth .26          x 12.5, 11 px, -0.8
```
Live (`drawDynamic`, in the `L.dyn` loop, before the figures, only while `ext` > 0.02): four needles per tile, from `SPK`
`[x in tile, depth, height, lean]` = heights 10, 16, 21, 11 px at depths 0.78 to 0.26, mirrored on odd columns (`m`); height and lean
scale with `ext`. Each needle: a soft dark shadow ellipse at its root, a dark outline wedge, a lit left face and a shaded right side.

| Look | Lit face | Lower shaft | Shaded side |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EGP.traps` (default): bronze tip on an acacia shaft | `#c8a060` | `#7a5a36` (55% of the height) | `#6a4a20` |
| `EGP.traps` off (v61) | `#eef0f5` steel | none | `#8d94a2` |

```
room picture   slab (254)  +  holes (251)
drawDynamic    ... L.dyn: stakes (255, drawn live) ... guards, prince ... near halves of gates and jaws
```
- He is drawn after the stakes, so he stands in front of them.
- `CUT_AT_WALL`: with a wall in the next cell to the right, the tile is clipped at that wall's face (`X+TW-HX`), as the floor stops there.
- No wear, no theme variant beyond `EGP.traps`; the same in every style and theme.

| Sky beams | not a blocker; the footprint (`fpGate`) lands on the holes (251) and on the slab (254) but not on the stakes (255, drawn live); Classic cuts a box round raised stakes (`ryStand`: the tile, `22*ext+3` px high) |

## Rules for changing it
- Look only: `EGP.traps` changes the colours only; hit tests, timing, generator and bot are the same in both looks.
- Anything drawn live standing on the floor stays at alpha 255 (so `fpGate` and `floorFx` keep off it) and has a box in `ryStand` for
  Classic; anything the room picture lays on the face goes through `rlBegin`/`rlEnd` (`RL_A`).
- The drawn jump's clearance reads `SPK`: change a needle's height there and `jhObs` follows.
- A new place for spikes pushes `tiptoe` (or a jump) onto `L.path`; `hazardEdge` and `jsHaz` must keep listing the tile.
- Placement counts as a trap for `metrics40.py` and the plain floor after it (`breathe()`) must stay.
