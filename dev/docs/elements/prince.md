# The prince

The player's figure: a bare-chested Egyptian youth in a pleated white shendyt with a gold sash and a bronze blade, on every floor and in
the Crossing. His body and every pose come from motion capture (`dev/FIGURE_SPEC.md`); this file covers him as an element of the scene:
states, the body that plays against the drawing, what cuts him, and what he does in each light and shadow pass.

## Example

He jumps up at a ledge whose wall block stands on its near side, catches it and hangs. `updateLook` sets `P.hideMode` 1 while he
rises (`jumpUp`: the wall's front face is cut out of him) and 2 once he hangs (only what is above the ledge's top is drawn): only his
hands show, as SDLPoP's `draw_tile_fore` and `clip_char` do in PoP 1989. In Torchlight `glMask` clips his lamp shadow the same way, so
the hidden part casts none, and the stone's faces are carved out of the shadow as drawn (front face `HX` left of its cell), so no
prince-shaped dark stripe lands on the lit face under the ledge. Before round 19 a 0.4 ghost of him showed through the wall.

## In play

The body that plays is a few numbers on `P`; the drawn figure never feeds back into them.

| Rule | Number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Position | `P.x` his middle, `P.y` his feet (on `SURF(r)`), `pCol()`, `pRow()` | everything tests these |
| Width against walls, gates, lifts' gratings | half-width `BW` 6; `clampMove` tests a body band from `P.y-34` to `P.y-6` (wall cells and floor lips in that band) | a pose leaning past a wall is pressed back by the drawing (`wallPress`), not by play |
| Head | `HEAD_UP` 52 (low leap `HEAD_LOW` 44) for the air clamp under `ceilY` | a ceiling at his height makes any jump the low leap (`lowceil.py`) |
| Hands | `HANG` 57 above the feet when hanging | ledge catches (`findLedge`) |
| Other bodies | a live guard blocks him 18 px off (heights within 52 px, not a falling guard) | `clampMove` `bodies` |
| Sword | his blow lands at 6 to 40 px ahead, heights within 8 px, at `P.t` 0.17 of `strike` | `resolveStrike` |
| Damage | every hurt is one life (`hurt(1,cause)`); lives `S.maxHp` start 3, gold flasks to 10 | see [gameplay](../gameplay.md) |
| Grace after a hit | `P.inv` 0.3 s (sword), 0.6 s (snake), 0.9 s (`trapHit`) | no double hits from one trap pass |
| Trap hit while grounded | pushed back 12 px, `landHurt` 0.6 s | `trapHit` (stand, run, stop, step, fight states, land, drink, edgeWalk, lowStrike) |
| Falls | under 100 px safe; 100+ one life, `landHurt` and `P.stumble` 0.6 to 2.2 s; 170+ death; landing on spikes death | `land` |
| Look-only offsets | `P.yo` (drawn y: `jumpYo` in jumps, `jumpUp`, `bonk`), `P.pose.ox` (`poseOx`), the hang's swing (`rot`, `ox`, `oy` on `livePose`'s copy), `P.rdx`/`P.rdy` (`updateLook`), `P.exX`/`P.exY` (stairs), `P.enU` (entry) | poses never move the hit box; the bot, `estPath` and every timer read `P.x`/`P.y` only |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| A guard within 64 px on his level (`nearGuard`) | `enterGarde`: blade drawn (`draw`), `garde`; not while `P.peace` runs (1 s after Down sheathes it) or while crouched in a tunnel |
| Fight inputs | Grab strikes, Up parries (also from `blocked`), a direction advances or retreats 13 px (never off the floor), Down sheathes |
| He is struck | red `flashScreen`, `hurt` 0.35 s, knocked 7 px (never off the floor) |
| A snake on his row | Grab within 44 px ahead strikes low (`lowStrike`); in a tunnel only with the blade drawn low before (`poke`) |
| Guards and him | bodies block each other (`clampMove`); a pushed block cannot be moved onto a guard |
| The bot | plays his moves from `L.path`; fight runs (`run2.py ... fight`) parry and strike in `bot.js` |

## How it is drawn

Live in `drawDynamic`, every frame, after snakes and guards and before the near halves of gates and jaws:

```
drawDynamic, the figures' part
  snakes      drawSnakeHD | drawSnake
  guards      figClip(wallPress) | dead: deadCut | dropping in: dropCeil -> drawActor | dissolveGuard
  prince      drawShut (the dark only)
              liftOccl (when liftAround: the shaft's near wall faces)
              figClip(P.hideMode, P.hideLg, wallPress)   a ledge's wall (1-2) or slab (3-4): hands only; faces either side
              deadCut (dead: the faces within 28 px)
              doorClip (exit stairs from EX_CUT; entry while deep in the doorway)
              drawPTint (tinted or fading: exit, entry, the Crossing) | drawActor
              drawHeldFlask (drinking)
  trails, rings (fx)
  near halves: gates, jaws, a fallen roof block, the front stake; lifts (drawLift half 1: right mouths' near half, left mouths' front face)
```

`drawActor` (shared with guards):

| Step | What |
|---|---|
| `livePose` (New characters on) | breathing (`BREATHE` states), the hang's pendulum, a long fall's limbs, the kilt's hem lag, `lk.sway` |
| `updActorLook` (run each frame in `updateLookFx`) | cloth wind spring (`cw`), squash and stretch (`sq`, from `P.vy` at take-off), the sway after a sudden stop, the strike smear |
| Contact shadow | dark ellipse 11 x 2.4 px under him when grounded (not `AIRY`), Lit characters on |
| `keyLight(x,y)` | lit side `sx` from all lamps in reach (never the nearest one), colour, strength; forge light mixed toward white |
| `figDraw` | `drawFigureHD` with `PAL_HERO` (`HERO_VAR.P`: skin `#7c4a2c`, white pleated shendyt, gold sash, cropped hair; `lean`, so his own `RD`); `drawFigure` when New characters is off (Lit characters on: a mono rim copy offset by `kl.sx`, prince only) |
| Hit | `P.flash` (0.3 s from `hurt`) redraws him white on top at up to 0.75 |

Parts worth knowing: the shendyt gets a seat point `KS` once both hips flex (all fours, round 20b); the straight blade is leaf-shaped
bronze in two bevels with a bright edge, guard, grip and pommel (round 20b; steel only for a palette without `egy`), sheathed at the hip when
not drawn (`hasSword`); the khopesh is the captain's (and the page-only dresses A, B, C). Low-detail figures (`drawFigure`) do not draw `p.ox`/`p.oy`.

What cuts him:

| Cut | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| A face beside him on the right (the next column's wall, low ceiling or block) | `wallPress` (drawn left by up to `HX`+12, the rest clipped by `figClip`'s `wc` from the face over its rows) | always, both modes |
| A face on his left (round 39) | `wallPress`: the column before's front face and its side face's near half hide a mid-depth point left of that cell's right edge; drawn right by up to `HX`+4 (`figReach`'s `left`, mirrored), the rest clipped there; between two faces where he does not fit (a kick shaft, 24 px at mid-depth) drawn in the middle and cut by both | always, both modes |
| A block being pushed or pulled | `wallRows` takes the moving block's drawn face (`drawBlock`'s x), not its two cells (round 39: the press holds at -13.1 px; v83 jumped between -8 and -20) | `push`, `pull` and anyone beside a moving block |
| Behind a wall's face at a ledge | `figClip` mode 1 (`jumpUp`), 2 (`hang`, `climb`, `climbDown`): only what is above the ledge | ledge on the wall's near side (`lg.s<0`, `wallBelow`) |
| The ledge's slab, over open space (round 39, owner: "Cut the arms at the lip, keep only the hands") | `figClip` mode 3 (the slab on his right, no wall below), 4 (the slab on his left): the slab's lip and the near half of its top face (and of its end) cut him, so below its mid line only the hands' grip (above `ys`+1.5) shows | `hang`, `jumpUp`, `climb`, `climbDown` (`ledgeOf`); with the wall below on his left he is pulled 2 px, not 6, so his hands stay on its corner |
| Dead | `deadCut`: not pressed (`figReach` does not know he lies flat); he keeps the press he was drawn with (`P.rdx` held) and is cut by every wall's or low ceiling's face within 28 px, from the face's left edge to its cell's right edge | `P.state` `dead` (round 39: v83 dropped the press, so the body slid into the face; 1,878 px over faces, 0 now) |
| A lift's shaft | `liftOccl` (walls' and low ceilings' front faces round the shaft); pressed at most to the shaft's left side, and (round 39) to the right no further than its right side's face | `liftAround()` |
| The doorway | `doorClip` (`doorHead`: lintel or round vault, jambs) | exit from `EX_CUT`, entry while `P.enU>0.3` |
| Tint and fade | `drawPTint` (drawn whole into `PTB`, tinted source-atop, laid down at an alpha) | exit (whitening to `EX_GONE`), entry (dark `P.enK`), the Crossing's palette |

Every cut here is the layering rule's R1 ([layers](../layers.md)): he is L2m, the faces and lips are L3, and the room picture holding them
is drawn before him, so the stone in front is a clip, never draw order. Left as it is (round 39, D11): his shadow, the beam cut, the fill
cut, the relief's ellipse and the trails use his play x, not the pressed x (10-20 px apart when pressed).

Variants: `crossPal(tod)` multiplies `PAL_HERO`'s colours for the Crossing's hour (night `[0.52,0.58,0.78]`, dawn, dusk);
`HERO_VAR` keeps rounds 6-7's dresses (R7, A, B, C) for the pages.
