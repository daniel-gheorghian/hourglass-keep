# Swing bar

A wooden pole hung on two ropes over a pit four tiles wide, two storeys deep with fixed spikes at its floor: he jumps to it, swings
under it as on a trapeze and lets go with Jump to land on the far side. In the runs from floor 6.

## Example

Floor 6, seed 502 (`genLevel(6,502)`), heading left on row 27: the lip is column 185, the pit columns 181-184 (row 28 open, row 29
floor with fixed spikes), the far floor 178-180; the bar hangs over column 183 (`f.x` 5872, `f.y` 1708 = `SURF(27) - 80`) from the
ceiling two rows up (cell 183,25 must be wall). Rows 26 to 29 over columns 176-188 (`=` floor, `#` wall, `.` open):

```
 26  .............
 27  =====....====     he comes from the right; the bar over the third pit cell from the lip
 28  #####....####
 29  #####====####     spikes (fixed) on the pit's floor
     176  181 185
```

The bot's action is `{a:'swing',from:185,to:180}`: it runs from 187, jumps 44 px short of 185's middle and catches the bar in the air
(every jump arms `P.autoGrab`). A running catch keeps `SW_ETA` 0.7 of his speed along the swing, which is already past `SW_CLEAR`: the
chime sounds at once, the grip glows, and Jump on the forward swing throws him onto 178-180. From a standing catch instead he hangs
nearly still and pumps: a press of the way he will swing next, at human timing, about three presses and 1.4 s to the chime
(`swingfeel.py`).

## In play

| Rule | Example or number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The catch: the hands (`P.x`, `P.y - HANG`) within 15 px across and 20 px up or down of the bar (`barNear`), with Grab held or `P.autoGrab` | the bar he just left is ignored for 0.5 s (`P.lastBar`) | any jump catches it |
| The swing is a damped pendulum: angle `P.swTh` (positive forward), speed `P.swOm`; period 1.15 s; damping time `SW_TAU` 2.2 s plus friction `SW_FR` | left alone it dies to a still hang in about 4 s | v57, owner: a trapeze, not a clock |
| He hangs 34 px from the bar (his hip), `swingPlace` | the amplitude is capped at `SW_MAX` 1.0 rad | |
| A press of Left or Right (the way he will swing next, or is swinging) arms one pump (`P.swArm`); it fires as he next passes the bottom going that way within `SW_PRE` 0.8 s, or at once just past the bottom (under `SW_LATE` 0.35 rad) or from a still hang | a kick of `SW_KICK` amplitude over 0.12 s (`swingKick`, sound `swpump`) | round 16b, owner: a held push braked him whenever the switch came after the turn |
| A wrong or late press does nothing and never brakes; the held lean `SW_K` only helps (it acts only with the motion); holding one way gives one pump | | |
| Two opposite presses inside `SW_MASH` 0.2 s cancel | mashing never pumps (`swingtest.py`) | |
| Within 1.2 s of a kick the damping is lighter (`SW_TAUP` 6, `SW_FRP` 0.25) | | pumping is not fought by the decay |
| Ready (`P.swRdy`): amplitude at least `SW_CLEAR` 0.55 rad; once ready it stays so down to 0.5 | chime `swready` over `ropecreak`, a glint up the near rope, a warm glow at his hands | the throw from here clears the pit |
| Jump lets go with a throw from his speed: c = `P.swOm` / (`SW_A` `SW_W`); over 0.15: vx = 40 + 165 c, vy = -(60 + 165 c); else a feeble hop | from `SW_MAX` let go at 0 to 0.3 rad going forward he lands on c+5..c+7, never beyond; from 0.4 rad he never reaches the far side (`swingtest.py`) | the throw grows with the swing; learns `swing` |
| Down held 0.15 s with no side lets go (`P.swDn`), a plain drop | | round 16b: a thumb rocking across the pad's lower sector dropped him |
| The ropes swing too: pulled toward `SW_KAP` of his angle while he swings, free and dying down after (`barStep`) | on the feature, so a rewind restores them | |
| The run onto the bar is a running jump on `RJ_K`'s clock (round 35a); `swingCatch` multiplies the speed by `rjK()`, so the catch swings as before | `estPath` `swing` (careful 2.4, else 1.6) less 0.05 s | the flight is shorter, the catch the same |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Missing the bar, or letting go short | a fall into the pit onto fixed spikes ([spikes](spikes.md)) |
| Caught | state `swing`, `P.bar`; `hint('swing')`; the touch controls ghost while he swings over them (`CTL_WATCH` has `bar`) |
| Approaching a gap of three tiles or more with a bar in the next four cells | `hint('swing')` instead of `wide` |
| Rewind | `P.swTh`, `P.swOm` (in `P`) and `f.ps`, `f.pw` (on the feature) come back (`swingtest.py`) |
| Guards | none: never placed in a run's pit, never swing |
| The bot | pumps by holding the way he moves while the amplitude is under 0.8, then lets go of the pad and presses Jump moving forward at 0.15 to 0.3 rad; on a hang it climbs; fails with "missed the bar" or "swing short" |

His body through the swing is CMU 43_02 keyed by the swing's phase (`swingPose`, `MCK.swing`, round 23), the arms solved each frame to
the bar's grip; see `dev/FIGURE_SPEC.md`.

## How it is drawn

Live in `drawDynamic` (`case 'bar'` in the `L.dyn` loop, before the actors), the same calls in both modes. `drawBar` (round 18b, owner:
"replace the chains with rope, and make the whole thing Egyptian looking"):

```
   ceiling's lintel edge (ty = ceilingY) -- everything above it is clipped: no ceiling underside is seen
   [log end grain, 3 px proud]=====log under the ceiling, receding up-right=====
        \\ near rope (in front, wound three turns round the log)      // far rope (behind the pole, out from behind the lintel)
         \\                                                            //
          [end]======[ rawhide grip ]=========================[end]     pole: runs front to back along the depth axis
                     his hands here, on the actors' line
```

| Part | Look |
|---|---|
| Log | a round timber set across under the ceiling, its end grain to us 3 px proud of the lintel's face, a growth ring, lit on the left, a drying check |
| Ropes | `barRope`: 2.5 px, S-plied lay as diagonal grooves, each strand's crown lit on its left, a shaded right side; ply 2 or 3 by theme; loose fibres with wear |
| Pole | in its own frame along the depth axis (drawn up and to the right, as the vent's bars), a gradient body with grain; rope ends lashed three turns round each end; a frayed tail (2 + wear fibres) |
| Grip | rawhide binding 13 px long, its turns and a shine of wear |
| Ready cue (`P.swRdy`) | a glint runs up the near rope to the log over 0.45 s (from `P.swGl`); a warm glow 12 px round his hands, full on the forward swing, 0.35 on the way back, flickering; both added (`lighter`) |
| Wear (`f.wl` = `wearLevel(f.x,f.y)`) | darker timber from 2, greyer fibre (`BARF`'s four rows), more loose fibres and a longer fray |

## Rules for changing it

- The swing is a damped pendulum: never a fixed clock, and a press never brakes him.
- Every number that decides whether he clears is in `swingtest.py` (rest, hold, pump, run-in catch, releases, rewind, the cue within 2 s,
  mashing never clears); `swingfeel.py` measures human input styles. Run both after a change.
- `jsSwingPath` must use the release's own numbers (the `swing` state's throw), or the hint lies.
- Wood and rope only: no metal mask, no chains; a new hanging part uses `barRope`/`ropeOf`.
- Anything above the lintel's edge stays clipped (the view sees no ceiling underside).
- A new bar placement needs its pit, its bot action `swing` and its `estPath` time.
