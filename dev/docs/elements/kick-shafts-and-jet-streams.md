# Kick shafts and jet streams

A kick shaft is a one-tile shaft two or three storeys high with a ledge at its top: he climbs it as a chimney, locked between the walls,
one kick a press of Up, and leaves it at the top by a standing leap onto the ledge (round 48b; chambers going up, floor 5 on). The jet stream is the hint drawn for it and for two other moves: the theme's sand blown along the
move's path, as a chimney's draft carries smoke, in place of the arrows the game no longer draws anywhere (round 18).

## Example

Floor 6, seed 502 (`genLevel(6,502)`), heading left: `shaftSeg(2)` from column 186 on row 8 opens column 185 for rows 7 and 6, keeps
the wall at 186 behind him (`keepWall`), and lays the ledge at 184-183 on row 6 over the far wall; the foot cell (185,8) carries
`shaftfoot`. Rows 5 to 8 over columns 181-189:

```
 5  ..#..####
 6  ====.####     the ledge (183-184), the shaft's top storey (185)
 7  .###.#..#     walls both sides of the shaft
 8  =###====.     the foot: shaftfoot on 185
    181 185
```

The bot's action is `{a:'walljump',from:185,k:2}`: a standing Jump at the foot (facing the ledge's wall) winds up and jumps him into the
first stance, feet 50 px over the foot's floor on the ledge's wall, his back on the wall behind; Up kicks him across to the second stance
(96 px, on the wall behind; 0.32 s); the lip is 32 px over his feet there, so the next Up is the Y: 0.18 s gathering (the near foot to the
wall behind, the hands to the lip), the push up past the lip and across, the standing jump's landing, standing 12 px onto the ledge. From
the press at the foot to standing on the ledge 1.93 s (a running Jump 1.65; a three-storey shaft 2.43 and 2.15). Sand rises up the shaft
along that zigzag and spills over the lip onto the ledge.

## How it is drawn

### The kick shaft

Live in `drawDynamic` (`case 'shaftfoot'`), both modes: finding the ledge within four rows up on either side, a pale light falls down
the shaft's back wall from the ledge's top to the foot (a 16 px band, `rgba(236,228,200)` from 0.34 to 0.04) with chipped grips on
alternate sides every 14 px (a dark notch with a lit top edge). The walls and the ledge are ordinary [stonework](stonework.md). Round 18
removed the pulse of chevrons that pointed up.

### The jet stream

`jetStream(ctx,p,str,v,w,seed,x0,x1,y0,y1,wall,spr)` blows the theme's sand along a path `p` built once with `jsPath`/`jsAdd`/`jsQuad`
(a polyline from quadratic arcs, at most `JS_MAX` points, with its bounding box), with a strength `str` 0..1, a grain speed `v` px/s and a
half width `w`. Round 18c (owner: "needs to be more organic"; after Ghost of Tsushima's guiding wind and vorticles, Bridson's curl noise,
incense and chimney plumes, saltation streamers, Journey's glitter):

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Grains | up to `JS_NP` 200 (L / 1.8 x (0.55 + 0.45 str), at least 24); 70% a dense core 1.1 to 2.5 px, 30% fainter, finer dust spread 1.9 times wider |
| The draft | quickens up the path (`JS_ACC`), a long slow sway of the whole thread, gusts of strength travelling along it (`JS_GUST` 1.7 s) |
| Eddies (`JSE`, `jsTwirl`) | 2 to `JS_EDK` 12 (one per 48 px of path), turning each way in turn (a vortex street), drifting at about half the grains' speed; each twirls nearby grains about its centre by an angle falling off as a Gaussian (divergence-free: no bunching); weak at the entry, strong further on, easing at the exit |
| Puffs (`JS_PUFF` 7) | grains ride in clumps with a dense head and a tail that stretches behind; each pass thick or thin, wide or narrow (hashes of the cycle) |
| Life | each grain fades in over the first 7% of the path and out before or at the exit; the last 20% fans out, slows and rises (a free jet spreads, warm air rises) |
| Walls (`wall` {`wx0`,`wx1`,`wy`}) | in a shaft, below the ledge's level, a soft exponential clamp keeps grains 2 px off the inner faces: thrown wide by an eddy they hug the wall and slide along it, then peel off (Coanda). `wx0` is the cell's left edge `X`, `wx1` the right wall's face `X+TW-HX` (round 39: it was `X+TW`, so grains kept to `X+30` ran 6 px onto that face, 236 of 236 shafts) |
| Streaks (`jsStamp`) | each grain is a soft sprite stretched along its own motion between now and `JS_DT` 0.05 s before (at most 14 px), through a transform and its exact inverse: nothing allocated, state left as it was |
| Batches | (1) added light (`lighter`): haze wisps (6 to 26, growing as they go, on the same eddies) and a faint glow round the larger grains; (2) the grains (`source-over`, `jsSprites().g`: a radial sprite in `SAND_LOOK[look]`, cached per look); (3) rare glints (`lighter`) |

Everything is a pure function of the clock and each grain's index (hashes of index and seed): no state, no allocation a frame, the same
after a rewind. Drawn only when the path's box is within 30 px of the view; one `drawImage` per grain or wisp, which `G2D` gathers into
one draw per batch in Torchlight.

Where it is drawn (`drawJetHints`, in `drawDynamic` after the secret cues, before the flasks and the actors):

| Place | Path | Strength | v, w |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every kick shaft, ledge on either side (`L._jet.shafts`, from each `shaftfoot` with a ledge within five rows) | drawn in at the foot, up into the first stance on the ledge's wall, kick to kick on the stances' grid (round 48b: `chimH`, one arc a kick, each topping out at the far wall), the Y over the lip onto the ledge; `wall` = the shaft's faces | 1 until `shaft` is learned, then 0.65; x0.85 while he is in it and not standing | 90, 3 |
| The jump up to a ledge across a pit (bot action `jgrab`, `L._jet.jg`) | the run-up, the leap caught at the ledge's edge, pulled up onto it | 0.85, only until `jgrab` is learned (a running jump catching a ledge over 40 px up, `learnGrab`) | 95, 3.6 |
| The swing's throw while `P.swRdy` (`jsSwingPath`, rebuilt each frame in `JS_SW`) | from the back of his swing through the bottom, then the flight the release would give at this amplitude, to where he lands or meets a wall | ramps in over 0.4 s from the cue; x1 swinging forward, x0.5 back; x0.75 once `swing` is learned | 125, 4 |

Not on gap jumps, lifts, banners, hurtdrops or secrets (clutter, or against their look). The Crossing calls the same routine with its
own sprites (`spr`, `crossSprites`) for sand streaming off the dunes' brinks and the gust ([the Crossing](the-crossing.md)).

## Rules for changing it

- No arrows in the world (round 18, owner): a hint that shows a way is the jet stream, never a chevron or arrow; the paternoster's and the
  kick shaft's chevrons are gone.
- The jet stream is look only and a pure function of the clock: no state, no allocation a frame, nothing from a generator stream; three
  sprite batches a stream; drawn only in view.
- A new place is one entry in `jetPlaces` (or a path built per frame in a kept `jsPath`, as `JS_SW`) and one call in `drawJetHints`; its
  path must be the move's real path (the swing's uses the release's own numbers).
- Keep it off places where it would clutter or fight the look: gap jumps, lifts, banners, hurtdrops, secrets.
- Kicks are the locked chimney's only (round 48b): no kick off a single wall; a lift shaft never locks; `chimtest.py` and `liftkick.py` must pass.
- The stances stay on their fixed grid whatever the way in, and the jet stream's zigzag follows it (`jetPlaces` reads `chimH`).
- The jet stream is air in the corridor: its clamp stays between the faces as drawn (`wx1` on the right wall's face), never on the stone
  (layering rule R1).
