# The damaging drop (hurtdrop)

A one-tile hole in a floor with plain floor two storeys (128 px) under it and a broken lip with nothing to hang from: dropping in
costs exactly one life and skips a stretch of the safe way. It is placed only where it is worth that life. Two homes: the chamber
shortcut `hgap` (floor 5 on) and the damaging forks, kinds 2 and 4 (floor 6 on). Same look in every theme.

## Example

A chamber going down, floor 5 or later. After a landing from which exactly two storeys and two moves remain, `hgap` cuts a hole in
the landing and `hurtCorridor` runs the floor under it along to the chamber's own foot. The safe way jumps the hole and takes the
remaining moves and their landings, at least one with an obstacle; the drop lands two storeys down on that foot's floor with a
life gone. If the saving is under a quarter of the chamber's careful time or ten tiles of walking, the kit is tried again without the
hole. `hdtest.py` finds such floors (floors 5 to 27, seeds `40000+s*131+n*17`) and plays the drop.

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Walks or runs off the lip | falls two storeys, one life, `landHurt` and a stumble, then he goes on along the lower floor |
| Jumps it | a standing jump clears the one-tile hole (`sjump`): the safe way |
| Presses Down at the lip | no hang (`edgeBelow`) |
| One life left | the drop kills him (`hurt` reaches 0): the same one life |
| Guards | guards never stand on chamber landings, and a fork's short way holds one only in kind 1; nothing about the hole tests guards |
| Bot | jumps it; `hdtest.py` replaces the chamber's long stretch with one `drop`; `forktest.py` plays `L.forks[i].sc`, whose first action (`drop2`'s `hangdrop`) it swaps for `drop`, since the lip cannot be hung from |

## How it is drawn

Baked into the room picture (`drawRoomStatic`, stage 2, floors), drawn from the tile type so every hurtdrop looks the same; the same
in Classic and Torchlight. It is drawn on the scratch canvas of `rlBegin` / `rlEnd`, so what covers a floor face is marked `RL_A` 251.

```
   row r      ====[bites]|  hole  |[bites]====     lip and top face of both neighbours bitten
                         |########|                 a few stones hanging from the broken floor (th.lo)
   row r+1               |########|                 dark shaft, full cell (rgba 2,4,16 .84 -> .93)
   row r+2               |#######_|                 shaft stops 18 px short of the floor
              ===========[ rubble ]===========      drawHeap(force): a pile of fallen stone on the floor
```

| Part | Look |
|---|---|
| Broken ends | on each floor cell beside the hole: soot and dust on the slab behind the lip, six jagged triangular bites of different sizes out of lip and surface with pale edges, four small dark chips on the top face near the end, a faint crack |
| Shaft | both empty cells darkened (the second to `TH-18`), three jagged stones still hanging from the broken floor at the top of the first |
| Landing | `drawHeap(..., true)`: a shadow and a pile of stone chunks on the floor two storeys down, as on a jammed plate |
| Variation | hashes of the cell (`mulberry32(hash2(...))`), no stream; the theme shows only in `th.lo` and the floor's own slab |

Never like a spiked pit or a plain gap: a plain gap has clean lips, a fork's safe hole (kinds 1 and 3) is a `loophole` or an existing
feature marked `fork`, and lifts are "never broken, never like a hurtdrop".

## Rules for changing it

- Owner (2026-10-14): a drop that costs health must be worth it and must look like one. Keep the worth tests (obstacle or guard
  skipped, 25% of the route by `estPath`, 10 tiles); if they fail, place nothing.
- Its look comes from the tile type only: never a spiked pit's or a plain gap's look, never theme art in its place.
- Exactly one life at full health: keep the drop at two storeys (128 px) and the lip ungrippable (`edgeBelow`).
- The bot and the length metrics take the safe way; `hdtest.py` and `forktest.py` take the damaging one. A new placement records
  itself in `L.hurtdrops` or `L.forks` so they can find it.
- Looks use hashes, no generator stream; the chamber roll uses `vrng`, the forks `frng`.
