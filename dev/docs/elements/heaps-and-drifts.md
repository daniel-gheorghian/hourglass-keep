# Heaps and drifts

Slowing terrain on the plain floor of a run (round 43, owner: "build something like 'hills' in straight stretches, the purpose is to
slow him down"). There are two kinds:

- A **rubble heap**: two tiles, its crest 22-25 px. He clambers over it, holding the arrow.
- A **sand drift**: 3-6 tiles, knee deep. He wades through it, holding the arrow, with no run and no jump.

Neither is a trap or a feature. They are a list of their own, `L.hills`, laid by a pass that uses hashes only after the floor is built, so
layouts, `L.path`, the trap counts and the timers do not change.

## Example

Floor 21, seed 45063, the Foggaras by moonlight: a run heading left holds a three-tile drift.

1. He runs in at 100 px/s and drops to the wade (42 px/s) as his feet reach the sand.
2. The wind blows against him (round 43b), so he meets the steep slip face first and wades up it with the walk's short steps, sunk to the
   knee and leaning into it, sand streaming off the crest toward him, a soft sand footfall each step.
3. Over the brink he goes down the long, gentle windward back and speeds up to the run again within 0.1 s of leaving it.
4. Jump does nothing while he is in it.

On floor 5, seed 43485, in the Halls of the West, a two-tile heap of limestone blocks stands on a run. Holding the arrow, he clambers up
and over it at 35 px/s, drawn on its surface; a running jump taken before it clears it.

## Placement

`placeHills(L,n,seed)` runs last in `genLevel`. It takes plain floor from the run menu's own picks (`flat` segs), never `breathe()`'s
padding after a trap.

A cell can take a hill only when all of these hold:

- it is FLOOR with no feature, and no wall above it;
- it is not on a timed stretch: no seg from a timed gate's plate (`!latch`, `dur` > 0) to its gate, and for a plate off the route, no
  cell on the gate's row between the two;
- it is not in the dark's chase (its rows, its columns ±4);
- it is not on a guard's or snake's ground (`lo..hi` ±2 cells), nor an ambush's;
- it is not a lamp's own cell, and it is not within a cell of a clock or a flask;
- it is not within 2 cells of either door, not by a lift (its columns ±2), a movable block's track (±2) or a side passage (±1).

The hill then keeps plain cells along the run, on plain floor (breathe's padding may serve):

| Margin | Cells | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Before it (`HILL_M0`) | 1 | |
| After it (`HILL_M1`) | 3 | a running jump's run-up (the bot's `rjump` starts two cells back) stays off it |

How many and which kind:

- **How many:** at least one a floor wherever a stretch can take one, and one per `HILL_RATIO` (4) stretches that can. Stretches are
  picked by a hash of the seed and the stretch.
- **Heap or drift:** heaps from floor 3. From floor 5, in the sandy themes (`HILL_SANDY`: every theme but the Waters of Nun, the Green
  Hall of Osiris and the Stelae of Aksum), a stretch of 3 or more takes a drift 60% of the time, else a heap.
- **Heap look:** B1, or B2 where the theme builds in mud or kershef (`HILL_MUD`: Kerma, Shali). One heap in four is B3.
- **Sizes:** a heap is 2 tiles. A drift is 3 + 0..3 tiles, as the stretch allows; one drift in three has half-buried blocks.
- **Headroom:** a heap needs the cells above it open, since he stands up to 25 px higher. A drift needs only no wall above it.
- **The timers:** they do not count the slower pace, because no hill lies on a timed stretch. estPath is unchanged.

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Running into a drift | his speed falls to 42 px/s, and he walks (the walk cycle from the clip, 43 px a cycle) leaning 0.1 forward, up to 0.25 more up the slip face into the wind (round 43b, look only; the pace stays 42) |
| His drawing in a drift | 13 px (`HILL_WD`) under its surface at mid depth, never under the floor. The near sand covers his shins, so the legs go down into it, knee deep |
| His drawing on a heap | on its surface at mid depth (up to 25 px; B3: on the column's top, 22 px), easing as `updateLook` does. Up its slope he leans up to 0.35 more |
| Standing, crouching, careful steps | as on the floor, drawn on or in it |
| A jump landing in or on it | lands on the floor's level as always, drawn on or in it |
| Sound | a sand footfall (`sandstep`) each wading step |

## How it is drawn

Each half of each hill is painted once, into a canvas at the view's scale `VS` (as the room pictures are), and laid down every frame
(`hillCanvas`, `drawHills`). The canvas is remade when `VS` or the theme changes.

The surface is a height field `hillH(h,u,d)`:

- `u` is px along the hill, `d` the depth from 0 at the front plane to 1 at the back wall;
- a point is drawn at `x + (d - 0.5) DX`, `y + (0.5 - d) DY - h`, so it lies on the floor's top face in the corridor's oblique view;
- the surface is drawn as quads, 4 px by 8 depth rows (`HILL_R`), back to front;
- each quad is shaded by its slope against the key light's side (`keyLight` sx, read when the canvas is painted), as `egSlab`'s faces
  are.

and the blocks; then blocks, darts, flasks, the figures; then `drawHills(...,1)`, the near rows, near stones, grit and ripples, and the
glints. The near half covers his shins (a drift) or his toes (a heap), so no clip is needed.

| Pass / effect | This element |

## Rules for changing it

- Placement stays stream-free (hashes) and after everything else. Layouts, `L.path`, features and timers must stay identical
  (`layoutcmp.py`).
- Only the run menu's plain floor (`flat` segs): never `breathe()`'s padding, a timed stretch, the chase or a fighter's ground. Keep the
  margins (`HILL_M0`, `HILL_M1`) so no jump's run-up is slowed. If a hill may ever lie on a timed stretch, `estPath` must count it first.
- Look only: P.y and every hit box stay on the floor. A new state he can be in on a hill must be in `BLK_STEP_ST`, or he is drawn on the
  floor.
- The canvas ends at the floor's front edge (L3 is never covered). A change to the surface must follow in `hillSil` (SHC, relief,
  footprint).
- Checks: `hillscan.py`, `run2.py`, `layoutcmp.py`, `layerscan.py kinds=heap,drift`, `shadowscan.py kinds=heap,drift`.
