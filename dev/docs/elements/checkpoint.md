# Checkpoint (the sky clock)

A clock on a small pedestal standing on the floor of a run. Touched, it lights, refills the rewinds (`S.sands`) and becomes the place he
rises at after a death on that floor. Every floor has them, about one every 66 tiles of route, and one just before the dark's chase.

The clock drawn is **Nut's sky clock** (round 8, `nutClock`): Nut arched over Geb on hands and feet, twelve hour stars inside her arch, the
sun disc riding from her mouth toward her feet as the time runs out. CLAUDE.md, the tools and some comments still call it "the water
clock" (the Karnak clepsydra of round 4) or "the hourglass" (`drawHourglass`, `HG_MAT`); round 7's barque clock (`barqueClock`) and the
other concepts (`conceptClock`, `conceptClock2`) are kept only for the picture tools (exported on `__HK`). The game's words say "sky clock".

## Example

Round 37c (owner, iPhone screenshot): a clock at the end of a run with a wall block in the next cell. The clock stands at mid-depth, about
32 px wide at scale 1.9; the wall's front face is drawn HX to the left of its cell, so Nut's feet, the arch, the pedestal's rubble and the
halo lay up to 7 px over the brick face. Now `cpX` moves the drawing left until its right reach `CP_HR` ends `CP_GAP` short of the face, and
`cpCut` clips it by the faces beside it. The trigger still reads `cp.x`. `cpscan.py` found 88 of 88 such clocks over a face in each mode
before, 0 after (floors 1-48, seeds 1-3).

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| He walks, runs or lands on it | lights at once if within 14 px; it is not solid and never blocks |
| He jumps over it | nothing until he stands within 14 px on its row |
| A guard reaches it | nothing: only `P` is tested |
| The bot | lights it in passing; there is no checkpoint action |
| Rewind | the rewind ring (`snapAll`) holds no checkpoint state: a clock lit after the rewind's target stays lit, and `L.cpIndex` keeps it as the rise point. Continue (`snapshotResume`) does save the lit clocks (`cps`) |

## How it is drawn

Drawn live in `drawDynamic` (after the lamps and doors, before the lifts and `L.dyn`), never in the room picture. Same calls in both modes.

```
pedestal (drawHgPedestal: FIT_STONE, style ornament, wearLevel)   } cut by cpCut
contact shadow, lit ring on the pedestal top                       }
halo (lit: radial 30 px, 'lighter', theme glow)                    }
[Classic with Lighting off only: wide glow 70 px, not cut]
nutClock at s 1.9, bobbing 1.6 px (cy = floor - 25)                } cut by cpCut
rising drops of light (lit: 5 cool specks, 'lighter')              }
```

- Place: `cpX(cp)` is `cp.x` (the cell's middle) moved left so `x + CP_HR + CP_GAP` stays short of a nearer face in the next cell, or right
  so `x - CP_HL - CP_GAP` clears one in the cell before; never out of its cell. `cpFace` counts a `WALL` (full TH) and a `low` ceiling
  (TH-34 = 30 px) as faces.
- Cut: `cpCut` clips with an even-odd rectangle per face in the cells c-1..c+1 on its row (the face drawn HX left of its cell).
- Pedestal: the theme's drawing style's stone (`FIT_STONE`, shared with the dart spout) with that style's ornament (iron band, carnelian inlay,
  lapis tile, glowing seam, turquoise glaze), chipped and cracked by `wearLevel` at its place; the clock above it is never damaged.
- Unlit: `nutClock` with `dim` (stars out, no sun, gold dull). Lit: `glow` on, `kl: keyLight(x, cy+12)` gives her body a thin sheen on the
  lamp's side.
- `CP_DBG.off` draws only the room glow (cpscan's comparison frame); `CP_DBG.old` restores v81's placement with no cut.

The same `nutClock` draws the HUD's three rewind measures (`drawGlass`, scale 0.86: lit, spent and dim, or running back in a rewind), the
nearest timed gate's clock at the top centre with its seconds (amber, orange under 50%, red and blinking under 25%), the roundel on a timed
gate (`gateRoundel`), and the rewind's large clock running back ("The hours turn back", `rev`: a cool white sun). Every timing is
`S.sands` and the gate's `f.tm`; the clock only shows them.

## Rules for changing it

- Look only: the trigger (14 px of `cp.x`), the respawn spot and every timing read `cp.x`; only the drawing reads `cpX`. Keep it so.
- Everything the clock draws is cut by the faces beside it, except the room light (a lamp-like light at mid-depth); `cpscan.py` compares
  the faces' pixels with and without the clock and must print `cpscan: ok`.
- A new face kind (a new near block) must be counted in `cpFace`, or the clock draws over it.
- `CP_HL`/`CP_HR` are the drawn reach at scale 1.9 with the outline; change them with the scale or the art.
- The pedestal's damage is a hash of the cell (`hash2(cp.c*37+11, cp.r*19+3)`) and the bob a function of `cp.x` and the clock: no
  generator stream.
- A HUD clock passes the clock's time only while it runs (`drawGlass`): a still clock draws the same calls, so the HUD canvas (redrawn
  only when its drawing calls change, `hudKey`) is not redrawn for it. New HUD timers use `drawGlass`.

## History

- round 4: the water clock (Karnak clepsydra) replaces the hourglass as look and words; `S.sands` and timings unchanged.
- round 7: the barque clock (lotus bowl, djed uprights); kept for pictures only.
- round 8: Nut's sky clock replaces it everywhere (checkpoint, HUD, gate timers, rewind); owner: nothing like a water glass or hourglass.
- round 19: `lampClear` keeps lamps off the clock.
- round 37c: `cpX`, `cpCut`, `cpscan.py`: never over a wall's, step's or low ceiling's face. Details in CLAUDE.md.
