# Roof blocks

A block of the ceiling's lowest course that lets go as he passes under it: sand trickles from its joints, it shifts for 0.3 s and
drops, and then lies across the corridor as a knee-high obstacle he walks over. Feature `stone`, on floors built in the dungeon
generator style (`L.gstyle`), so in the Waters of Nun, the Foggaras of Garama and the Labyrinth of Hawara, floor 2 on.

## Example

Floor 2 in the Waters of Nun, `stones()`: five floor tiles, a block over the second (and over the third, 60% of the time). He runs
in; at 22 px from the block's middle sand begins to pour from its two ends and it creaks; 0.3 s later it drops, and at 100 px/s he is
38 px past it when it reaches head height. Had he stopped under it he would lose a life ("Crushed by a falling block"). It lands across
the corridor with a crash, a billow of sand and a small shake; he can walk back over it.

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Running through | safe at full speed (`runover`) |
| Stopping or stepping carefully under it | hit if within 11 px of its middle as it passes his head |
| Jumping under it | not hit (`air` is exempt) |
| Hanging or climbing | does not trigger it; if it is already falling, a hit costs a life with no `landHurt` |
| On the lying block | walks or runs over it with no key; drawn standing on its top; the near half is drawn again after him |
| Guards | never trigger it and are never hit; `clampMove` stops a guard at its edge (`sc*TW+16 - dir*BLK_W/2`) while the guard is on the floor's level: guards keep to their side |
| Bot | `runover`: runs through holding the way, no Grab |

## How it is drawn

Drawn live (`drawDynamic`, `drawStone` in the `L.dyn` loop) in both modes; the room picture adds only the rubble chips on the floor
(`L.rubble`, inside `rlBegin`/`rlEnd`). The block is a box in the game's projection (`stoneBox`: front face, top receding up and
right, right side face), textured with its theme's stone (`THEME_STONE[look]`, `stoneTex`: soft grains and crystals drawn once at 4 px
a pixel and sheared with each face, so the pattern travels with the stone) and worn as the wall there (`wearLevel`, `stoneWear`).
Stones by theme: Nun granite, Foggaras sandstone, Hawara quartzite (the table has the other nine looks too). Granite-like stones
(`cry`) twinkle (`stoneGlints`, additive) while hanging.

| State | Drawn |
|---|---|
| Waiting (`f.s 0`) | `egRoofHang`: only its front face, flush with the ceiling's front plane (`HX` left of the tile's middle), a hair proud, sand in its joints; thin slow trickles from both ends (`sandFlow` at `k` 0.05), cut at the next column's front plane (round 39: the right one at `X+27` ran down over a wall's face under a high ceiling, floor 41, and over a ledge's lip and top: `nearCut` from the ceiling's row down, and a wall's face on its own floor by a rectangle) |
| Shifting (`f.s 1`) | it sags 0.6-2.2 px, its joint with the ceiling opens dark; the pour grows exponentially (`sandK(f.tm,0.15,0.06)`: one to three strands, wider, faster); heaps (`egHeap`) build on the floor under both ends once `k>0.2` |
| Falling (`L.debris` `rock`) | `egRoofFall`: front, top and right faces; sand still pouring from the slot behind it; a billow (`sandBillow`) as it lets go. All of it drawn only in the slot's hole and below the ceiling's edge, with the next column's front plane cut out (`roofFallClip`: two rectangles, then `nearCut` on that column from the ceiling's row to the block's floor; round 39). Before, for its first 0.15 s its top and right side lay over the ceiling's face round the slot (411 of 411 blocks) and under a high ceiling (floor 41 seeds 1 and 3) the whole fall crossed a wall's face in the next column (2,207 / 5,934 / 5,720 px in the audit's frames, 0 now) |
| Fallen | `egRoofSlot`: a dark hole in the ceiling with its left inner face showing, as a doorway's jamb. `egRoofLying`: a shadow on the floor round its foot, two sand heaps beside it, the block chipped one wear level more with fresh dust, `wearRubble` along it; for 1.3 s after landing a billow and the dust settling (`sandSettle`) |
| Hanging over the HUD band | `hudStones`: the HUD's top gradient leaves a hole over a hanging block, so it shows as the scene lights it |

Draw order of the lying block, so his feet stand on it at his depth (the corridor's middle):

```
drawStone (full block, depth 0..1)  ->  snakes, guards, prince  ->  egRoofLying(half): depth 0..0.5 again, faces only
```

`EGP.traps` off (debugging, v61's look): a cracked ceiling stone with a jagged fissure that shakes while shifting and sheds dust motes,
a plain rock polygon falling, and no lying block drawn (play is the same). Sand colours follow the theme (`sandC`, `SAND_LOOK`).

## Rules for changing it

- The lying block is a look-and-step obstacle: never solid to him again, and `blockStep` stays look only (place, falls, hit boxes on
  the floor). Guards keep to their side.
- Its look keys on hashes of its cell (`hash2`, `roofStone`'s `sd`), never a generator stream.
- The generator never reads the theme: it places blocks by `L.gstyle`. A block in a new theme takes that theme's stone from
  `THEME_STONE`.
- A change to the fall or hit must keep `runover` at full speed safe (`run2.py`, 0 damage).
- A change to the block's box must follow in its `SHC` outline (`shcBox`), in `glRelRoof` and `glRelRoofFall`, and in `roofFallClip`.
- The ceiling's face round the slot and the next column's front plane are nearer than the falling block: it stays inside `roofFallClip`
  (layering rule R1; `layerscan.py`).
