# Openings and backdrop

Windows, open arches and breaches in the back wall, and the theme's distant view seen through them. Arches stand along most floors with
headroom; windows sit in the headroom the open-hall pass makes; breaches open where the wall is partly fallen. All of it is look only and,
but for the windows' placement, a hash of the cell and `L.seed`. With Graphics' background off they are painted instead of cut open.

## Example

He drops one storey (a third of the screen). Until round 25e the view was pinned to the screen vertically, so every opening slid over it at
the camera's full speed: a window went from the dark land through the bright horizon into the sky. Now each opening has an anchor, the camera's
rest on the floor under it (`bgAnchor`); its view sits where it sits at that rest and moves with the keep, less a parallax of
`BG_VK` (0.2) of the camera's travel away from the rest for the farthest layer, `(1-f)` of it for a layer of depth `f`. The window keeps
looking at its own storey's horizon; the view moves 0.8 to 0.85 as far as the stone.

## In play

| Rule | Example or number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Openings are look only | a window, arch or breach is back wall to play: nothing passes through it | the keep's back wall is never walked |
| Arches stand two storeys high | `isArchTop(c,r)`: an `EMPTY` cell over a floor cell, not between two walls, not a lift shaft's cell, in 3 rooms of 4 (`hash2` per room); drawn from the cell's top to the floor below (`Y+2*TH-4`) | a hall with headroom reads as a colonnade |
| Some stretches open onto the view | `isOpenArch`: per block of four columns, 40% by hash, never two open blocks in a row; cut from the parapet (18 px) up to the crown | the keep keeps its walls |
| Windows only where the open-hall pass made headroom | 16% of opened cells (`rng`); none where the pass kept stone (the window draw is made anyway) | so the rest of the floor is the same |
| No window in an open arch or a breach | `inOpening`, `inBreach` | the larger opening shows the view |
| Background off (Graphics) | arches are dark recesses, windows painted (`skyOf`), breaches show mortar; `clearRooms()` on the toggle | cheaper; the same layout |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Standing in front of an opening | his figure covers the sky; the fill light he carries stays on his outline and never lights the sky (round 25c) |
| In a beam from an opening | a weak, capped cool light on the side toward it, no bloom (`RY_FIG`, `RY_FIGC`); in Torchlight his body shades the haze (round 28b). See [rendering](../rendering.md) |
| His lamp shadow | Torchlight's (mask r) is scaled by the scene's alpha, so it falls on the back wall round an opening, never on the sky |

## How it is drawn

### The openings in the back plane

Drawn in `drawRoomStatic` stage 0 (see [stonework](stonework.md)): arch recesses, then breaches cut through, then arch frames in front of
any break, then windows. With the background on an opening is cut out of the room picture (`destination-out`); the backdrop is painted
behind the room pictures, so it shows only there. Bars, grilles and tracery stay in front.

| Style | Arch (`drawArch`, `archPath`) | Window (`windowPath`, at `c*TW+16`, `r*TH+38`) | Window, background off |
|---|---|---|---|
| dungeon | round vault, thick ring with voussoirs, stone piers | barred slit 5.2 x 19 (an upright bar and two cross bars) | painted sky, two stars |
| palace | lintel under a cavetto cornice, slender posts | clerestory 12 x 26 under a lintel (`winArch`), open | sky gradient with the moon, a far pylon, obelisk and palm |
| tile | lintel under a cavetto cornice, faience ring | clerestory with a grille of upright stone slats | the theme's glow |
| forge | lintel under a cavetto cornice, dashed ring, stone piers | round, 6.2 radius, iron grid | the furnace's orange glow |
| crypt | lintel under a cavetto cornice | five-pointed star in a round frame | a pale star |

Post and lintel everywhere but the dungeon style's vaults (themes Nun, Garama, Hawara). An open arch has a sill on its parapet; wear
chips the sill, the ring and the posts, and at wear 3 snaps a post (see [stonework](stonework.md)). A breach opens the shape of its hole
(its pieces) with the wall's thickness inside.

### The backdrop

`drawBackdrop` runs first in the frame (after the void's fill), before the room pictures, and only over the boxes the openings cover (`openingsIn`: open arches,
windows by their style's box, open breaches; neighbours with the same anchor merged). `paintBackdrop` composes the sky and layers into
a target at `BGQ` (0.42) of screen resolution (`bgC` in Classic, `GLS.Fbg` in Torchlight) and lays one scaled copy on the screen.

Each look's `BGART` entry is a sky (four stops) and layers far to near, each a strip `BG_W` 640 by `BG_H` 210 painted once per look, time of
day and scale (`bgLayers`), drawn three times a strip apart so it tiles. A layer moves `f` of the camera's horizontal travel (`f = Z0/Z`:
a camera `Z0` 5.65 m from the play plane, the layer `Z` away), so it slides past the openings by `1 - f`. A thing `H` m high drawn `h` px
high stands where `f = h/(s H)`, `s` = 29.4 px/m (the prince's 1.75 m is 51.5 px): the factor follows from the drawing's own size (round 27b;
before, factors were 3 to 10 times too large).

The sky, stars, moon and sun are at infinity (0); 0.25 (`near`) is the nearest anything in a view stands. Clouds and smoke also drift with
the clock. Every layer's colour turns with the time of day (`todColor`, `todSky`: night darker and bluer, dawn rose-gold at the horizon);
a far ridge is mixed toward the sky's horizon stop (`bgHaze`, its `haze` 0.5 to 0.55: aerial perspective). A night sky gets stars even
where its look has none, unless `nostars` (Meroë's smoke). Stars: 90 at dusk, 170 at night, 22 faint at dawn. At dawn a sun glow sits just
under the skyline at 0.62 of the view.

Vertically (round 25e) each opening looks out at the eye level of its own storey, and since round 47b (owner chose C, "one horizon per
storey") every opening of a storey shares one anchor and one horizon:

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Anchor | `bgAnchor(r)`: `bgRest` of the opening's foot (`bgFoot`): a window's own (it stands in the headroom row, so the rest of the row above its floor); an open arch, a breach or a court bay counts its foot one storey up (round 47b, owner: "Do NOT move the windows' anchor ... the court and the arches on a storey must match the windows"), so all the openings of a storey share the window's anchor. Openings that touch share the largest one's anchor (found once a floor, `L._bgA`), so no seam runs through a hole |
| The horizon | `bgEyeY(f,an)` = `BG_EYE` - (1 - `BG_VK` (1 - f)) (camera y - anchor - TH): at the camera's rest on the storey's floor the eye line of every depth is the floor's eye, 44 px over its back edge (`BG_EYE` 67.2 px from the view's top), the court's and the Crossing's; away from it 25e's parallax |
| A layer | `bgLayerY(f,an)`: its ground line (strip y `BG_BASE` less its `lift`) at the horizon plus 29.4 f Hc px: the land lies under the eye by perspective, Hc the keep's height over it |
| The tower | `bgHc()`: 9 m on floor 1, 0.6 m more a floor, to 60 m (floor 86): as he climbs the near land falls away and the far ridge stays on the horizon (until 47b `L.prog.sink` lowered every layer 1.7 px a floor to 90 px, the horizon too) |
| The interiors | `BGART` `inner` (Nun's Cistern, Meroë's furnace halls): their halls keep 25e's old placement (`sink` and the storey clamp), on the shared anchor |
| The sky | its gradient (made once a time of day) and the dawn sun move as the farthest layer (`BG_FSKY` 0), the horizon stop (0.72) on the horizon; stars, moon and clouds (`sky`) stand `BG_SKY` 90 px over their strip's top, so the moon is 44 px over the horizon |
| The moon | a layer of its own after the stars, drawn only for the storey the camera is at (its rest is the anchor + TH), crossfading over `BG_MOONW` 48 px, so stacked windows do not each show a moon |
| Past the strip | its top and bottom rows are carried on (the ground below; Nun's architrave and the interiors' ropes above) |
| The shake | moves the view with the keep; a zoom does not slide it |

The 51 px: until 47b a window's view stood 0.8-0.85 of a storey (51.2-54.4 px) above an arch's or a court's on the same storey (its anchor a
storey up). The arches and courts now take the window's anchor, so their views moved up to the window's; the horizon's constant puts the eye
line at the floor's eye at rest, so the anchor's storey shows in nothing but the parallax's reference. `viewscan.py` holds it: every layer at
the same screen y in every opening of a storey (within 0.5 px; 0.00 in 2,520 views on floors 3-48 x 2 seeds; v88 54.4 px). A layer's x is the
same in every opening by construction (no term of the opening).

A pure function of the camera and the opening: a rewind, respawn or camera snap cannot make it jump. No seams: outlines repeat in whole
periods over the strip (`BGK`; random outlines closed by `bgClosed`), and copies meet on whole target pixels (`sn`).

Distance blur (`GLFX.bgblur`, both modes): each layer is softened once when painted, never per frame, by a small image pyramid
(`bgSoften`), radius `bgBlurR(f)` on the log of the depth (1.2 for f 0.2 and nearer, 4 for 0.005 and farther); stars stay sharp.

## Light, shadows and effects

| Pass / effect | This element |
|---|---|
| Light map (`gatherLights`) | every drawn window adds a `win` light in the sky beam's colour (84 px); a forge or crypt window its own glow (`lk.win`, 92 px, bloom) and a `sky` light spilling under it. With the background on, every opening in view adds a `sky` light at its middle in the sky's horizon colour; Torchlight adds a bounce where the beam from its middle lands on its floor, dropped when `solidFront` stands between |
| Key light (`keyLight` sx) | `win` lights count toward a figure's lit side; `sky` lights do not |
| Lamp shadow mask | the backdrop is not a surface: Torchlight's shadows are scaled by the scene's alpha, which is 0 on the sky |
| Relief | openings sink in the height map (the room pictures hold only the keep); the view itself is not in it |
| Metal | none |
| Sky beams | each opening is a beam source: see below. The pass itself: [rendering](../rendering.md) |
| Mist / wall looks | the Blue Chambers' glaze is times the scene's alpha, never on the sky (round 27d) |
| Floor looks | none |
| Heat, the dark | none |
| Bloom, grade, vignette | graded with the scene; the beam's floor patch and the opening's rim bloom (Torchlight) |
| Classic equivalents | the same backdrop; `fillCut` cuts the figures' fill lights wherever the room pictures are clear (except over a figure's silhouette); `ryBeams2D` draws the beams |

How the sky is kept apart in Torchlight: the void and `drawBackdrop` are drawn through `G2D` with `na` set, which leaves the scene's alpha at
0; the room pictures and everything in `drawDynamic` are drawn with `na` 0, so alpha is how much of a pixel is not the backdrop. The
composite and bright pass take the figures' fill light off where it is under 1 (round 25c). A new full-screen draw after `drawDynamic`
stays under `na`; the `?wgl=0` path still lights the sky.

### What each opening sends

One light for the whole floor, from its time of day (`skyBeam`, `SKYB`): every window, run of open arches and breach casts it in its own
shape (`ryShape`), swept along the light; bars, grilles and the star's points leave streaks (`ryProfile`, made once per floor).

| Time (`L.prog.tod`) | Direction (from straight down) | Strength | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| dusk (0) | -48 degrees: the low sun on the left | 0.7 | warm `255,172,112` |
| night (1) | 21 degrees: the moon on the right, where the backdrop draws it | 0.6 | cool `160,186,255` |
| dawn (2) | 48 degrees: the low sun on the right | 0.78 | `255,206,152` |

| Source (`ryOps`) | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A window | its style's opening; bars shade lines of the beam | not one in an open arch or a breach (the breach casts its own) |
| A forge or crypt window | `own`: lit from inside (furnace, tomb lamps) | a glow at the pane in `lk.win` at `RY_OWN` 0.85, no shaft |
| A run of open arches (up to 6) | one shape; the piers leave gaps between beams | only with the background on |
| An open breach | its hole's outline | only with the background on |

The light travels toward the viewer as it falls (`q`) and lands on the floor's top face in the opening's shape; the wall it comes through
gets none; Garama's foggaras take it from nearly overhead (`LFX` `beamA` -9). Torchlight reads up to 16 sources a frame (`GLCMAX`).
Wall faces, low ceilings, blades, slabs and sand cut it (see [stonework](stonework.md), [rendering](../rendering.md)).

No staircases (round 46a, owner: "The jagged light beams. I think we fixed this at least half a dozen times. Let's try to specify it
better and to document and enforce it."): Torchlight draws the beams in a pass at a quarter of the canvas, so every edge they have is at
least two of its texels wide (rules S1-S7 in [rendering](../rendering.md#low-resolution-passes-s-m-no-staircases)). Each shape's profile is
prefiltered once a floor (`ryPrefilter`: the lines beside the shape take the nearest line's entry and exit, then how open each line is and
its entry are blurred across the lines by `RY_BLUR` 1.4 px; the exit is not, so the lit rim stays sharp) and read `LINEAR`; Classic draws
its sprites from the same profile. A floor's shadow, a low ceiling's and a wall's in the haze are ramped over the march's step and two
texels. `beamjag.py` measures every theme at every hour and the forced cases, both modes, each `GLP.rs` and Battery saver.

## Rules for changing it

- Look only: arches, open arches and breaches are hashes; only the windows' placement reads `rng`, and its draw is made for every cell.
- Never key the backdrop to the player, a row he is in, or anything the view culls: it is a pure function of the camera and the opening.
- A new layer takes `f = h/(s H)` from what it shows and is kept in order far to near; its outline repeats in whole periods (`BGK`,
  `bgClosed`).
- Nothing the figures carry lands on the sky: a new full-screen draw after `drawDynamic` stays under `G2D.na`; a new dst-alpha blend on the
  main scene must not read that alpha.
- A new opening must stay off lift shafts and register in `openingsIn` (view, anchor), `ryOps` (beam) and the `sky` light.
- Post and lintel, but the dungeon style's round vault.
- No edge in the beams narrower than two texels of the quarter-size pass (S1-S7); a new opening's shape goes through `ryProfile` and `ryPrefilter`.
- Every opening of a storey shares the window's anchor (`bgFoot`) and the horizon (`bgEyeY`); a new opening registers its foot accordingly.
- Check with `egyptr25e.py` (slide under 1 px a frame), `viewscan.py`, `openscan.py`, `beamshot.py`, `beamjag.py`.
