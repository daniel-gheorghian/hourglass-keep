# Gates

A gate is a slab (or, with `EGP.traps` off, a barred grille) that stands across the corridor in the middle of its cell and lets
him through only while it is at least half up. A plate opens it (see [plates](plates.md)); it either stays open (latched) or runs on
a timer shown by a sky clock. Gates are laid from floor 3 on: in-line gates in runs and corridor fills, gate puzzles (side
passages, alcoves, slab puzzles, double gates), remote gates in the next turn, and the violet gate of a fork with a grating.
Lift mouths are drawn with the same code and covered here as far as they are gates; the lifts themselves are in [lifts](lifts.md).

## Example
Floor 5, seed 4405 (`__HK.genLevel(5,4405)`, The Halls of the West, granite): on row 12 a run heading left has a plate at column 41,
plain floor 40-38, a closer at 37 and the gate at 36, with wall over the gate's cell (its lintel). The gate is timed: `dur` 1.9 s,
`(m*TW+16+BW)/RUN + 0.2` for the closer (m = 4 tiles: 1.7 s) times a slack drawn between 1.15 and 1.03, rounded up to 0.1 s. He
runs over the plate, jumps the closer from the run (the bot's `dashjump`) and is past the bars as the sky clock in the roundel over
the gate empties. Stepping on the closer slams the gate in 0.25 s.

## In play
| Rule | Number / example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A gate is a barrier only while `open < 0.5` | `clampMove` stops a body's front (`BW` 6 px) at the cell's middle | a thin plane across the corridor, not a wall cell: walls, kicks (`wallTouch`) and grabs ignore it |
| Opening | `open += 3/s` while `tm > 0` (full in 0.33 s) | |
| Closing | slammed by a closer (`slam`) 4/s; a timed gate (`dur > 0`) 2.6/s once `tm` runs out, so it blocks again 0.19 s later | a closer is beaten only by jumping it; a closer can also shut a latched gate, and pressing the latched plate again reopens it |
| Latched | `tm = Infinity` from the first press; sound `door`, message `gateMsg` | most in-line gates and most side-passage gates |
| Timed | `tm = dur`, reset on every frame he stands on the plate, counts down once he leaves it | race: the clock starts at the plate's edge |
| Held | `dur` 0.2 (`blockGate`: slab gate P2, block gate): shut 0.2 s + 0.19 s after the plate is released | only a slab or a block can hold it |
| In-line timer | `(m*TW+16+BW)/RUN` + 0.2 closer / -0.1 pit / +0.05 plain, times `lerp(1.15,1.03,...)` | the fastest honest run plus a thin margin; `gateopt.py` measures it |
| Puzzle timers (P1 detour, double gate) | `estPath(backs+exit) * timerK() + 0.3`, plain table (no `careful`) | `timerK` = 0.84 + 0.14 rng + 0.1 (1 - d): 0.84 tight .. 1.08 |
| P4 timer (race gate in the next turn) | `estPath(..., true)` (the careful table) `* timerK() + 0.3` | the bot's own measured pace; `p4bot.py` checks every one is beatable |
| Fewer timed side gates | after the build, 62% of timed `far` gates (not paired, slab or `fix`) become latched, by `hash2` | layout unchanged |
| Hints | within 110 px of a shut `far` gate: `latch`, `sandgate`, `racegate`, `slabgate`, `double`, `loopgate` | |
| Under a lintel | the wall over the gate's cell is a ceiling at his height: a jump whose full arc meets it is the low leap (`beamOverhead`) | the open-hall pass keeps the lintel over gates and blades |
| HUD | the nearest running timed gate (finite `tm`, `dur > 0`, not held by a block in the last 0.2 s) shows a sky clock and its seconds at the top centre (details in [plates](plates.md)) | |
| Rewind | `applySnap` restores `open`, `tm`, `slam` (feature fields) | |
| Respawn at a checkpoint | latched plates pressed before it (`S.cpPlates` = `pressedPlates()`) reopen their gates (`tm` Infinity, `open` 1); timed ones start shut | `respawnplates.py` |
| Continue (`S.resume`) | latched-open gates (`lg`) reopened by `applyResume` | |

Sounds: `gate` (five ticks) when a plate starts a shut gate, when a closer slams (with `shake(0.9)`) and when a closing gate in view
passes half shut; `door` (a long rumble) when a latched plate first latches.

## The prince and the guards
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Running into a shut gate | `runMove` is clamped: `bump`, he stops standing |
| Jumping into one | `stAir` is clamped and `vx` set to 0: he drops straight down beside it |
| Standing in the gate's cell as it shuts | nothing: no damage, no push (no `hurt`/`die` cause for gates exists) |
| `hazardEdge` (edge-walk before a jump) | stops looking at a shut gate |
| Guards | the same `clampMove`: a shut gate stops a guard's advance, retreat and knock-back. Guards never press plates. The budget posts a guard just past a solved gate or puzzle (`gateDue`) |
| Bot | walks through open gates; on a timed one races (`dashjump` for `sjump` while any non-held gate's clock runs) |

## How it is drawn
Live, every frame, in `drawDynamic`, both modes alike (Torchlight draws the same calls through `G2D`). The room picture draws the
gate cell's floor and the lintel's wall as usual.

```
the plane (plane(X,Y,ys)): from the floor's front edge (L0 = X+16-HX) to the back wall (R0 = X+16+HX), through the tile centre
       R0 (back) ____
            /    /|        egSlab: narrow front face at L0 (lit from the left), broad face = the right side face,
           /    / |        sheared along the depth (in shade from the left), top hidden in the ceiling
   L0 ____/    /  |
  (front)|    |   /        near half: x < X+16  (drawn after the figures: crosses in front of him)
         |____|  /         far half:  x >= X+16 (drawn with the other features, before the figures)
```

Draw order in `drawDynamic`: features (gate far half: `slots`, slab or bars, `ceilShade`, then roundel and plaque) ... flasks, sand,
debris, snakes, guards, the prince ... then the near halves of gates and jaws, then lifts' near parts.

| Variant | Look |
|---|---|
| `EGP.traps` on (default): the portcullis (`egSlab`) | a stone slab `T_SLAB` 7 px thick in `SLAB_STONE[look]`: granite (west, blue, osiris, nun), quartzite (hawara), syenite (aksum), sandstone (napata, meroe, deffufa, foggara, wells), limestone (shali); `stoneTex`; crystal glints for crystalline stones (`STONES[kind].cry`). A dark slot across the floor and a groove up the back wall, the far lip lit; the slot's mouth at the ceiling's front edge; wear by `wearLevel` (`stoneWear`, chips capped 3.2/2.4 px) and rubble beside its foot (`wearRubble`, drop > 8 px). Raised, only the floor's slot and the back wall's groove show |
| Key light (`keyLight`) | `uL = sstep((0.35 - sx)/0.7)` cross-fades the faces (round 20b): light from the left lights the narrow face and its outer edge and shades the broad face; from the right the reverse. Always a crisp bright arris between the faces, a core-shadow band (4 px) on the broad face beside it, a dark far edge and contact line (round 16c). Its cast shadow falls across the floor along its foot (only where that side is floor) and down the back wall, length growing with how far it is down, strength `lw = 0.45 + 0.55 k` |
| Top piece (round 14, 26a) | where the ceiling's block ends at the tile's right edge, the slab runs up along its own slant in front of that side face, under a dark slot, shaded at `rgba(6,4,10,.9)` as the ceiling's shade starts |
| `EGP.traps` off: the iron gate | 5 bars (`GATE_G.NB`) with lit left and dark right edges and pointed feet, cross bars every 11 px along the slant in `metalPal('gate')` (the style's metal); the first cross bar in the pair's colour on a paired gate |
| Timed gate | `gateRoundel`: a roundel cut in the wall over it (12 px up in dungeon and forge styles, else 16) holding the sky clock (`drawGlass` -> `nutClock`), its recess glowing warm and pulsing while the clock runs. Not on slab gates |
| Paired gate | `pairPlaque`: an enamelled lozenge in `PAIRC[pair]` (amber, teal, violet) over the gate, above the roundel when both |
| Lift mouth (`liftMouth`, `sandMouth`) | `drawGate` with `{latch, dur 0, lg:1}`: with `EGP.traps` the same `egSlab` in the theme's own stone (`THEME_STONE`), standing wholly outside the shaft; bars with it off |

What cuts it: its own clips only (`egSlab`'s rectangle from the ceiling's edge to below the floor, and the half's rectangle). Nothing
else is cut by it.

| Pass / effect | This element |
|---|---|
| Light map (`gatherLights`) | a timed gate whose clock runs adds a small warm `item` light (r 34, `255,200,120`, 0.55) at its roundel; otherwise none |
| Key light (`keyLight` sx) | models the slab (faces, arris, cast shadow), both modes |
| Lamp shadow mask (`glShadow`, `glMask`) | **not carved.** `glMask` carves walls, floor lips and tops, low ceilings and (round 37b) the slicer's jaws (`JAWP`), not gates or lift mouth slabs: a figure's lamp shadow that reaches a down slab is laid on its face as if it were the back wall (the jaws' fault before round 37b). The "solid blocks and slabs" its comment names are wall cells (movable blocks included) and floor slabs |
| Relief (`glRelSlab`) | with `EGP.traps`, every gate (and lift mouth, and sand lift foot mouth) that is not fully up is rubbed back over its whole box (`glRelQuad` to 0.4, near full strength to the edges); the iron gate only while moving, by an ellipse (`glRelEx`) |
| Metal mask / shader | portcullis: none ("the granite portcullis is not metal"); iron gate: `msGate`, both halves, kind `msKind('gate')` (`metaltest.py`'s scene) |

| Mist / wall looks | none of its own |
| Floor looks (`floorFx`) | the slab and slot are drawn over the floor's face (alpha 255): no embers or sheen on them; the floor beside keeps them |
| Heat, the dark | none |
| Bloom, grade, vignette | none of its own |
| Classic | same drawing; beams cut as above; no relief or shadow mask |

## Rules for changing it
- Look only: the barrier is `open < 0.5` at the cell's middle; nothing in drawing may change `open`, timers or hit tests.
- A new kind of gate needs: its hint, `gateMsg` text if it has a plate, the HUD rule (`dur > 0`, finite `tm`), a bot sequence, and
  `puzzlescan.py`/`orphanscan.py` coverage if it is a puzzle.
- Timers come from `estPath` times `timerK()` (or the in-line run formula); a timed gate in a turn with a chain lift is never chosen
  (`CYC`: its wait cannot be sized).
- A slab must slide up into stone (round 25d): keep the lintel; `slabscan.py`.
- A new slab-like thing drawn live in front of the back wall needs its relief rubbing (as `glRelSlab`), its `beamBlockers` quad and,
  if figures' shadows must not land on it, a carve in `glMask`.
- No metal in the Egyptian looks except where a style table says so (`METAL_EG`); the portcullis stays out of the metal mask.

## History
- round 4: the themes' re-skinned traps built in: the granite portcullis (`egSlab`) replaces the iron gate (`EGP.traps`).
- round 12: the portcullis's perspective: narrow front face, sheared broad side face, slot and groove, no log and ropes.
- round 14: the top corner where the ceiling block ends; key-light modelling and cast shadow; lift mouths become stone slabs outside the shaft.
- round 16c: the edge reads in every theme (core-shadow band, far edge, contact line); `glRelSlab` whenever a slab is down; shadow only on floor.
- round 20a: a down portcullis blocks sky beams (`beamBlockers`).
- round 20b: `keyLight`'s balance `uL` cross-fades the faces.
- round 25d: the lintel kept over lift mouths too; `slabscan.py`.
- round 26a: the top piece in the ceiling's shade.
- Details in CLAUDE.md (Themes, 16c, 20a, 20b, 25d, 26a, Forks, Trap density).
