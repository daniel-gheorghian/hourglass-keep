# Pendulum

A crescent blade on a long arm swings across the corridor at chest height; he crawls under it or times his run. Only on floors built in
the tile style (the old Lapis theme, `L.gstyle` 'tile': floors 9-12, 29-32, 49-52 and so on, drawn as the Blue Chambers or the Well
Corridors), in runs, on landings, in side corridors and in side galleries.

## Example
Floor 9 seed 2 (the Blue Chambers): a pendulum at cell 32,15, period 2.17 s, ten columns past the slicer of [slicer jaws](slicer-jaws.md)'s
example. Walking up he hears it pass the bottom (`swoosh`) every 1.08 s; standing within 11 px of the blade while it is low costs a life
and knocks him back 12 px. Crouched, he crawls under it untouched, as the bot does (`crawl` along the stretch).

## The prince and the guards
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Crouched or crawling, or poking with the sword low | never hit |
| Standing, running, in the air near the bottom of its swing | one life |
| Hanging or climbing on its row | not hit |
| Drawn with him | before the figures: he is always in front of the blade and arm |
| The bot | `crawl` from the stretch's first tile to its last (the pendulum on the middle one); it also handles a snake if one lurks (the action is shared with tunnels) |
| Guards | never placed on its tile (`guardOk`); not tested by it |

## How it is drawn
Live only (`drawDynamic`, in the `L.dyn` loop, before the figures), from `pendGeom` each frame:

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| pivot cap | a dark 8 x 4 px block with a gold (`#d8b04a`) cap at the ceiling's underside |
| ghosts | two faint copies of the blade 0.035 and 0.07 s behind (motion), in `pal[3]` |
| arm | a 2.6 px bar in `metalPal('pend')[1]` inside a 4.4 px dark outline (so it stays apart from the tile style's gold colonnettes), bevelled: a lighter core and a thin lit edge on its left |
| lashing | four turns of rope round the arm 4 to 10 px below the pivot (`ropeOf()`'s colour over a dark line; round 18b: were chain links) |
| blade | `pendBlade`: a crescent 30 px wide, rotated by `-th`; the cutting (lower) edge dark, the upper edge lit; a dark outline; a gold hub |

`metalPal('pend')`: steel in the tile style (the only style that builds it), tinted cool; `EGP.traps` does not change it (`METAL_EG` holds
only the jaws). No wear, no cut by walls (it swings inside its hall).

## Rules for changing it
- The play test reads `pendTip`, the drawing and the mask read `pendGeom` and `pendBlade`: the same numbers; change them together.
- No chains: a hanging part is rope (`barRope`, `ropeOf`).
- Its place follows `L.gstyle` (the generator never reads the theme).
- Look changes are look only: arm, swing, period and hit window are play.

## History
- The Lapis theme (before the Egyptian themes): "a blade swings at chest height; crawl under it or time it".
- Unit MS: the arm, blade and gold fittings in the metal mask.
- Round 18b (owner: "replace the chains with rope"): the arm lashed to its pivot with four turns of rope.
- Round 19 (owner): lamps keep clear of it (`LAMP_NEXT`).
- Round 38a: the blade and arm carved out of the lamp shadow (`SHC`).
