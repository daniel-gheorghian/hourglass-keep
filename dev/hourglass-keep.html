How to play

Run
Hold left or right on the pad.
Tiptoe
Hold Grab and tap a direction. Stops at edges and walks over spikes safely.
Jump
Jump from a standstill clears one tile. From a run it clears two, and on a three-tile gap you catch the far ledge.
Climb
Up under an opening to pull yourself onto the ledge. Down at an edge to hang, down again to let go.
Catch
Hold Grab while falling to catch a ledge. Two-floor drops hurt unless you hang first, and a hard landing leaves you unsteady for a moment. Hold Grab as you fall past a long tapestry to slide down it.
Walls
Run at a wall two floors high and press Jump to run up it and catch the top. In the air against a wall, Jump kicks off it: bounce between close walls to climb a shaft.
Swing
Jump to catch a bar over a wide pit, then Jump again as you swing forward to fly across.
Blocks
Hold Grab and press toward a loose block to push it, away to pull it. A block holds a plate down for good, and you can climb over it.
Crawl
Down gets you on hands and knees, left or right crawls, up stands. Low tunnels and swinging blades call for it.
Fight
Grab strikes. Up blocks when the guard raises his blade, then strike back fast; better guards parry a flurry and answer it. Down sheathes. Strike snakes before they coil, or crouch and Grab to draw your blade low and thrust at them in a tunnel. Skeletons only fall to a pit, so drive them over the edge.
Gates
A plate raises a gate. An hourglass on a gate means it shuts again, with the time left shown at the top. A red plate right in front of a gate slams it shut, so jump over it. Some gates open from a plate out of sight, up a climb or down a hole; colour-marked gates answer to the plate of the same colour.
Traps
Jump saw blades as they come. Crouch under darts. When sand stirs behind you, run and do not stop until the arch.
Sands
When you fall, Grab turns back time about five seconds. You carry three turns of the glass; every hourglass refills them.
Flasks
Stand on one and tap Grab to drink. A small red flask heals one life, a large red one heals them all, gold adds a life, dark blue is poison. Large red flasks are rare and wait at the far ends of gated side passages; gold ones hide in passages without a gate.
Exit
A floor plate somewhere opens the exit. Press up at the open door.
Keys
Arrows move, Space jumps, Shift grabs, P pauses.
