# Lifts

Three ways to ride between storeys inside one column of a chamber: the counterweight lift (a platform on ropes, floor 3 on), the
paternoster (an endless rope loop carrying slabs, floor 8 on) and the sand lift (a one-use slab on a column of draining sand, floor 4 on,
built in place of a banner slide). All live only in turn chambers; the `tower` flag fills a floor with them.

## Example

Floor 8, seed 1 (`genLevel(8,1)`): a `lift2d` heading right in a chamber, segment `turn-lift2d` from column 228 row 9 to column 231
row 11. `liftSeg(2,true)` (c 228, r 9) empties column 229 for rows 9 and 10, lays floor 229..231 on row 11 (the foot and the bottom
landing), keeps the walls at 228 (row 10) and 230 (rows 9, 10), and writes:

```
 row 9     [lplate e0] |MS| [top mouth]  [wall 230]        platform rests here (f.y = f.yT); L.base[top mouth] = FLOOR while it does
 row 10    [wall 228]       [shaft]      [wall 230]
 row 11    [wall 228]       [foot]  |MS| [lplate e1] [floor 231]
             228             229           230         231
```

He stands on the plate at 228: the platform is already at the top (it rests at the end he arrives by). He steps onto it, holds Down:
it sinks at 64 px/s, the top mouth slab drops shut behind him, the platform docks two seconds later and the bottom slab rises; he is handed
back to `stand` with Down still held doing nothing (`P.liftHold`) until he lets go. The bot's action is `{a:'lift',from:228,to:231,k:2,up:false}`.
The far side of the foot (column 228, row 11, `sc - sB`) is wall, so the foot cell draws as a 5 px pit, not a tile (`liftPit`). Floor 37
seed 60685 and floor 44 seed 53683 hold the two lifts (of 716 on floors 1-48 x 3 seeds) whose far side opens on a drop; they keep their
tile. Other kinds: floor 8 seed 1 also has a `lift3u` (column 79, rows 6-9); floor 8 seed 2 a sand lift (`turn-sand3`, column 189, rows
9-12, foot mouths `f.ms` [1,-1]: a corridor runs on past its far side); floor 8 seed 23 a paternoster (`turn-chn2u`, columns 301-302, rows
11-13, 3 slabs).

| Rule | Example or number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Counterweight: on the platform, Up or Down held rides it at `LIFT_V`; let go, it stops where it is | a storey takes 1 s | he controls the ride; he is on it within 17 px of the shaft's middle and 2.5 px of `f.y` (`liftUnder`) |
| A call plate on each landing (`lplate`, `f.e` 0 top, 1 bottom) sends the platform to its landing | not while he rides it or it is already there | so the platform can be fetched from either side; `hint('lift')` |
| Each mouth is shut while the platform is away from it (`f.gT`, `f.gB`: 1 open, eased at 3/s) | `clampMove` stops him (and guards) at the plane `liftGx` | nobody walks into an empty shaft |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Falling onto a counterweight platform stopped mid-shaft | `liftAir` lands him on it (`land`): the fall counts from `P.fallY` as any fall (100 px hurts, 170 kills) |
| Falling onto, or into the path of, a paternoster slab | a slab meeting his feet within `CH_FOOT` 14 px carries him (`chainBoard`, the same fall damage); jumping up into one's underside (`CH_HEAD` 18) stops his rise with a `bump` |
| Standing on a paternoster's foot | a slab rising from under him (or catching his ankles) lifts him on; one coming down on him shoves him a step aside toward an open landing (`chainGround`, state `step`), never hurts |
| Kicking or planting on a shaft wall | never: `wallTouch` returns 0 inside any lift's shaft (`inLiftShaft`); he slides down as down a smooth wall (owner, v59; `liftkick.py`) |
| Riding | state `lift`, `P.lift`; `P.slab` on a paternoster; learns `lift` or `chain` (`setState`) |
| Guards | never posted in or beside a shaft (`guardOk`: columns `ca-1..cb+1`, rows `rT..rB`); they never ride; a shut mouth stops them as it stops him (`clampMove`) |
| Hints | `lift` (a plate pressed, or on a lift's row within 110 px), `chain`, `sandlift` (as it starts) |
| The bot | `lift`: stands on the call plate until the platform is there and its mouth is over 0.9 open, walks on, holds Up/Down to the far end, walks off; paternoster: presses toward the shaft only on a frame a slab passes, holds on toward the far landing; `sandlift`: walks onto the slab and waits |

## How it is drawn

Live in `drawDynamic`, the same calls in both modes: `drawLift(ctx,f,0)` before the features of `L.dyn`, `drawLift(ctx,f,1)` after
the actors (a right mouth slab's near half). The room picture draws the top mouth cell as the open shaft (`blockUnder` = `EMPTY`) and,
since round 37a, no floor tile in a pit lift's foot cell but `liftPitDraw`: a pit `LP_D` 5 px deep (the platform's thickness), the
shaft's back going on down into it, the floor's end beside it as its left face with a lit top arris, a dark stone floor whose front edge
sits in the lips' shade. `liftPit` is true for every sand lift and for a counterweight lift whose foot's far side (`sc - sB`) is wall in
`L.base0`; the paternoster's foot keeps its two tiles (he walks and lands on it). At rest at the foot the platform's top and lip are
exactly a floor tile's (`SURF(rB)`, a 5 px lip).

The paternoster (`drawLiftChain`): back plane two tiles wide; a dark slot along the foot; the rope loop (its lay travelling with it) and
its two upright runs (`liftRope` with `ph`, so the entry run rises and the exit run falls), wooden wheels with peg teeth top and bottom,
all clipped above the slot's lower lip; then the slabs (`liftSlab`), each with a hole, its rope and a seized stopper while it runs
upright, and a dark underside; the slabs are cut by `liftNearCut(...,foot)` so the foot floor's lip stands in front of a slab on its
bottom run. No mouths, no rig over the shaft.

The sand lift (`drawSandLift`): the shaft's back seen only through the hole, the outlet on the pit's floor; left foot mouths whole;
the sand (`sandCut`, the wells' `sandTex`) from the slab's front edge down into the pit, a puff (`dustCloud`) as it gives, grains running
down the sides (`sandFlow`); the slab (`sandSlab`: a floor tile of the theme's stone, lip over the whole cell from X-HX, no side face),
cut by the right column's wall faces and floor tops; right foot mouths' far half, near half after the actors. No rope (`liftRope` none).

## Rules for changing it

- Look only vs play: the foot pit, the mouth slabs' places outside the shaft, the rig, ropes and wear are looks; `liftGx`, `f.gT/gB`,
  `L.base`, `LIFT_V` and the boarding windows are play. The foot cell stays `FLOOR` in play; a pit is drawn only where nobody can stand on
  the foot while it shows (a lift whose far side opens on a drop keeps its tile).
- A lift is never a trap: a slab coming down shoves, a platform catches; only an empty shaft is a fall.
- No wall plant or kick in any cell a lift travels through (`inLiftShaft`); `liftkick.py` must print `ALL OK`.
- Everything in a shaft is at or behind his depth: anything new drawn there goes inside `liftOccl` (and `liftNearCut` for the column on its
  right), and back-wall parts inside `liftOcclSide`. `liftscan.py` must print `ALL OK`.
- A new moving part in front of the back wall needs its box in `glRelLifts` (the relief embosses the wall's art on it otherwise).
- A new rope uses `liftRope`/`barRope` in the theme's fibre (no chains, round 18b); no metal unless `EGP.traps` is off.
- No arch, breach or window in a shaft or beside it; such looks are hashes, no generator stream.
- A sand lift must stand in a shaft walled on every storey above its foot, its foot storey shut by mouth slabs (`sandshaft.py`: `sandshaft: ok`);
  the choice `sandOr` is a hash, so building one or a banner moves nothing else on the floor.
- A new lift kind is one line in `MV`, one in `PRIM`, one in `LAND`, its bot action in `bot.js`, and its time in `estPath`.
