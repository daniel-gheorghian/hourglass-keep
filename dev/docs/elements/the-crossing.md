# The Crossing (the interlude)

At each change of theme (every fourth floor from 5) he crosses the open land of the theme he is going to, side-on, under that floor's
hour, and walks into the doorway of the next part of the keep. Wordless, skippable, moved only while the pad is held. Twelve themes in five
settings, both modes. Look only: built from the floor's seed by hashes, no generator stream, nothing in the save touched. The research and
its sources: [INTERLUDE_RESEARCH.md](../../INTERLUDE_RESEARCH.md).

## Example

Floor 8 (the Halls of the West) ends: he climbs the exit's stairs into the light, the card comes, the player taps Climb on.
`nextFloor()` starts floor 9 as always (`startLevel(9, -1, true)`: the save, the rewind ring, the hours, the floor clock), and since
`crossDue(9)` (floor 9 is the Blue Chambers) `crossBegin()` puts `mode='cross'` on top. The screen fades from the exit's white over 0.6 s onto low dunes at Saqqara
at dusk, the sun low on the left. Held Right he walks at 40 px/s; after 1.6 s held he runs. At 45% of the way a gust lifts sand off the
crests and sends a sheet of it along the path. At the doorway of a ruined temple wall he turns into its depth and darkens, a thud, black,
and floor 9's own entry plays (the leaf drops behind him).

The beats: the dunes' gust (`crossPlumes` flare, a sheet of sand along the path, the wind swells); the oasis's Egyptian geese run over the
water and climb away toward him, white forewings showing (`crossGoose`: a solid bird, broad wings by `GW_*`); the heron crouches and lifts
off with slow wingbeats (`crossHeron`); the grain's travelling wave (`crossWave`, `CROSS_WAVE`: a front along world x from the camera's
left edge at the beat, so no state); the reeds' wave and the lake's cat's paw (`paw`); ducks bursting from the papyrus; in the wadi
0.15 s after the beat `RF_BIG` 6 big stones and 10 gravel break off the gully's head and bound down the fan with dust and a plume
(`RF_PL` puffs), two ravens lift off a boulder; on the highland a flock bursts out of the grain above him and wheels away.

## How it is drawn

The view (round 27b's depth rule): the path is the play plane (f 1), his feet at `CROSS_GY` 134, the horizon (eye level) at `CROSS_HZ` 96;
ground at depth factor f lies at `y = HZ + 38 f` (`crossGYf`) and a layer moves f of the camera's travel (`crossPX`: a pinhole round the
view's middle), f = h / (s H) from the drawing's own size (s 29.4 px a metre).

```
z  0     sky (f 0)                       todSky, sun/moon, stars
z  1     ground                          haze to near sand, ripple rows
z 10-49  land behind him: crossZ(f) = 10 + 39 min(f,1)   bgart, erg, pool, stand, reeds, cliff, terr ...
z 49.x   dyke / tfront (the bank in front of his path)
z 50     the doorway (gate)
   ---   he (drawn when the first layer with z >= 60 comes)
z 60.x   dfield in front of him (f 1.3, 1.7; never above his feet's line)
z 70     foreground (fore, ofore, gfore; f 1.9-2.45, faster than him, never over him)
         the gust's sheet of sand, then the fades (white in, black out)
```

- Bake once, draw every frame: `CROSS_KINDS[kind].bake(ly, cr)` into a canvas (uploaded once by G2D in Torchlight); `crossBake` bakes
  one layer a frame (the sky, the ground and `fore` do not count) while the fade from white (`CROSS_FIN` 0.6 s) hides them.
  `draw(ctx, ly, cr)` places the baked strip in view units (`crossStrip`).
- Stands (`stand`): every palm, acacia, olive, hut or wall is drawn once into one atlas per layer (`crossCrown`, `crossTrunk`,
  `crossThing`), at most 4000 px a side (the scale shrinks to fit).
- Water (`pool`): an outline in world x and depth placed by `crossPX` (the far shore moves slower than the near), the hour's sky mirrored
  at its height with a middle stop, the sun's or moon's glow and glitter, every `refl` layer mirrored about its own ground line, darker
  toward the near shore, damp ground round it; reflections draw only inside the water's box.
- Shear: in the wadi `cr.tilt` 0.045 tilts the ground along the way: every layer on it is sheared about the view's middle (full on the
  ground, fading with depth), his feet ride it (`crossFY`), the sky and the doorway stay level; layers reaching the screen's foot go
  `crossTiltX` further down so no corner shows the page.
- Sunk fields: a flat layer below the path's level is scaled about the horizon (`sk` metres), so the field is seen from above.
- The doorway (`gate`, baked): framed as the next floor's start door (posts, lintel or the dungeon style's round vault, `doorArch`), in
  the setting's own build: `ruin` (coursed sandstone, top broken in steps, a cavetto over the door, a drift, a fallen block, column drums),
  `pylon` (battered mudbrick towers with torus and cornice, the enclosure wall; whitewashed in West), `rock` (cut in the cliff, a rebate,
  a cleared step, scree on the end face), `granite` (rubble courses between timber beams with monkey-head ends, the doorway in dressed
  granite on a granite step), `mesa` (the `plain` fallback).
- He is drawn by `crossFigure` with `frameLights = CR.lights`; at the doorway cut by `doorHead` and tinted by `drawPTint`.
- Classic: the same layers and figure, a soft glow at the sun (not the moon) and the vignette. Torchlight: see below.
- No HUD: the HUD canvas is cleared and carries only the fades in Torchlight.

## Rules for changing it

- Look only, from hashes of the floor's seed: no generator stream, layouts unchanged; never touch `levelTime`, `S`, the save or the
  rewind ring.
- Animals only, no people (empty huts, an idle shaduf); nothing Christian at Aksum; Egypt's own gardens, never the Babylonian "hanging
  gardens"; nothing in the wadi may read as a pyramid, tower or door but the real doorway (the spur is an irregular outcrop, scree comes out
  of a gully's mouth with concave flanks, never a peaked cone).
- The eye is 1.3 m up: keep the near side of ground-level water and fields clear (or sink the field), or they are hidden.
- The light's side must match the hour (dusk left, dawn and moon right) in every drawing.
- For a phone: bake everything once (rims too; no per-frame allocation), one transform a layer for the shear, reflections only inside the
  water's box, under 20 layers a crossing and atlases under 4000 px (round 36d's measure; the atlas cap is in the stand's bake), every layer
  baked within the fade from white.
- A new setting is an entry in `CROSS_LOOK` and `CROSS_SETS`, its kinds in `CROSS_KINDS`, its sounds in `crossSound`, and its kinds in
  `crossscan.py`'s `SETX` (and `SETL` for per-theme features).
- `crossscan.py` must print `crossscan: ok`: every theme's first crossing in floors 2-180 starts only where the theme changes; 3 s without
  input leave him in place (and the cue shows); Jump before 0.5 s does nothing; walk, run, the beat and the doorway; the entry plays; save,
  `S`, `levelTime` and rewind unchanged; a skip ends within `CROSS_SKIPF` and a frame; never off, with `?cross=0`, on Continue, Restart,
  respawn or under the bot; every layer baked within the fade (the longest bake frame printed); no void in the wadi's bottom corners at any
  hour; no page errors; render time median under `HK_CROSS_MS` (8 ms Classic, 40 Torchlight in software WebGL) and within `HK_CROSS_K` 2.5
  times a play frame. `quick`: Classic, one theme per setting and the never cases (its header says about 2 min).
