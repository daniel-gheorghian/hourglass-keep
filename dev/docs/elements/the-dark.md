# The dark (the chase)

A storm of darkness that wakes behind him on one long run of a floor and drives him to an arch at its far end; it kills on contact. From
floor 6, on about two floors in three. Opaque black, a rip in the picture with a torn, gilded edge; Anubis's jackal head slides out of it
when it wakes and whenever it is close. It replaced the sandstorm and the granite plug in round 29 (the death cause is still named
`'sand'`: "Taken by the dark").

## Example

The bot on a chase floor: the dark wakes as he crosses column `c0+1` heading right, its front set 4 tiles behind him. It holds for 0.7 s,
then comes on at `v` (55-92 px/s, against his run's 100), surging when he gets more than `190 - 30d` px ahead. The jackal's head slides
out at his height for the first three seconds, lamps ahead of it gutter, and his shadow on the back wall leans back toward it. The run is
26-34 columns (plus up to 8 with depth) of obstacles that never ask him to wait. At the arch the dark rushes him once, tears apart round
him in black tatters with gold edges, and settles as ash.

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Behind the kill line on a storm row | dies at once, whatever his state |
| On a row outside `rTop..rBot` | untouched (the rows are the segment's span) |
| Through the arch | safe; the dark breaks |

| His shadow | pulled back toward it (`drawShut`) from 240 px, whole at 80 px |

## How it is drawn

One table a frame, `dkTable` (made at most 30 times a second, its clock in 1/30 s steps; the top and foot from `x0` in 256 px steps; the
front kept as offsets from `fx`, so the front's motion does not remake it): `F` the front's offset per world px of height, `G` its gold,
`J` the torn back edge while it opens, `Tt`/`Tb` the ragged top and foot per px of width; packed into `u8` (1024 x 4, two bytes a value)
for the shader. Both modes draw from it.

```
          ~~~ ragged top (Tt) ~~~       head and tongues lean far ahead only above the top row
   ######\                            <- tatters only from head height up (d 44-62) and above the top row
   ########\   jackal (at his height)
   ########|  <- from feet to waist: within a few px of the kill line wx+4, a gold crest on the floor at the line
===========+============ floor (row rTop..rBot)
   ######/
   ~~~ ragged foot (Tb) ~~~
   <- black behind the front                 -> veil (greys, darkens) and lens ahead
```

- The tear: `dkTear`, piecewise-linear value noise at three scales sliding along the edge (straight runs and sharp corners, never a
  billow), and `dkSplint`, a splinter now and then; the lean (in `dkTable`, after `stBulge`): a small sway from the feet to the waist, then
  straight runs at head height and a big forward lean only above the top row, where nobody stands.
- Opening: `dkOpen` widens the rip from a 3 px crack to the whole corridor over 0.6 s after it wakes.
- Gold (`DK_GOLD` 226,180,92): along the tear in three strengths, along the top inside it, and a crest along each floor at the kill line,
  trailing back into the dark; a soft dark falloff just outside the tear (both modes).
- Tatters (`dkTatters`, `DK_TN` 32 slots, each its own 2.1-3.0 s cycle): ragged ribbons torn from the edge, fluttering (a wave running out
  along them, a curl), each drawing back or (55%) tearing loose and flying 26-70 px on ahead, shrinking. Opaque black; only from head height
  up of each storm row and above the top row.
- The jackal (`dkArt`, drawn once at `DK_Q` 4 px a world px; `dkJackalPath`): black, gold inside the near ear, a calcite eye ringed in gold
  with a kohl line, a brow, a gold edge along the brow and muzzle, a gold collar (Tutankhamun's Anubis, Cairo JE 61444). `dkLook`: it comes
  out at the wake (0.3-3.2 s) and whenever the front is within 52-90 px of him, at his height (its jaw just over his head, its neck back in
  the dark), a pure function of the chase, his height and the clock. Otherwise up to two pairs of eyes open, blink and close in the dark
  (most of a 3.7 s and a 4.6 s cycle).
- Ahead of it (`drawStormHD`, in `drawEmissive`, Graphics' atmosphere on): 48 flakes torn off the edge blowing ahead (black, one in four a
  gold fleck), 6 shards tumbling out; in Classic also a 190 px darkening of the air.
- His shadow (`drawShut`, in `drawDynamic` before him): his black silhouette (`figDraw` mono) in three leaning, stretching copies along the
  back wall toward the dark and a smear on the floor behind him, from the front to just ahead of him, never on the sky through an opening
  (`openingsIn`). Round 39 (the layering rule, [layers](../layers.md)): it lies only where the back wall shows, clipped cell by cell (one
  nonzero path of rectangles): each open cell of his row and the open cells over it, from its back wall's left edge (the cell's + `HX`)
  to the next cell's face; the strip `HX` either side of the next cell's edge where that cell is open and no slab's end or lip stands
  there; under a wall from its foot, under a floor slab from its lip's foot, under a low ceiling from its face's. The smear lies only on
  the floors' top faces, cut at a wall's face. Before, it was cut at his own column's ceiling only, so the copies lay over the wall he
  hung or climbed behind in a chase and over the faces and slabs beside him (2,089 px in the audit's frame, 0 now).
- Dawn: holes burn through it with gold edges (Classic: ragged holes on a 16 px grid as `fade` rises; Torchlight: noise against a rising
  threshold), black tatters with gold edges rush past and part round him (`drawStormBurst` / the shader's burst), a warm light from the
  arch (`drawStormDiss`), and flakes (`STORMG`) pour past, are thrown round him (70 px), whirl, drop and lie on the floor as ash.
- The arch (`sanctum`): a pale gold arch outline drawn live on its cell, pulsing while the dark runs, dim once it is done.

Order: Classic draws the dark (`drawDark2D` -> `stormLayer2D`) last over the world, after the light, emissive and haze passes, so it stays
black; Torchlight draws it in `glStorm` after the composite (`compose`, then `storm`) at full resolution over the dark's rectangle of the
screen only, with analytic coverage across the edge (no MSAA), the jackal and eyes from the same art, and the composite's vignette. With
`GLFX.storm2` off Torchlight falls back to the 2D layer on its scene. Nothing runs without a chase, and never in a crossing.

## Rules for changing it

- Look only: the front's speed, the kill line (`P.x < wx + 4`), the rows and the bot are play; the tear, gold, tatters, jackal, eyes, veil,
  lens and the shadow must never move them.
- Fair: from the feet to the waist of anyone on a storm row the drawn edge stays at the kill line. `stormfair.py` (`stormProbe(torch, look)`:
  the 2D layer or the shader drawn alone) needs the alpha to reach .55 within 6 px of the line and nothing over .25 past `wx + 10`, on every
  row without the jackal and eyes (`DKT.noLook`) and with them on his own row. Tatters and the big lean stay at head height and above.
- Opaque black, never a haze (owner: "100% black opaque, like rips in the scene"): a translucent black reads grey.
- Both modes draw from `dkTable` and `dkTatters`; change one picture and the other must follow (the shader's `u_ta` holds 32 tatters).
- Everything is a pure function of the chase, his height and the clock (rewind-safe); the only state is `STORMG`'s flakes at dawn.
- No per-frame hashing: `dkL` reads the streams made once (`DKR`); keep new noise there.
- The rip and what goes with it are L4 and never cut; his pulled shadow is a thing on the back wall and stays behind the stone in front
  (`drawShut`'s clip).

## History

- until v75: the sandstorm (v60's boiling dust, `STB`, and its bakes), with a granite plug in the Egyptian looks from round 4.
- round 29: the dark: opaque rip, gold, the jackal and eyes, `dkDrain`, the veil, `drawShut`, dawn at the arch, `howl`; then the edges:
  falloff, tatters, Torchlight's lens. Details in CLAUDE.md.
- round 32/35a: the running jump runs on after landing; the chase's `dash` holds the way; with round 35a's faster running jump the chase is
  0.1-0.5 s quicker for the bot, the dark's speed unchanged.
- round 39 (v84): `drawShut` only where the back wall shows, its smear only on the floors' top faces. Look only.
