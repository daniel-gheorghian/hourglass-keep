# Darts

A shooter in the riser of a one-storey step fires a dart at chest height down the five tiles of run before it, on a fixed
period: he crouches and crawls under them, or times his run, then climbs the step. Floor 6 on, any style and theme, built by the
run's menu on the run's lowest row.

## At a glance
| | |
|---|---|
| Code | feature `darter` (`f.x`, `f.y`, `f.per`, `f.off`, `f.x0`, `f.dir`, `f.lastPh`); darts in flight `L.ext.darts`; `cyc`, `spoutMouth`, `SPOUT_L`; `drawShooter`, `drawDart`, `dartStreak` (`DARTSTREAK`), `puff`; spent darts `SPENT`, `spendDart`, `updateSpent`, `clearSpent`; hit `trapHit('dart',dd)` |
| Layer | the shooter's head fixed on the riser's side face, jutting into L2m (a shooter firing left is hidden by the face); darts in flight L2m, cut on the mouth's side only; spent darts L2f; placement keeps other faces out of the lane. See [layers](../layers.md) |
| Generated by | `dartSeg` in the run menu (`buildLevel`, main `rng`): weight 0.12, `n>=6`, only on the run's lowest row (`r===rMax`); not a featured obstacle family (`OBS_FAM`), not scaled by `TK` (barracks), not in the chase, forks, landings or side passages |
| Bot | `darts` (`from`, `to`): crouch, crawl the lane, wait for a dart to pass, stand, climb the step |
| Checks | `run2.py` (0 damage); one-off: `dartstress.py`, `traptest.py`; `layerscan.py` (round 39, by hand: kind `darter`, the shooter; darts in flight are not covered) |
| Pictures | `egyptshot.py` (TRAPS `darter`), `trapshots.py` (scene `darts`), `vs_traps.py` |

## Example

Floor 7, a run heading right ends at a step up. Five plain tiles lie before the step's riser; the riser holds a shooter at
`SURF(r)-34`. Every 1.8 to 2.2 s (`per`, fixed for the shooter) a dart leaves the riser, flies left at 190 px/s and runs
out over the first tile of the run (`f.x0 = (c+1)*TW+27`), where it tips, drops and fades. Standing in the lane he is hit (one life, pushed back 12 px); crouched or
crawling he is not. The run heads right, so the shooter sits on the wall's hidden left face: no head is drawn, and the darts come out
from behind the step's front face with only a tick and a hiss to tell of them.

## In play

| Rule | Example or number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | `dartSeg`: `k=5` floor tiles, the riser at `c+k+1` (row `r`), the step's top at row `r-1`; the run goes on one storey up | a run toward a step: the climb at the end holds him in the lane |
| Period | `per = 1.9 - 0.5 d + U(0,0.4)` s, `d = min(1,(n-1)/24)`: 1.8-2.2 s on floor 6, 1.4-1.8 s from floor 25 | harder deeper down |
| Fire | when `cyc(f,gameTime)` wraps (`ph < f.lastPh`); random phase `f.off` | steady rhythm, read by ear |
| Tell | the last 0.35 s (`ws = 1-0.35/per`): sound `tick` (round 50c: a stone click, was a square blip); the visible head glows and shivers | warning without moving the shot |
| Flight | `d.x += dir*190*dt` at `y = SURF(r)-34`, from `f.x` to `f.x0` (131 px, 0.69 s) | |
| Hit | same row, `P.inv<=0`, `abs(P.x-d.x)<7`, `P.y-50 < d.y < P.y-6`, state not in `crouch, poke, hang, climb, climbDown, jumpUp, exit` | crouching and crawling (both `crouch`) pass under; a jump does not clear it |
| Damage | `trapHit`: 1 life (`hurt(1,'dart')`), `P.inv=0.9` s, push 12 px along the dart, `landHurt` from standing states; the dart is removed | death card "Struck by a dart" |
| While invulnerable | the hit test is skipped and the dart flies on | |
| End of run | at `f.x0`, a dart whose tile is in view becomes a spent dart (`spendDart`); otherwise it is just removed | it has no wall to hit |
| Hint | none since round 49a (no hint banners; until v89: `darts` within 200 px on his row: "Darts fly from that wall at chest height. Crouch and crawl under them, or time your run.") | |
| Sound | `dart` and a dust `puff` at the mouth, both only when the shooter's tile is in view | |
| Rewind | `L.ext` (darts in flight) and the feature's `lastPh` are in every snapshot (`snapAll`); `SPENT` is not saved and is emptied on a floor or clock reset (`clearSpent`) | spent darts are look only |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Standing, walking, running in the lane | hit at chest height |
| Crouching or crawling | safe (`crouch` is in the exempt list; crawling is the `crouch` state moving at `CRAWL`) |
| Jumping (`air`) | not exempt: the band `P.y-50..P.y-6` still contains the dart at the jump's height |
| Jumping up, hanging, climbing the step (`jumpUp`, `hang`, `climb`) | safe: exempt states |
| Guards | darts test only the prince; a guard in the lane is never hit. No guard placement rule refers to darts |
| Bot | `darts`: walks to a step short of the lane, crouches, crawls to `to`, waits crouched until no dart is within 75 px ahead and the shooter's `lastPh < 0.45` (a dart has just flown), stands and climbs the step (`jumpUp`/`hang`/`climb`) |

## How it is drawn

All live in `drawDynamic`, both modes alike; nothing of it is in the room picture except the step's wall.

```
drawDynamic: lamps, doors, clocks, lifts(back) -> L.dyn features (drawShooter here) -> blocks
             -> darts in flight -> spent darts -> flasks, wells, dust, debris -> snakes, guards, prince -> near halves
```

So a dart passing him is drawn behind his figure.

| Part | Look |
|---|---|
| Shooter, firing right (`f.dir 1`, runs heading left, built mirrored) | a carved beast's head in profile jutting `SPOUT_L` 12.5 px from the wall's right side face at the darts' height, a spout like a gargoyle's: the theme's carved-fitting stone `FIT_STONE[style]` (shared with the sky clock's pedestal) in a vertical gradient, a sliver of its top going back into the wall, its own drop shadow on the wall (0.34 black, offset), mane and locks, lit brow and snout, open jaws with fangs |
| Ornament by `style` | dungeon an iron band and rivets; palace a gilt collar and carnelian inlay; tile a lapis glaze band, gold star and turquoise edge; forge a glowing seam and rivets (and the eye always faintly lit); crypt a turquoise glaze band (the first three in `HG_MAT[style]` colours) |
| Wear | `wearLevel` at the head: 1, 2 or 4 chips off brow, snout or jaw; a crack from wear 2 (hash `mulberry32(hash2(...))`, no stream) |
| Tell | the eye and jaws glow amber (`lighter`), a small glow at the mouth; the head shivers `0.45*tell` px |
| Shooter, firing left (`f.dir -1`, runs heading right) | not drawn (`drawShooter` returns): it sits on the wall's left side face, which the wall's own front face hides from this front-right view |
| Dart, in its own frame (forward +x, `scale(dd,1)`) | a reed shaft in a dark outline, thread wound under a thistledown tuft, a dark iron head with a lit edge and a flickering glint; in flight a cached warm streak behind it (`dartStreak`, a 64x12 gradient canvas, drawn `lighter`) |
| Leaving the mouth | clipped to the part past `d.mx` (`spoutMouth`: the jaws' tip for a visible head; `xb-HX`, the front face's left edge, for a hidden one), so it comes out of the jaws or from behind the face. | Spent dart | a fixed pool of six (`SPENT`): tips up to 0.85 rad, drops at 55 px/s forward under 520 px/s^2 to `SURF(r)-1.4`, lies at 0.07 rad, fades from 1.2 s to 1.6 s |

## Rules for changing it

- Look only vs play: the tell (glow, shiver, tick) never moves the shot; the hit box, speed and period are play.
- Looks use hashes of the place (`hash2`), never a generator stream.
- A shooter firing left stays undrawn: the wall's front face hides its side face. Its darts must keep coming out from behind the front
  face (`spoutMouth`'s `xb-HX` and the clip in `drawDynamic`, cut on the mouth's side only).
- Spent darts are cosmetic: keep them out of the snapshots and the save.
- A new variant that changes the action needs its bot action (`darts` in `bot.js`) and `run2.py` at 0 damage.
- If darts are made metal, they need a `drawMetalMask` case and a `METAL` role.
