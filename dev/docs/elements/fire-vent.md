# Fire vent

A grate in the floor that flares on a rhythm: a sheet of fire over the whole grate for the last quarter of its cycle. Only on floors
built in the forge style (`L.gstyle` 'forge': floors 13-16, 33-36, 53-56 and so on, drawn as the Forges of Meroë or the Pyramids of
Napata), in runs, on landings, in side corridors and side passages, often two to a stretch.

## Example
Floor 13 seed 1, the Forges of Meroë: a vent at cell 206,6 with a period of 2.15 s. From 0.12 to 0.6 of the cycle (1 s) the grate is dark; then it
warms and smokes, at 0.70 a row of low pilot tips shows, at 0.75 it whoomps (a flash, sparks, the jets catching from the middle out) and
burns him for 0.54 s if he is within 13 px of its middle; it dies down red to 1.0 and a small red tail is left to 0.03. The bot waits
at the start of the stretch until a run would never be within 21 px of the vent while it is lit (0.70 to 0.03).

## The prince and the guards
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Runs through between bursts | safe |
| Caught standing, crouched or jumping over it while it burns | one life |
| Hanging or climbing on its row | not hit |
| Drawn with him | the grate before him; the flame after everything (`drawEmissive`), so it burns in front of him |
| Its light | casts his and the guards' lamp shadows in Torchlight (`fire` is in `GLSTRONG`) and pulls the key light he is lit by |
| Guards | never placed on its tile (`guardOk`); not tested by it |

## How it is drawn
Room picture: a plain floor slab (marked 254).

Live, in two parts:

```
drawDynamic   L.dyn loop: drawVentBase (grate) + fpMark ... figures ... near halves
render        light pass (Classic: lightPass, bloomPass; Torchlight: grade only), atmoPass
              drawEmissive: drawVentFire (flame), the dark, FX particles      <- not dimmed by Classic's light pass
```
(With `GFX().light` off, `drawEmissive` runs at the end of `drawDynamic` instead.)

The grate (`drawVentBase`), set into the middle of the floor's top face in its perspective (`onFloor`, depth 0.08 to 0.92) so it reads as
a hole in the walkway: a dark frame, a glow down the shaft that warms from 0.6 of the cycle (orange, up to 0.88 alpha) and cools over 0
to 0.12, five bars running front to back, a lit front lip, and three grey smoke puffs rising while it warms.

```
the grate seen from above (x -11..11 px about the tile's middle, depth .08 front .. .92 back)
   back   +----------------------------+
          |   j0    j1    j2    j3     |   back row, depth .7: x -6 -2 2 6 (drawn first, 0.86 high, 0.75 alpha)
          |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |    |   five bars front to back, x -8 .. 8
          |   j4    j5    j6    j7     |   front row, depth .3, drawn after the merged sheet
   front  +============================+   lit lip
```
The flame (`drawVentFlame`, only in the drawn window): a warm glow sprite (`lighter`, 0.4 x strength) and an ignition flash; eight jets
(`VJETS` `[x, depth, height]`: x -6, -2, 2, 6 px in a back row at depth 0.7, drawn first, shorter and fainter, and a front row at 0.3),
each from the same 16-frame flame sheet on its own frame, neighbours flickering in opposite phase, the cooled red row cross-faded in as
it dies; at the whoomp the jets catch from the middle outward. Above about 45% of their height (`m = sst(.45,.85,H)`) they merge into one
sheet drawn from the whole grate (two broad jets between the rows), so what burns is as wide as what hurts. Then licks while the flame stands over
0.7 of its height, ignition sparks and embers. `S.set.calm`: a third of the flicker, half the frame rate, no flash or sparks. The profiler's
`PF.off.flame` skips it. Same sprites in Classic and Torchlight; no per-style or per-theme variant, no `EGP` variant.

## Rules for changing it
- Look only: `ventState` and the flame never touch the cycle (`cyc`), the hit test (0.75, 13 px) or the bot's window (0.70 / 0.03).
- The flame covers the whole grate, as wide as what hurts (owner, v56).
- A flat thing drawn live on the floor calls `fpMark` and is listed in `FT_FLAT` (round 35b), so the footprint lands on it and the floor
  looks keep off it.
- Flames come from the prebuilt flipbook: no per-frame allocation, no new canvas.
- Its place follows `L.gstyle` (the generator never reads the theme).

## History
- The Forge theme (before the Egyptian themes): "floor vents flare on a rhythm".
- Torchlight's composite: heat shimmer over vents and forge torches (effect mask g).
- v56 (owner): fire over the whole grate, a jet per slot in a back and a front row merging into one sheet (`VJETS`); timing and hit test
  unchanged.
- Round 31: the floor's looks moved into the composite and kept off things drawn over the floor.
- Round 35b: the grate marked as floor for the footprint (`fpMark`), `FT_FLAT` keeps the floor looks off it.
- Round 39 (v84): FX particles (its sparks among them) are not drawn on a face (`onFace` in `drawEmissive`). Left as it is: the back row's
  flames are drawn after the figures with the front row (emissive, after the light pass).
- Details in CLAUDE.md.
