# Flasks and secrets

Flasks stand on the floor; he drinks one with Grab. Red ones heal, the large red one heals fully, the gold one adds a life for good, the
dark blue one hurts. The gold flask sits at the far end of a secret passage (a side passage with no gate) on every third floor, with a
twinkling cue where the way to it starts; alcoves and gated side passages hold red flasks; fork kind 4 sometimes keeps one under a grating.

## Example

Floor 9 (a gold flask floor: `n % 3 === 0`). Between 25% and 55% of the route (`secretAt`), `secretPassage` cuts a gallery two storeys above
the corridor: he climbs from the corridor to a ledge and on into it, walks out over a mini level of obstacles to the gold flask at the
far end, and comes back. On the ledge's lip over the corridor, five small gold four-point stars rise and fade (`drawSecretCue`); within
120 px of it the hint says "Something glints up there. Side passages without gates hide gold flasks." From floor 5, half the floors (a hash
of the seed, `wantAmb`) with guard budget left put an ambush in the passage: when he steps on the flask's tile a guard comes 1.1 s later,
and when the passage drew a chain (60%) five cracked tiles behind him start to give way.

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sword drawn (`garde`) | Grab does not drink: only standing (`stStand`, Grab with no direction) or crouching (`stCrouch`) reaches `tryDrink` |
| Hurt or bumped while drinking | the drink ends, the effect applies if the flask was in hand, the flask falls |
| Guards | never drink or take flasks; the ambush guard is part of the floor's budget (`guardBudget`) |
| Snakes | a `tunnel` piece in a passage may hold one (`snakeOk`) |
| The bot | drinks the gold flask in the secret passage (`drink` presses Grab standing); passes all others |
| Rewind | the snapshot keeps `P.drinkIt` by index; taken flasks are restored with the items |
| Respawn | the floor is rebuilt: red flasks come back, the gold flask does not (`S.lifeTaken`) |

## How it is drawn

Drawn live in `drawDynamic`, never in the room picture, the same in both modes:

```
hidden flask's stars (3 slow four-point stars round it, while hidden and not taken)
drawSecretCue (the lip's stars)
drawJetHints
drawFlask for every item not taken      (base at SURF(r)-3)
drawLooseFlasks (L.flk: the empty one falling, fading)
... the prince (drawHeldFlask while drinking: in his front hand, tilted by drinkFrame)
```

- `flaskBody` (round 9, owner: "if an Egyptian would have made the original potions"): v62's round bulb, neck and sizes (`flaskR`: big
  5.4, life 4.2, others 3.2), clear glass with a pale turquoise tint, the liquid glowing through, a bubble rising; touches from
  Tutankhamun's grave goods: `heal` a gold rim and a blue faience knob; `big` a lotus-bud stopper and a gold cartouche tag on a cord; `life`
  gold leaf over the shoulder and a gold papyrus-umbel stopper; `poison` obsidian-dark glass and a black stone stopper. No painted pattern.
- The liquid stays level in the world as the flask tips (`fill`, `tilt`); it drains as he drinks.
- The drink's poses are motion capture (`MCK.drink`, CMU 79_38 and 81_14; skel's mouth term `mh` puts the flask at his mouth): see
  [FIGURE_SPEC](../../FIGURE_SPEC.md) and [prince](prince.md).
- The cue (round 17, `secretCues`, found once a floor from the tiles and kept in `L._scue`): for an ascent (a gallery's ledge, or an
  alcove holding the gold flask) on the ledge's open side over the corridor, the stars lift 2-5 px off the lip; for a descent on the floor
  edge beside the hole he hangs from, they slip over the edge and down into it. `SCUE_N` 5 stars, each on its own `SCUE_T` 2.6 s cycle
  from `hash2`, along the lip's top face in depth: a glow (`lightSprite`, 'lighter') and a gold star with a white-hot core. No
  allocation a frame; gone once the flask is taken.
- Under a grating, a red glint shows between the bars while the flask below is there; crouching on the grating turns the camera down the
  shaft to the flask and its gate (`gratepeek.py`).

## Rules for changing it

- The cue is look only: nothing is placed for it, it is found from the tiles; it must stay a pure function of the clock and hashes (no
  allocation a frame) and die with the flask.
- A new flask type needs a colour in `flaskBody`, a light in `gatherLights`, a size in `flaskR`, an effect in `drinkFx`, a rank in the
  thinning (`rank`) and the Grab label stays Drink.
- The gold flask always survives thinning; a grating's flask always stays (its gate guards it).
- Placing a flask on a run uses the main stream; keep the large flask's draw on its hash so layouts do not move.
- A flask's light and Classic beam cut (`ryStand`) follow `L.items`; a new thing standing on the floor that should not take beam light
  needs the same.
- The secret's `trigger` must keep the chain's and ambush's delay above the drink's `DR_T`.
