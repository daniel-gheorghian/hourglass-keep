# Cracked slabs and sand wells

A cracked slab (`loose`) is a floor tile that gives way 0.5 s after he stands on it, grabs it or bumps it from below, and falls. Play
is the same everywhere; the look depends on what is under it (`looseWell`): over a pit walled in on both sides down to its floor it
rests on a well of sand that drains through an outlet and drags it down; over anything open (a corridor, a landing) it crumbles in
plain air with a billow of dust. Slabs appear from floor 3 (the `loose` obstacle), in side passages, as the collapse move, as the
crumbling chain of a passage, and in the slab puzzles; the rotten flag makes them the floor's featured obstacle.

## Example
Floor 9, seed 802 (`__HK.load(9,802)`, the Blue Chambers): a gallery side passage's crumbling chain, five `sturdy` slabs at columns
139-143 on row 16, over the corridor on row 18. Until round 30 the three inner slabs each counted a neighbour as a wall and stood on a
column of sand right through his corridor. `looseWell` now tests the run as a whole: each slab's pit must be walled on every row down
to its floor by stone or by a neighbour's pit that is itself a well; the ends fail (open to the corridor), so their neighbours lose
their support, and so on until all five are corridor slabs: grit and dust, no sand.

## The prince and the guards
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Running across | the slabs give way behind him; he is past each before its 0.5 s |
| Stopping on one | he falls 0.5 s later, with it |
| Below one when it falls | 1 life (`crush`) if it lands on him |
| Grabbing or bonking one | it starts to give way; he drops with it from a hang |
| Guards | never trigger slabs and are never hit by them: guards keep to their posts' plain floor (`lo`..`hi`), the debris test reads only `P` |
| Bot | `runover`, `collapse` (waits up to 20 s for the ledge to give), and jumps the gap on the way back in a passage |

## How it is drawn
Live in `drawDynamic`, both modes; the room picture leaves the cell out while `s < 2` (and `aoFloor`, Classic's embers skip it);
after the fall the cell is a hole and the rooms are redrawn (`invalidateCell`). Without `EGP.traps` there is no sand: the shaking
slab and the plain falling slab (`slab()`) of the v61 look, a few dust particles where it lands.

```
draw order in drawDynamic (cracked-slab parts marked *)
  features ........ * the slab (s < 2): dynSlab + cracks, sunk, shaking, tipping, cracking, sand in its seams, pour/grit
  blocks, darts, cues, flasks
  sand ............ * drawWellGroup, once per group (its first slab), clipped to the pit's cells under the slabs' lips
  DUSTC ........... * landing clouds, cut at their floor's front edge and the faces round them (airCut, round 39)
  debris .......... * fallen slabs: halves / tip-and-turn / crack (well), four pieces + billow (corridor), planks;
                       the corridor's and the planks cut by debrisCut (round 39)
  snakes, guards, the prince, near halves of gates and jaws, lifts' near parts, sandDraw
```

| State | Corridor look | Well look |
|---|---|---|
| Whole (`s` 0) | sunk 0.8 px, a shade off its neighbours, dark gaps at both side seams, a chip off the top-right corner, cracks across the top and two hairlines down the lip from where they meet its front edge (round 35c: the lip's lone '>' read as wall art); with `EGP.traps` pale sand in its seams | the same slab; the pit below it reads full of sand (the cut face, from just under the lip to the pit's floor) |
| Giving way (`s` 1) | random jitter (0.4 px, 0.8 without `EGP.traps`), sinking by `sandK` (0.06 to 1 over 0.25 s) x 1.6 px; grit sifting from three cracks, short | sinking x 0.6 px; grains sliding to its seams (`sandFunnel`) and sand running from them into the crater for the first half second; the first slab of a row sags and folds in two (`slabHalves`, up to 0.14 rad) once the crater under it is 6 px deep; a slab whose neighbour opened first leans into that hole (`wellTip`, to `WELL_TIP0` while still floor, on to `WELL_TIP1` as it goes), sand running off its open edge |
| Falling (debris) | four pieces parting (`slabChunks`, `CHUNK_V`), a billow tumbling out of the hole (`dustCloud` mode 0, 0.45 s), a ground-hugging cloud where it lands (`DUSTC`, mode 1, 0.7 s, size 1); the pieces and the billow cut by `debrisCut`, the cloud by `airCut` (round 39, below) | the halves fold into the emptied shaft, or a neighbour falls turning as one piece from its tip; clipped to the shaft (behind the walls' front faces); landing cloud size 0.55 |

What falls through a corridor's hole goes down behind what stands nearer beside it (round 39, the layering rule R1,
[layers](../layers.md)): `debrisCut` cuts the slab's pieces and its billow, a falling plank, and with `EGP.traps` off the plain slab and
rock, by the next column's faces and floor slabs on every row it passes (`nearCut` with `fl`: that neighbour's lip and the near part of
its top face stand in front of the hole's right edge) and by the column before's faces and its floor's lip on the hole's own row (that
slab's top face is behind the hole). Until v83 they fell unclipped: 740 of 1,838 corridor slabs and 181 of 697 planks in the audit's census had
a wall in the next column below, and their pieces lay 16 to 34 px over its face for about 0.3 s (a plank 0.6 s over the next cell's lip and
the pit wall's face; 7,070 and 1,031 px in the audit's frames, 0 now). The landing cloud (`DUSTC`) is drawn inside `airCut`: a box from
34 px over the landing to its floor's front edge, then the faces in its cells. A well's slab keeps its own clip to the shaft.

The well (`drawWellGroup`, round 21b): the outlets (dark arched holes in the back wall's foot under each open slab), the surface's top
seen along the depth diagonal, slopes rising to the right lit (`slopeL`) and falling ones shaded (`slopeD`) as from lamps on the left
(fixed, not `keyLight`), the slabs' shadow on sand within 14 px of them (`sandShadeTop`), grains running to each throat while it
drains, the cut face (`sandTex`: one 64 px tile per look of grains, packing and broken laminae, never courses or cell seams; darkened
down the shaft by the cached ramp `sandShade`), a flowing core over each open outlet, a lit edge where top and face meet. Colours from
`SAND_LOOK[look]` (`sandC`).

`wellSolve` (look only, a pure function of the slabs' `s` and `tm`, so rewind- and respawn-safe):

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| Body | the slabs side by side whose `looseWell` holds over one pit are one group (`wellGroup`, kept as `f.wg`); depth per column from `f.wl` (storeys to its floor, 1-3) |
| Outlet | opens when its slab is triggered (`s` 1, `tm` > 0; a chain slab counting up from below zero is not open); throat sinks at `WELL_VT` (1.6 `WELL_V`) to `WELL_CD` 36 px |
| Crater | walls never steeper than the angle of repose `WELL_SL` 0.67 (34 degrees) from the throat |
| Heaps | sand below the angle of repose from every outlet stays (the stagnant zones) |
| Level | the rest sinks level so that the sand left is the sand there was less what ran out: each open outlet drains at one steady rate (Beverloo; a storey in about 0.43 s at `WELL_V` 150 px/s); bisection over 2 px samples (`WELL_DX`) |

Rows of three or more (`g.org`, round 26d; rows of one and two keep 21b's look): the level sags toward each open outlet (`g.sa`) under
a low swell (`g.nz`); craters, level and heaps joined by soft max/min (`wSmax`, `wSmin`); each crater a funnel (`WELL_FL`), its outlet
off centre (`g.ox`, up to 6 px), its own wall slope (`g.sl`) slumping flatter in four avalanches (`g.sc`, only ever flatter); low
rounded heaps (`g.rs`); each slab's lean, onset and tip (`wellTip1` 0.14-0.30), its crack across its own flaw (`wellCrk`, 85% of
slabs: `slabCrack`, two uneven pieces), the fall's lag and turn, the first slab's unequal halves, the sand pouring through its seams
before it goes (`wellPour`), tongues sliding down the crater walls (`wellSlides`) and the dust from each hole: all seeded hashes of
the slab's column and row (`wellH`), no stream.

`looseWell` (round 30): the whole run of adjacent slabs on the row is tested at once; a slab is a well when its pit (k storeys, k < 4)
is walled on both sides on every row down to its floor by WALL or by a neighbour's pit that is a well and reaches that row; a failing
slab takes its neighbours' support, repeated until none fails. Planks are never wells. Kept on each slab (`f.wl`).

## Rules for changing it
- Look only: every break time (0.5 s, 0.42 s for planks), `DROP_G`, the crush test and the bot are fixed; `wellscan.py`'s timing check
  holds the break times.
- No generator stream for any of the look: the well/corridor choice is from the tiles (`looseWell`), the organic terms from `wellH`.
- The well's surface must stay a function of the slabs' `s` and `tm` (rewind, respawn); never keep per-frame state in it.
- Sand never stands in a corridor: a new way of laying slabs must keep `wellcorr.py` at 0.
- Dry sand never steeper than `WELL_SL`; the sand never grows back (`wellscan.py`: slope, volume).
- A new live tile over the room's picture needs its rubbing (`glRelFloors`), `fpMark` if flat, `FT_FLAT`, and `tilescan.py`.
- Anything new that falls through a hole is drawn inside `debrisCut` (or its own clip to the shaft), its dust inside `airCut`.
