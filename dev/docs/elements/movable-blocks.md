# Movable blocks

A loose block of wall stone, one cell (32 x 64 px) and a storey high, that he pushes or pulls a tile at a time with Grab held. Its one
use is to hold down a floor plate whose gate shuts far too fast to outrun; he then climbs over it and walks through. Floor 7 on, any
style and theme, from the run menu.

## At a glance
| | |
|---|---|
| Code | `L.ext.blocks` entries `{c, r, lo, hi, under, mv, under2}`; `rebuildBlocks` (fills `L.blockAt`, `L.blockUnder`), `blockAtCell`, `blockCanMove`, `tryMoveBlock`, `updateBlocks`; states `push`, `pull`; `drawBlock`; `terrainAt`, `wallBelow` |
| Generated by | `blockSeg` in the run menu (`buildLevel`, main `rng`): weight 0.065, `n>=7`; `ch(0.45)` picks the pull variant. After the layout `buildLevel` stores the floor under each block (`under`) and sets its cell to WALL in `L.base` |
| Bot | `push` (`from`, `n`) or `walk` + `pull` (`from`, `n`), then `climb`, `drop`, `walk` |
| Checks | `grablabel.py` (in the suite: the Grab button reads Push / Pull / Grab as `blockCanMove` says), `run2.py` (the bot solves it), `tilescan.py` (relief on a pushed block's top); one-off: `rewblock.py` (rewind puts the block back), `blockcheck.py`, `traptest.py`, `hinttest.py` (hint `block`) |
| Pictures | `trapshots.py` (scenes `push`, `blockplate`), `egyptr35c.py`, `vs_traps.py` |

## Example

Floor 7, `blockSeg`, push variant: nine floor tiles; a block on tile 2, a plate on tile 4, a gate on tile 8 with `dur 0.2` (it starts
to fall 0.2 s after the plate is let go). He walks to the block, holds Grab and the pad toward it: the block slides one tile in 0.55 s
and he follows it. After two pushes it sits on the plate and the gate stays open (its range ends at tile 5). He climbs onto the block,
drops off its far side and walks through. In the pull variant the block starts on tile 5 and one pull brings it onto the plate.

## In play

| Rule | Example or number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A wall to play | its cell is WALL in `L.base`: solid to him and to guards, climbable as a one-storey step, hangable from its top edge | it is a block of the wall's stone |
| Range | `lo..hi` = tiles 2..5 of its stretch; a move past either end is refused | the puzzle cannot be lost by pushing it away |
| Start a move | standing, Grab held, pad toward (push) or away (pull), the block in the next cell ahead and his body within 26 px of its face | |
| Refused when | the target is out of range or WALL; the target holds a feature other than a plate; a guard stands within 26 px of the target's middle; for a pull, the cell behind him is WALL, has no floor, or holds spikes, a cracked slab, jaws, a saw or a low ceiling | no crushing, no pulling him into a trap |
| A refused pull | eats the press: he does not turn | the pad away is read as a pull |
| The move | `bl.mv {from,to,t,dur:0.55}`; the target cell becomes WALL at once, the old one goes back to its floor at the end; he slides one tile with it (`P.sx -> P.tx`, eased), sound `push`, hint `block` | both cells are solid while it moves |
| On a plate | a block at rest on a plate presses it every frame (`pressPlate`) and sets `f.g.heldAt`, so the gate never starts its fall and the HUD shows no timer for it | "its weight holds a plate down for good" |
| Gate | `dur 0.2`, tagged `blockGate` (tools such as `gateopt.py` and `pace.py` leave it out of timer statistics) | a gate that drops far too fast to outrun |
| Hint | `block` within 90 px on his row: "A loose block. Hold Grab and press toward it to push, away to pull. Its weight holds a plate down for good, and you can climb over it." | |
| Grab button | reads `Push` or `Pull` when a move would happen (`grabLabel` asks `blockCanMove`), else `Grab` | |
| Rewind | `L.ext` (blocks, `mv`) and `L.base` are in every snapshot; `applySnap` calls `rebuildBlocks` | |
| Respawn, Continue | the floor is rebuilt from the generator; `S.resume` records no block, so the block is back where it started | |

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Pushing | pose `push` (lean 0.5 forward, both arms forward) on the run cycle; P.x eased over the tile |
| Pulling | pose `pull` (lean 0.3 back, arms forward) on the reversed cycle; he steps back a tile |
| Jumping up to, hanging from, climbing onto its top | as a wall: `wallBelow` counts a block under the ledge, so on its near side he is hidden behind its face as behind a wall's (hands only) |
| Guards | collide with it as a wall (`clampMove` reads `B`); cannot move it; a guard in the target cell blocks the move |
| Bot | `push`: approaches from a tile further back (tiptoeing with Grab beside a block would push it), edges in with Grab and forward, counts `n` pushes; `pull`: walks beside it and pulls `n` times; then `climb`, `drop`, `walk` through the gate |

## How it is drawn

Live in `drawDynamic`, both modes, right after the `L.dyn` features (so a plate under it is drawn first) and before the darts,
debris and figures. Its cell in the room picture is drawn as what lies under it (`drawRoomStatic`'s `B` reads `L.blockUnder`: the floor,
or the plate's cell, which the picture leaves to the live plate); `tryMoveBlock` and `updateBlocks` invalidate both cells of a move.

```
     top (th.top, darkened)            drawn at x = lerp(from,to,ease(t/0.55)) * TW - HX:
    ________                           the front face is HX left of its cell, like a wall's
   /       /|  side (th.lo, dithered)
  +-------+ |
  | front | |  front: dark rim, th.wallB and th.wallA shaded, brick courses at 21 and 42 px with
  |       | |  joints, a lit top band (th.hi), a dark foot band, two cracks, a chipped bottom
  +-------+/   right corner, a dark hollow in the middle of the face
```

Colours are the theme's palette (`th.top`, `th.lo`, `th.hi`, `th.wallA`, `th.wallB`): "a block of the same stone as the walls". No
stone texture, no wear and no per-block variation; the same shapes in every style.

## Rules for changing it

- The block's cell is WALL in `L.base`; the floor under it lives in `under` / `L.blockUnder`. Anything that reads tiles for drawing
  the room must go through `L.blockUnder` (as `drawRoomStatic`'s `B` and `terrainAt` do), anything for play through `B`.
- A move must leave both cells solid until it ends, and must be refused onto any feature but a plate.
- A new block drawing must keep its relief rubbing in `glRelFloors` (front, top, side at the drawn place) and pass `tilescan.py`.
- A new use (another puzzle) needs its bot actions (`push`, `pull`, `climb`, `drop`) in `L.path` and the Grab label kept right
  (`grablabel.py`).
