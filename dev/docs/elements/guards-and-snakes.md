# Guards and snakes

The keep's fighters. Guards are tomb robbers in four ranks (recruit, soldier, veteran, captain) who fight the prince with the blade's
rules; on floors built in the crypt style some are skeletons that cannot be killed, only driven into a pit. Snakes lie in the crawl
tunnels from floor 5. Both have their own small budgets per floor (unit GB); they are not traps.

## Example

Floor 10 (a captain floor). The run's last stretch past 84% of the route holds the exit plate; `exitGuard` lays seven plain tiles and
posts the captain at the sixth (`CAPT`: hit points `ghp+2` = 5, the khopesh, a leather corselet). The budget kept two places for
him (`reserved()`: the captain and his reinforcement, from floor 6). When he falls, 0.9 s later a veteran with his hit points drops in
2 to 4 tiles ahead of the prince on clear floor (`dropSpot`; no hint or message since round 49a), so the way to the door goes through a second fight.

## In play

Budgets (`guardBudget`, `snakeBudget`; x is the floor's draw from `gbrng`, so each range is one or the other):

| Floor | Guards | Snakes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 0 | 0 |
| 3-5 | 2 | 0 (floor 5: 1) |
| 6-11 | 3-4 | 1-2 |
| 12-19 | 4-5 | 2-3 |
| 20 on | 5-6 | 3-4 |
| barracks flag (floor 4 on) | doubled, cap 10 | unchanged |

The budget counts every fighter on the floor: guards, skeletons, the reinforcements they call and the ambush (`gSpent`).

Where guards stand (`L.guards[].src`):

| Source | Where | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `exit` | between the exit plate and the door, last run, past 84% of the route | every floor with a budget | the captain on every fifth floor from 5 (`capFloor`); his reinforcement from floor 6 |
| `gate` | just past a solved gate or gate puzzle (`gateDue`) | budget left after `reserved()`, at least `guardGap` (`max(30, 0.7 x route / budget)` of route progress) since the last, after 12% of the route | crypt style: one in two is a skeleton |
| `fork` | on a landing between the moves of a kind 1 fork's short way (`forkChamber`, `forkGuard`) | floor 6 on | rank from `frng`; reserved in the budget |
| `garrison` | on a barracks floor every guard of the `gate` kind, and `gateDue` is also set after 45% of other traps (not moves or plain floor) | barracks floors | 40% call a reinforcement (floor 6 on, budget for two) |
| ambush | drops in at a secret passage's gold flask, on the way out | floor 5 on, one floor in two (`wantAmb`) | at least a soldier; armed by the flask's tile (`trigger`, `armPassage`) |
| reinforcement | drops in 2-4 tiles ahead of the prince, 0.9 s after its caller dies | captain (floor 6 on), garrison | the caller's hit points and rank (a captain's is a veteran); never behind him; gives up after 8 s without clear floor |

Every posted guard gets seven tiles of plain floor (`W(c+1..c+7,r)`, set to `FLOOR`), stands on the sixth and keeps to its range
(`lo` = tile 1 + 10 px, `hi` = tile 7 + 22 px). None in an ordinary chamber's landings, a side passage's corridor fill (`fillCorridor`
has no guard in its menu) or a side passage (the ambush drops in). A drop-in needs `guardOk` floor: solid, no wall, no block, a column
clear of any lift shaft, nothing on it but a plate, trigger or exit plate.

Ranks and hit points (`d = min(1,(n-1)/24)`):

| Rank | Dress, weapon | Parry | Riposte | Aggression | Wind-up s | HUD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| recruit (`TIERS[0]`) | ochre head cloth, club | 0.12 | 0.2 | 0.32 | 0.40 | name and hearts in rose `#e894a4`, the hearts' line `#f6c6cf` (round 38b; light blue `#8fa3d8` before) |
| soldier (1) | green head cloth, axe | 0.32 | 0.45 | 0.45 | 0.34 | `#e08a3c` |
| veteran (2) | crimson tunic, leather cap, axe | 0.52 | 0.8 | 0.6 | 0.31 | `#d9483b` |
| captain (`CAPT`) | leather corselet and cap, violet sash, khopesh | 0.66 | 0.85 | 0.68 | 0.30 | `#d8b04a` |
| skeleton | bones, a pitted blade | 0.42+0.12d | 0.4 | 0.6 | 0.33 | a skull |

Parry and aggression rise by `0.12 d` and `0.1 d` (cap 0.85), the wind-up falls by `0.03 d` (floor 0.24). `tierOf`: floors 1-2 all
recruits, then a recruit share `max(0.12, 0.62-0.8d)` and a veteran share `min(0.4, 0.04+0.55d)`. Hit points `ghp = min(5,
2+floor((n-1)/5))` (2 on floors 1-5, 5 from 16), the captain `ghp+2`. Each landed blow takes one.

The fight (both sides use `resolveStrike`; a blow lands at 6-40 px ahead with the heights within 8 px):

| Guard state | Rule |
|---|---|
| `idle` | prince on his level (within 8 px, not dead, exiting or entering) and within 100 px: `garde`, sound `draw`; otherwise now and then (a 1.7 s clock, 40% by a hash of his post) `gshift`, his weight shifting, heard off screen (round 50d) |
| `garde` | faces him; gives up beyond 140 px. Every think (`(0.2..0.55) x (1.25-0.45d) x tk` s): over 32 px advance, under 17 retreat, else strike with `aggr`, else 30% a random step |
| `advance`/`retreat` | 13 px over 0.24 s, never nearer the prince than 20 px, inside `lo..hi` |
| `strike` | the blow at the wind-up `wu` (`windup` x (1 +- `jit`)), back to `garde` 0.3 s later |
| `parry` | 0.36 s; a prince's blow inside its first 0.32 s is blocked (`clang`, the prince `blocked` 0.3 s); riposte with chance `ripo` 0.08 s later at 0.8 of the wind-up (0.2 s at least) |
| `blocked` | after his blow is parried: reels `reel` s (0.42-0.6) |
| `hurt` | 0.4 s, knocked 7 px inside its range; may still parry (`up`) |
| `dying` | `alive` false, `guardDie`; queues its reinforcement |
| `drop` | a reinforcement or ambush falling from 74 px above, lands in `garde` (`land`, dust) |
| `fall` | a skeleton driven off its floor: gone on landing (`crash` if in view) |

The prince's side: Grab strikes (the blow at 0.17 s of 0.4); the guard parries with `parry + cu x combo` when ready, `up + cu x
(combo-1)` when reeling (combo: blows landed within 1.4 s; cap 0.95), 0.02-0.1 s after the press. Up parries, also from `blocked`. A
guard's blow costs him one life (`P.inv` 0.3 s, red flash, `hurt` 0.35 s, knocked 7 px). Down sheathes the blade (`P.peace` 1 s). The
camera zooms to 1.08 during a fight; trails, rings, sparks, a 0.05-0.06 s hit stop and a shake mark each contact.

Skeletons: hit points 1, never lost; each landed blow drives it 12 px (`SKEL_KNOCK`) toward its pit (its range is clamped only on the
far side); with no floor under it, it falls and is gone. `skeleton()` lays a six-tile zone, a pit two tiles wide with fixed spikes at its
foot and a landing tile beyond.

Snakes:

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| Placement | only in a `tunnel()` (low ceiling, `low` feature; also in side passages' corridor fills), from 12% of the route, `snakeGap` (`0.8 x route / budget`) apart; at 0.6 of the tunnel, ranging over it |
| Slither | 22 px/s inside its range; turns toward him within 44 px |
| Strike | facing him within 30 px: coils 0.42 s, `hiss`, lunges 16 px over 0.22 s; bites at 0.1 s if he is within 14 px of its head (18 px ahead) |
| Bite | one life, `P.inv` 0.6 s, `hurt` unless crouching, poking or hanging; then 1.3 s before it can strike again |
| Safe from it | in the air, hanging, climbing, sliding, swinging, wall-running, exiting (`same` is false) |
| Killing it | Grab with it within 44 px ahead: `lowStrike` (from standing or crouching outside a tunnel, reach 48 px) or `poke` with the blade drawn low (reach 40 px, works in a tunnel; drawing it inside one is refused) |

On a checkpoint respawn, guards and snakes placed before the checkpoint are removed; the rewind ring restores guards, snakes and
pending drop-ins (`snapAll`).

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Collision | a live guard and the prince block each other at 18 px (`clampMove` bodies); guards block each other too |
| Traps | guards never stand on one (`guardOk`, seven plain tiles); no trap tests a guard: spikes, saws, jaws, darts, pendulums, vents, loose floor and the dark never hurt one |
| Plates | only the prince presses them (`standOn`) |
| Gates, fallen roof blocks | shut gates and a fallen block stop a guard in `clampMove` (the prince steps over the block) |
| Movable blocks | `blockCanMove` refuses a push onto a guard |
| Lifts | drop-in spots stay a column clear of a shaft; posted guards never ride |
| Falls | only a skeleton leaves its floor; every other guard is clamped to `lo..hi` |
| Snakes and guards | no interaction |
| The bot | ordinary runs load floors with no guards (`__HK.load(n,seed,true)`: `G=[]`, `quiet` so no ambush) but keep snakes; fight runs keep guards: it parries when a blow is 0.02-0.24 s from landing, strikes a guard that is blocked or hurt, closes in beyond 34 px, else strikes at random (8% a frame, 16% at a skeleton). `Math.random` is seeded per run, so a failing fight replays exactly |
| Snakes and the bot | within 44 px ahead (36 with the low blade) while standing or crouching: turns to face, presses Grab |

## How it is drawn

Live in `drawDynamic`, snakes first, then guards, then the prince (see [prince](prince.md) for the order and `drawActor`).

```
for each guard in view (not gone; out of view a live one's press g.rdx is reset to 0):
  wc = wallPress(...) when alive          pressed off a face on either side, the rest clipped (figClip); g.rdx = wc.dx
  not alive: deadCut(g.x + g.rdx, 28)     cut by the faces within 28 px, the last press kept (round 39)
  dropping in: dropCeil(g)                clipped below the ceiling's lower edge (round 39)
  dying and DISS.on and t > DISS.t0  ->  dissolveGuard at g.rdx (crumbles into sand)
  else drawActor(g, x + g.rdx, palette = skel ? PAL_SKEL : guardPal(g),
                 {sword: alive and not idle/drop, hasSword: !skel, pale, flash})
```

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Body | the prince's skeleton and clips (`followPose` with `LAGM` overlap, breathing in `livePose` offset by `x`), drawn with `R23DR` (v70's MUS 1.28 widths; robbers are 1.15 times as broad, FIGURE_SPEC) whatever the prince's level: `drawFigureHD` swaps it in for any palette with `egy` and no `lean` |
| Dress (`GUARD_PAL`, `guardPal`: captain 3, else rank 0-2) | bare chests, undyed linen kilts, bare feet; recruit and soldier a head cloth (`head:'cloth'`, colour `khat`, tied ends trailing in the wind), veteran a crimson tunic and leather cap, captain a leather corselet and cap; no guard blue or white (the prince's colours) |
| Weapons | drawn along the sword's line (`robWeapon`: club, axe; `khopesh` for the captain), only while fighting: a robber carries nothing in hand otherwise |
| Skeleton | `drawSkeletonHD`: shaded bones, ribcage, skull with a jaw and a cold glow in its sockets, a trailing rag, a pitted blade |
| Hit | `g.flash` 0.12 s (skeleton 0.1): drawn white over |
| Death | where he was last drawn (`g.rdx`: v83 dropped the press, so a pressed guard snapped up to 20 px into the face as he died), cut by the faces within 28 px (`deadCut`), the crumbling and the sand heap too, and a falling grain is not drawn where the stone in front hides it (`midHid`; round 39); lies still `DISS.t0` 1.1 s, then crumbles over `DISS.dur` 1.0 s: a ranked noise eats the body, a sand-coloured band at the edge, grains stream onto a heap that slumps, puffs and fades (`dissGrains`, `sandHeap`, `sandDraw`). Torchlight's WGL path does the threshold on the GPU in up to four slots (`F._diss`); else two small canvases by `putImageData`. With `DISS.on` false: a fade from 1.1 to 1.9 s and a puff |
| Dropping in | while `drop` (a reinforcement or an ambush falling 74 px, about 0.46 s), drawn and its silhouette made (shadow, beam cuts) only below the ceiling's lower edge: a wall's foot, or under the storey above's floor slab its lip's foot (`dropCeil`, round 39: it came down over the ceiling's stone for about 0.35 s) |
| Lit outline | the low-detail figures' lit rim (New characters off) is the prince's only (owner 2026-10-16: on guards it read as an outline) |
| Contours (round 46d) | every outline `selC` of the shaded fill at the line (`FIG_K` .58: skin, linen, the head cloth, the cap, the corselet, the weapon's wood or bronze), never v87's fixed `#120c18`/`#0b0812`; the corselet's straps .62 and its lit strap 1.12, the cap's lit rim 1.15; the skeleton's bones by `BONE.shade` (its sockets, nose hole and teeth stay dark: holes); the snake by its shade (`SNK.shade`), its slit pupil and nostril dark of their own; the silhouettes (`o.mono`) unchanged |
| Snake (`drawSnakeHD`) | a tapered shaded body, pale belly, dark diamonds (`SNK`), a viper's head with a slit pupil, jaws open on the strike, an S-curve as it rears, a flicking tongue; contact shadow and key light with Lit characters; fades 0.9 s when killed (green sparks). `drawSnake` with New characters off |

## Rules for changing it

| Invariant | Source |
|---|---|
| Guards and snakes are not traps: own budgets, own stream (`gbrng`, seed constant 8317), never counted in trap density | unit GB |
| Guards only at meaningful spots (exit, after a gate or puzzle, a fork's short way, the ambush, the garrison); `guardscan.py` must print `guardscan OK` | unit GB |
| Every key `drawFigureHD` reads for a robber must exist in every `GUARD_PAL` rank, or the silhouette is null (no shadow, beams over him) | round 27c (`PAL_GSIL` lacked `khat`) |
| Robbers never grow with the prince: they draw `R23DR` | round 24 |
| An outline on a guard, skeleton or snake is `selC` of the fill beside it, never a fixed ink (`contourscan.py` kinds guard, snake) | round 46d |
| Rank reads by colour; no guard in the prince's blue or white | `GUARD_PAL` comment |
| A new fighter that should cast a shadow or cut the beams must be added to `GLCH` (`glMask`), `glcOcc`, the metal carve, `fillCut`, `ryBeams2D`'s figure list and the relief's `glRelEx` rubbings | effect mask code |
| Look only: the robbers' dress, weapons and dissolve leave reach, timing and the bot unchanged | rounds 7, S3-A |
| A new kind of guard placement needs a `src` that `guardscan.py` accepts | `guardscan.py` |
