## The sand echo of an earlier thief (round 49 research, 2026-10-30)

From the research on dropping every hint message (round 49; pictures were in `.claude/research/r49hints/`, `r49_echo_3x.png`).
At the first encounter of a timing move, a faint figure of sand (a "ka" of a thief who came before) plays the move once and
crumbles: the swing's pump and throw, the locked chimney's kicks and the leap out, the sand lift's step-off. Like Dark Souls'
bloodstains and Super Metroid's etecoons, it shows how without words.

- Value: high for the timing moves, which no still cue teaches.
- Cost: high. Recorded pose tracks per move, playback that is rewind-safe and a pure function of the clock, its own look.
- Risk: clutter and tone; it must not read as a guard or the dark.
- Owner, 2026-10-30: "leave the sixth for later. Write it somewhere". Build only if players stall at the swing, chimney or sand lift
  after round 49's cues.

The generator introducing mechanics more gradually (closers, banners, sand lifts from floor 5; a safe first gap) was proposed in
the same research; the owner declined it ("No"), so it is not parked here.
