# Banners and slides

A long painted runner hung down a shaft three or four storeys deep: he holds Grab as he falls past it, sinks his blade into the cloth
and slides down at a braked, steady pace to the floor at its foot, splitting the cloth as he goes. Built in chambers going down from
floor 4 (and in forks' short ways); about 37% of these moves are built as a [sand lift](lifts.md) instead. The wall hangings that are only
decoration (`TAP` hangings, `drawHangings`) are [stonework](stonework.md)'s.

## Example

`bannerSeg(3)` at column c, row r: column c+1 is opened for rows r to r+2, each cell carrying a `banner` feature that shares one `bn`
(`bn.top` = r TH + 9, `bn.bot` 16 px above the last cell's foot), and c+1..c+3 on row r+3 become the foot's floor (a `G3` gate goes on
c+2 if the turn holds one). He walks off the landing; in the air Grab is held: as he falls past the cloth he is set on the cell's middle,
`P.vy` capped at 140, state `slide`. For 0.07 s the blade bites (speed toward 30 px/s, sound `swish`), then the drag holds him near 170
px/s, a little uneven (+-10% at 31 rad/s) as the weave gives; the cloth parts above his hands, `rip` fourteen times a second, lint
flying. Three storeys take a little over a second. Floor 16 seed 1 (the hanging in column 250 from row 17, its lip in column 251) is round 33c's
case: from a hang on the lip, Grab and Down take the cloth at once.

## The prince and the guards

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| He falls past the cloth without Grab | an ordinary fall down the shaft (three or four storeys: it kills) |
| His pose | `PS.slide` blended from `PS.reach` in the bite, shaken a little, knees folding as the floor comes within 52 px; the blade drawn short along the depth direction (it points into the cloth), `po.swLen` = `slBlade()` |
| Taking the cloth (round 33c, look only) | the physics snaps `P.x` 9 px to the cell's middle; the drawn body keeps that offset (`p.ox`, at most 24 px) and swings in over `SI_T` 0.2 s (a decaying offset, Bollo's inertialization); one hand takes the cloth first, the other `SI_D` 0.06 s later, each over max(`SI_H`, 1.875 d / `SI_V`), along a 1.5 px arc; from a hang each hand leaves the lip where it held it and slips off over `SI_S`; the nearer hand goes first from a fall; the blade shows once the sword hand is halfway there and sinks in to `SL_BLADE`; the knees fold at `SI_RATE` 24 with the slide's overlap (`LAGM.slide` 2) |
| Letting go of any hang (round 33c, look only; not only over a banner) | `P.lgo`: each hand stays on the lip until `HF_R` (near 0.03, far 0.07 s), slips off over `HF_S`, rides up with the body for `HF_H`, then eases to the fall's arms over `HF_W`; carried through `land`/`stand` if he lands at once (PoP1's hangfall; CMU 01_10) |
| Guards | none: they never stand in a chamber's shaft, never slide |
| The bot | walks off the landing, holds Grab after 8 frames in the air; if it catches the lip it lets go and presses Down, then holds Grab again; walks on to `to` once standing |

Largest move in one frame, v78 to round 33c (CLAUDE.md): hands 15.2 / 22.0 to 3.5 / 3.4 px, head 9.6 / 9.1 to 3.6 / 3.2 px; the bot's
frames identical, `estPath` unchanged.

## How it is drawn

Live in `drawDynamic` (`case 'banner'` in the `L.dyn` loop, before the actors), one tile at a time by `drawBannerTile`, the same calls
in both modes:

| Part | Look |
|---|---|
| Rod (top tile) | a bar across the top of the shaft in the theme's `trim`, with end caps |
| Runner | 28 px wide (nearly the shaft's width), from 9 px under the top cell's top to 16 px above the last cell's foot; `tapCloth` pattern 6, the shaft runner: a repeating run of 32 px panels in the manner of Egyptian painted linen, in the look's `TAP` colours; a dark shade on its right, pale selvedges so it parts from the wall in the dark |
| Fringe (bottom tile) | ten threads in `cloth2` (or `trim`) |
| Cut | between `bn.a` and `bn.b` the cloth splits along a ragged edge (hashes of the height) with loose threads; the gap opens widest between the cut's ends, or toward the free end once cut through; each half curls into shadow at its cut edge and sways, dying out (exp(-0.7 age)); a cloth cut through shows the fringe's ends on each half |
| Particles | threads and lint from the blade in the theme's cloth colour; pale dust at his hands |

Nothing cuts the banner (it hangs in the shaft at his depth); he is drawn over it. No wear level of its own.

## Rules for changing it

- Round 33c is look only: hit box, `P.x`/`P.y`, the cut, timings and the bot are unchanged; anything new in taking the cloth or letting go
  is drawn on `P.pose` after the follow (`handsIn`), never by moving the physics.
- The slide never hurts: keep `P.fallY` riding with him.
- The sand lift's choice must stay a hash (`sandOr`): a banner and a sand lift have the same tiles, so the floor's layout does not move.
- A new banner placement needs the shared `bn` record, its bot action `slide` and its `estPath` time; keep lamps, hangings and breaches
  clear of its cells (their tests read `t:'banner'`).

## History

- the slide: the blade bites and the drag holds a steady `SL_V` (the first version crept at 75 px/s); the cloth splits as he goes.
- round 16a: about 37% of banner moves become the sand lift (`sandOr`).
- rounds 26e, 26g: a sand lift that cannot stand in a shaft is a banner again.
- round 33c: taking the cloth and letting go of a hang drawn (`SI_*`, `HF_*`); Grab with Down in a hang takes the cloth.
