# Slicer jaws

PoP1's slicer: two toothed plates across the corridor, one hanging from the ceiling, one rising from the floor, that snap shut on a
rhythm and kill whatever is between them. From floor 4 in runs, on landings and in side corridors (side passages from floor 6), in every
style; sometimes two in a row. Cast bronze in the Egyptian looks (round 37b), the style's steel or iron without them.

## Example
Floor 9 seed 2, the Blue Chambers, the slicer at cell 22,15 (period 1.58 s) with a lamp two columns to its left (the owner's case in
round 37b). He stands between the lamp and the jaws, so his lamp shadow is cast to his right across them. Until v81 that shadow was
painted on the plates as if they were the back wall, and the relief embossed the wall's bands on them (a pale glass panel). Now the
plates are carved out of the shadow (it falls on the wall seen between them when open) and the relief is rubbed off them.

## How it is drawn
Live only (`drawDynamic`); the room picture holds a plain floor slab. The open-hall pass leaves the wall over it, so it hangs from a
one-storey ceiling: a wall, or the slab of the corridor above.

The plate stands in `plane(X,Y,ys,c,r)`: across the corridor's depth through the tile's middle, from `L0 = X+16-HX` on the floor's front
edge to `R0 = X+16+HX` at the back wall; `fl(x)` the floor line (`ys+HY` to `ys-HY`), `ce(x)` the ceiling's underside, `top = Y+co`
(`ceilOff`: 5 under a floor slab's lip, 0 under a wall). The upper jaw hangs `upOff = lerp(co?2:7, 31-co, k)` below `ce`, teeth down
(`CH_UP`, 7 teeth up to 11 px); the lower stands `loOff = lerp(3, 21, k)` over `fl`, teeth up (`CH_LO`, 8 teeth). Open, the upper teeth
just clear the ceiling's front edge; shut, the teeth interlock.

```
drawDynamic
  L.dyn loop    far half (planeClip half 0: x >= X+16)   slots, lower jaw, ceilShade, upper jaw, dust shaft   -> JAWP recorded
  figures       guards, prince
  near halves   near half (half 1: x < X+16+JAW_OV)      lower jaw, ceilShade, upper jaw, dust shaft
```
The near half laps `JAW_OV` 0.6 px over the far one so no wall shows through the seam; the upper jaw is drawn after the ceiling's shade
(it was lost in the dark under the ceiling). Each jaw, inside its own outline (`jawPath`):

| Layer | What |
|---|---|
| fill | `jawGrad`: across the face near edge to far (`JAWG`): a dark rim, the body, a crisp reflection band (0.30-0.37), the body, a darker stretch, the far edge catching the light |
| brushing | faint light and dark lines along the plate |
| key light | `keyLight(X+16,ys)`, `ks = sx*m`: lamp beyond the plate (ks > 0.05), the face darker by 0.32 ks and the far edge brighter; lamp on our side, the near edge lit |
| spine | a band `JAW_SW` 3.4 px wide `JAW_SP` 12 px back from the teeth, lit toward the teeth, three rivets (at 0.2, 0.62, 0.88); slides into the slot with the blade |
| wear | four spots of verdigris and tarnish toward the root, from a hash of the cell (no stream) |
| bevel | a dark 3.6 px line where the honed edge meets the face, the edge itself 1.8 px bright (`pal[3]`); Classic adds a 0.8 px white glint (`METAL_PAINT_HI`) |
| root | in the slot's shade |
| outline, blood | 1 px dark outline; red on the teeth after a kill |

`slots` (far pass only) draws the floor slot along the plane and the dark slot in the ceiling; `ceilShade` the ceiling's shadow down the
plate. While k < 0.6 a faint dust shaft joins the jaws.

## Rules for changing it
- Look only (round 37b): silhouette, hit box, timing, `beamBlockers`, layouts and the bot unchanged by a look change.
- One outline: `drawJaws` builds `jawPath` and records it in `JAWP` (far-half pass, both modes); the metal mask and the shadow carve fill
  the same paths. `beamBlockers` and `glRelJaws` recompute `upOff`/`loOff` from `chompK` by hand: change the offsets in all three.
- Wear and blemishes from hashes of the cell, never a generator stream.
- A new metal look is a `METAL_EG` role read by `metalOf`, `metalPal` (cached in `MPAL` by style, role and `E`) and `msKind`.
- A new thing standing across the corridor in front of the back wall needs its outline in `SHC` (carved by `glMask`), its rubbing in the
  relief and, if solid to light, a `beamBlockers` quad.
